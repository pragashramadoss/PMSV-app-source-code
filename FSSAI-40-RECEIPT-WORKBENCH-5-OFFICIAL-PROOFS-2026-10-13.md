# PMSV FSSAI Helper: 40-case locally saved evidence review workbench

13 October 2026, GitHub gh-pages preview only. WordPress unchanged. Canonical regulatory ledger remains **533 catalogue / 493 partial / 40 pending**. The workbench records 160 separate received/not-received documentary checklist states (4 per product), a reference field, and review notes in browser localStorage only, with clear and copy-JSON actions. It sends no data to PMSV, WordPress or GitHub, and no checkbox or note creates a legal match, lab verification or regulatory approval.

Five directly rechecked official source distinctions: quinoa is a grain under FSSR 2.4.6(18), but excluded from CTR metal 'cereal grains' lead/cadmium; a separate decision is necessary for the broader cereal/cereal-product aflatoxin article. Chia is mint-family Salvia hispanica under 2.4.6(25), not automatically CTR oilseed. Cube sugar under FSSR 2.8.1(5) is made from refined sugar and has total ash <=0.03%, not proof of sulphated ash <=0.03% for lead; the separate chromium refined sugar article also needs finished scope proof. Misri under FSSR 2.8.2 can derive from any sugar or palmyrah juice, not necessarily refined sugar. Dried glucose syrup under FSSR 2.8.7 has sulphated ash max 1%, whereas CTR lead high-ash starch conversion row requires over 1%; do not apply it to conforming dried glucose syrup.

Official sources: [CTR IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf), [Chapter 2.4](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4%20%28Cereals%20and%20Cereal%20products%29%281%29.pdf), [Chapter 2.8](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_8%20(Sweetening%20agents%20including%20Honey).pdf).

Limitations: local receipts do not sync between browsers, and copying JSON is for manual verification, not a certificate. Data model is fail-closed; actual manufacturing evidence and qualified review remain necessary.
