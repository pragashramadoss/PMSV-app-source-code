## Non-transferability safeguards

- Sterilised fungi in a jar, dried fungi and fungi in oil must not inherit the **canned mushrooms** metal rows or the canned-food tin limit merely because FSSR 2.3.62 groups these product forms. Species, presentation, container and test basis must be established independently.
- Dehydrated onions and non-onion dehydrated vegetables have **different** named lead rows. Dry-matter analytical basis matters; no blended automatic result.
- Thermally processed fruit salads and cocktail mixes may be canned, bottled or aseptically packaged. A named canned fruit cocktail lead article requires actual canned cocktail identity.
- Industrial vegetable pulp/purée and juice concentrates must not inherit ready-to-drink fruit-juice metal values or an assumed ready-to-consume sample basis.
- Coconut cream, coconut milk and coconut milk powder are **not dairy milk** or coconut oil, and cocoa beans are **not cocoa powder** tested on a dry fat-free solids basis.
- Mixed fruit/vegetable pickles and chutneys must not automatically inherit **pickled cucumber** or **mango chutney** articles; declared vegetable/fruit identity must be verified.
- Water chestnut flour is **not canned chestnuts/purée**. Different product forms cannot inherit canned articles merely from a shared word.
