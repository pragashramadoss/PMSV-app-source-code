## Nine distinct Gazette changes recorded (future-only)
- **Lead**, regulation 2.1.1, printed Gazette page 4: Pulses is replaced by Pulses and Pulse flours in the lead article.
- **Cadmium**, regulation 2.1.1, printed Gazette page 4: Pulses excluding soybean dry is replaced by Pulses and Pulse flours excluding soybean dry.
- **Inorganic arsenic**, regulation 2.1.1, printed Gazette page 5: Fish-oil-specific note specifies inorganic arsenic ML 0.1 mg/kg and total arsenic screening with conditional speciation follow-up. Future-only reference 0.1 mg/kg.
- **Total aflatoxins**, regulation 2.2.1, printed Gazette page 5: Revises source commodity text distinguishing oils and oilseeds for further processing from ready-to-eat oilseeds.
- **Aflatoxin B1**, regulation 2.2.1, printed Gazette page 5: Revises corresponding oil/oilseed commodity wording for aflatoxin B1.
- **Saffrole**, regulation 2.2.1, printed Gazette page 5: Replaces the commodity entry with beverages and foods containing mace and/or nutmeg kernel as an ingredient, table value 10. Future-only reference 10 table unit (verify parent CTR table).
- **Clause 2.3.2(1)**, regulation 2.3.2, printed Gazette page 6: Omits clause (1) of sub-regulation 2.3.2. Independent regulatory review of the deleted clause and implications is required.
- **Trimethoprim**, regulation 2.3.2, printed Gazette page 6: Adds seafood including shrimp, prawns, fish/fishery products to trimethoprim row, value 0.05. Future-only reference 0.05 table unit (verify parent CTR table).
- **Oxolinic acid**, regulation 2.3.2, printed Gazette page 6: Adds oxolinic acid row for seafood including shrimp, prawns and fish/fishery products, value 0.3. Future-only reference 0.3 table unit (verify parent CTR table).

Special non-transfer safeguards:
- Mace and nutmeg raw spices are not automatically the "foods containing" ingredient class.
- Fish-oil inorganic-arsenic testing steps and 0.1 mg/kg limit do not attach to fish fillets or vegetable fats.
- Fish sauce and processed fish products need case-specific confirmation under the defined seafood/fishery-products scope.
- Trimethoprim and oxolinic acid seafood entries are not automatically limits for royal jelly or other animal ingredients.
- The 35 open legal article cases still require exact sold food/ingredient mapping. Aflatoxins in composite nutritional premixes may require assessment even where no product-name watch ID is present.
