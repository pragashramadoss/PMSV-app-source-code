## Controlling effective-date distinction — directly checked original gazettes

1. **Final 2024 CTR amendment:** [Original FSSAI Gazette PDF](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf), English original PDF **page 17**, Regulation 1(2): **in force from 1 April 2025**. The same page gives, in 2.2.1, an Ochratoxin A article list specifically naming **wheat, wheat bran, rye, barley and coffee**, with a **5 µg/kg row for those specified articles**. **Coffee-chicory mixture (held case #2) is not expressly named**. Do **not** map 5 µg/kg automatically as the mixture’s applicable legal limit. Requires signed ingredient/classification applicability determination.
2. **Final 2024 CTR amendment:** [Original PDF p.18](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=18), Regulation **2.3.2(3)** discusses **antibiotic use during honey production and MRPL testing for such misuse**, followed by its specified table. **Royal Jelly (case #31) is not expressly named as honey** by this provision. Neither the honey table nor veterinary milk/meat/egg/seafood EMRL automatically approves a limit for a royal-jelly commercial sample. A competent signed legal assessment of actual substance and exact operative provision remains needed.
3. **Final 2026 CTR amendment:** [Original FSSAI Gazette PDF page 4](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/272840.pdf#page=4), Regulation **1(2)** explicitly says its commencement is **1 December 2026**. Therefore on **17 October 2026** it is **not operative**. This includes its proposed-now-final extensions to pulse flour, fish oil arsenic testing, aflatoxin oilseeds and residue entries. Neither a draft nor a final published but future-effective Gazette can override the then-current requirements.

These are legal **source-text facts**. None establishes how an unverified marketed product should be classified or which actual lot passed.

## Indexed original 2020 Gazettes — source verified with limits disclosed

These three PDFs are FSSAI-hosted original notifications with official-indexed English search excerpts and titles; **complete original PDF pages were not downloaded/rendered this turn**. This is lower confidence than the directly inspected 2024 and 2026 original texts and the preview labels it that way.

4. [2020 CTR First Amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/08/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf) dated 7 August 2020: original published regulation replaced the lead and other metal contaminants under 2.1.1 and modified aflatoxin/mycotoxin sources. Gazette commencement on publication, with operators required to comply by **1 July 2021** (per indexed English original). This supports an **operative legislative framework**, *not* a direct proof for any of the 35 individual case maximum values or a reason to ignore 2024 later amendments.
5. [2020 FPSFA Third Amendment](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/08/5f362b888233bGazette_Notification_FSS_Additives_Third_Amendment_14_08_2020.pdf) dated 9 July 2020: explicitly includes standards for **Coconut Milk Powder (Non Dairy)** and **Chia Seeds**. By 2026 the standard’s identity topics were already in place, but an FPSFA food identity is not a CTR lead permissible level.
6. [2020 FPSFA Tenth Amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fedb66ac1b15Gazette_Notification_FPS_Tenth_amendment_31_12_2020.pdf) dated 29 December 2020: explicitly names **Water Chestnut Flour, Animal Casings, Egg Powder and Pickled Eggs** as standards; these are identity/quality specifications, not proof of exact CTR heavy-metal or pesticide residue applicability.

When these originals are visually checked, assess any subsequent supersession before declaring their complete clause language effective for an actual product. Indexed publication-topic verification alone is not enough for original-wording adjudication.
