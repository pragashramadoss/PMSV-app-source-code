## Affir­mative matched article

FSSR Chapter 2.3 Version 2, paragraph **2.3.47(5) Dry Fruits and Nuts**, explicitly defines food products prepared by drying mature **fruits and nuts**. The standard is an umbrella category: it includes a genuinely identifiable dried-fruit marketed subtype, but also distinct nut products.

The FSSAI Contaminants Regulations Version IX (03.02.2026), §2.3.1, pesticide **114 Malathion** defines the residue as combined *malathion plus malaoxon*, and explicitly lists:
- `Dried fruits` — **8 mg/kg**.
- `Fruits` — **4 mg/kg** (different article, must not be transferred to dried fruit).

This new source match is **only a separately identified dried-fruit subroute** under the combined FoSCoS catalogue item `04-04-1-dry-fruits-and-nuts`. It does **not** assert the MRL applies to nuts, mixed fruit/nut products, fruit bars, sweets, processed snacks, or an unspecified lot. A real manufacturer's formula/identity and sample basis remain unconfirmed. This source match is **partial official article evidence**, not an automatically applied finished-product limit, not complete pesticide/contaminant verification, and **not a laboratory or regulatory PASS**.

Official product standard (PDF printed p67): https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit%20%20Vegetable%20products.pdf
Official CTR Version IX (PDF p30; pesticide 114): https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf
