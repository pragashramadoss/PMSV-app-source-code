## Exact competing rows do not determine held cases

Selected **related named comparator** examples from CTR Version IX — used only to expose false-equivalence risk, not assigned to any of the thirteen:
- Lead *cocoa powder*, **5.0 mg/kg on dry fat-free substance basis**, CTR PDF p.3; not automatically a whole cocoa-bean, liquor/cake or cocoa-sugar mixture article.
- Lead *packaged drinking water*, **0.01 mg/L**, CTR PDF p.5; not automatically drinking water dispensed through a vending machine.
- Lead *infant formula (ready to use)*, **0.02 mg/kg**, CTR PDF p.4; does not prove formulated supplements intended for older children are infant formula.
- Lead *milks*, **0.02 mg/kg**, with concentration factor for partly/wholly dehydrated milks, CTR PDF p.5; not a blanket criterion for coconut milk powder, colostrum formulations or Ayurveda Aahara cooked milk-containing finished foods.
- Copper *pulp and pulp products of any fruit*, **5.0 mg/kg**, CTR PDF p.7; not automatically raw seedless tamarind flesh.
- Chromium *refined sugar*, **0.02 mg/kg**, CTR PDF p.13; not automatically misri without signed class determination.
- Nickel *sorbitol*, **2.0 mg/kg**, CTR PDF p.13; not a class-wide limit for an unnamed polyol, blended polyol or xylitol.
- Royal jelly and unspecified "antibiotics": a named veterinary substance and its actual covered commodity/legal route must be separately established; no generic numeric comparator is selected.

These rows are reproduced to make the precise non-transfer boundary auditable. If an amendment or original Gazette differs from a compiled compendium, the governing effective legal text prevails. **A related named row is not itself approval of its use for another as-sold matrix.**
