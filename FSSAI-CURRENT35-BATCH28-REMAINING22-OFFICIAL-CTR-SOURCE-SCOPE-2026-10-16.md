## Critical regulatory rule

Section 2.1.1 of [official FSSAI CTR IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) lists articles *by metal*. A named article for the ingredient is not necessarily the named article for the finished food. **A separately worded 'Foods not specified' or processing/container article must not be applied automatically without precedence analysis and specific product identity.** Analyte, analytical basis, units and official current effective date must all be checked.

The prospective December 2026 amendment is not treated as effective for this October batch.

## Mandatory review gate

A product can move from the pending status only after documentary proof of its marketed identity and formula, applicable finished form/process/container, the *actual source clause and analyte*, and a qualified reviewer sign-off on legal article precedence. If conformity is asserted, proper finished-lot sampling and a verified laboratory result against the correctly adjudicated article must exist. Entering a claim, importing reviewer notes, or documenting that the regulation lists an analogous food never constitutes a regulatory approval.
