# FINAL35 — action-ready regulatory closeout brief, 17 October 2026

**Desk research: 35/35 named cases reviewed. Formal legal-applicability approvals: 0/35. Representative lot CoAs: 0. Product releases: 0.**

This is the management decision page for the completed public-source review, **not a new batch, not a dashboard and not a compliance certification**.

## What is actually resolved

- Source-level product identity or specific named FSSR source for **28 cases**; **7 remain umbrella-group products** rather than one individual standardized product (cases #21, #24, #25, #26, #27, #28 and #29).
- **15 original source comparisons are demonstrably different from the commercial article**. For the other 20 no selected direct comparator exists in the dataset; this is not a negative legal finding.
- A decisive rejection: **Dried Glucose Syrup (#18)** meeting its FSSR sulphated-ash max ≤1.0% cannot simultaneously meet the **special high-ash (>1.0%) starch-conversion lead comparator**. Actual manufacturer conforming identity and a different lawful lead basis still need proof.
- Another rejection: group **polyols excluding those already standardized (#21)** cannot inherit the **individually standardized sorbitol Ni ≤2 mg/kg** as a universal nickel maximum. It needs a named polyol.
- For **24 Lead analyte cases**, published pressure-digestion ICP-MS has plausible Pb sensitivity ~0.04 mg/kg dry matter, but requires actual current adopted procedure, matrix recovery/LOQ and lab accreditation; duplicate FSSAI 2024 09.xxx numbers and the September 2026 method amendment need final source resolution.
- Original regulator-issued source texts are identified for 2011 Pb/Cu fallback clauses, October 2024 coffee OTA/honey antibiotics, 2017 sorbitol, 2017 salt substitutes, 2018 children's supplement, and 2025 Ayurveda Aahara recipes. None approves an actual marketed case.

## P1 — 22 pending source decisions grouped by actual work type

**Pesticide × exact commodity: 4 cases.** Obtain actual named pesticide(s), edible test part, crop/egg species and processing factors. Generic 'residues' has no single fixed threshold.

- **#4 Frozen Fruits/Fruit Products (Residues):** Crop species and mix BOM, pesticide analytes, IQF/form and consumption basis.
- **#13 Arrowroot (Pesticide MRLs):** Botanical species, purified starch vs flour, pesticide profile, transformation factors.
- **#16 Egg powder (Pesticide MRLs):** Whole, yolk or albumen egg powder; egg source, concentration ratio, pesticide list.
- **#17 Pickled Eggs (Pesticide MRLs):** Species egg, brine ingredients and pack proportions; pesticide identity.

**Aflatoxin B1/total and containing food: 2 cases.** Obtain exact botanical/food constituents; choose B1 or total aflatoxins against operative article and accredited mixed-matrix extraction.

- **#28 Ingredients listed under Schedule IV, Schedule VI and Schedule VIII of Nutraceutical Regulations (Aflatoxins):** Actual botanical/seed/food constituent schedule row, percentage, processing, total vs B1 target.
- **#29 Mixtures/preparations/premix of functional ingredients (Aflatoxins):** Controlled premix BOM with component percentages and spices/nuts/cereal identity.

**Coffee–chicory OTA: 1 cases.** Obtain declared coffee/chicory percentages from front label; classify blend versus coffee article; authenticate OTA assay on blend.

- **#2 Coffee-Chicory Mixture (Ochratoxin A):** Coffee/chicory ratio (≥51% coffee for named standardized ground/instant class), label, roasted vs instant identity, OTA method.

**Generic additives, polyols, enzymes, mineral classes: 4 cases.** Resolve individual INS/CAS/EC/substance, current status and impurity monograph; no blanket grouped-comparator transfer.

- **#24 Sajji Khar (Lead):** Sajji Khar exact salt species/CAS, compositional analysis, manufacturer licence.
- **#25 Additives permitted under Appendix-A of FSS (FPS&FA) Regulations, 2011 and other food safety standards regulations excluding those already standardized and additives of Nutraceutical Regulations (Lead):** Individual INS/CAS, grade, food category, monograph, supplier CoA.
- **#26 Additives VA-VF of Nutraceutical Regulations excluding those already standardised (Lead):** Current effective schedule mapping and substance-level identity, source.
- **#27 Permitted enzymes and their preparations excluding those already listed under Processing Aids and Nutraceutical Regulations (Lead):** EC and microbial production species, carriers, food-use role, current monograph.

**Named lead/chemical/salt/carbohydrate candidates: 11 cases.** Apply original law to specific named article; assess fallback only after exclusions, then validate analytical LOQ and lot basis.
