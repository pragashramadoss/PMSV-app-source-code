# PMSV — FSSAI Version IX Confectionery Hydrocyanic Acid evidence (10 October 2026)

**Decision:** two exact FoSCoS finished confectionery identities gain **partial** NOTS evidence, with unchanged metal-article locks and **no full compliance PASS**.

## Source evidence

- FSSAI Contaminants, Toxins and Residues Version IX (3 February 2026), §2.2.1 Naturally Occurring Toxic Substances lists **Hydrocyanic acid — Confectionery — 5 ppm**. Official source: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf (PDF page 15 / zero-based index 14).
- FSSAI food standards Chapter 2.7 identifies **Sugar Boiled Confectionery §2.7.1** and **Lozenges §2.7.2** as confectionery products. Official source: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_7%20%28Sweets%20and%20Confectionary%29.pdf.
- FoSCoS standardized catalogue explicitly places **Lozenges** (ID `05-05-2-lozenges`, FCS 05.2.1, FSSR 2.7.2) and **Sugar boiled confectionery (Soft Candy)** (ID `05-05-2-sugar-boiled-confectionery-soft-candy`, FCS 05.2.2, FSSR 2.7.1) within the 05.2 confectionery category. Official catalogue: https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product.

## Scope and safeguards

- This establishes a single specific naturally occurring toxin article, **not** a comprehensive finished-product contaminant profile.
- The existing six Chapter 2.7 automatic product-level metal/crop article locks remain in place. The dedicated, name+FSSR+FCS source-gated hydrocyanic-acid exception **does not permit** Soft Candy to borrow Hard Boiled Confectionery's Lead/Copper/Arsenic/Tin articles, nor Lozenges to borrow Cocoa Powder or hard-candy metal limits.
- The exception is suppressed if the Version IX source value, source article, unit, product identity, FCS, source version, or safety flags drift.
- Other NOTS, ingredient-specific MRLs, contaminants, amendments, applicable standards and actual lab evidence remain unverified.
- The full regulatory compliance count remains zero.
