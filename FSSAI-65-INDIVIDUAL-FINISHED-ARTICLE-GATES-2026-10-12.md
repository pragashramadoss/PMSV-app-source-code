# PMSV current 111 — 65 product-individual legal source boundaries (2026-10-12)

**Branch:** GitHub `gh-pages` only. WordPress remains unchanged.

**Conserved official-source status:** 533 catalogue identities; **422 partial exact contaminant-source matches**, **111 pending first finished-article matches**, and **zero legal compliance or tested-lot PASS**.

This batch documents **65 unique current pending identities**, including eight dairy analogues, ten edible-fungi forms, nine sugar/syrup forms, four processed-egg forms, nine cereal/bakery forms, four cocoa/coffee forms, three coconut nondairy forms, eight other source/article qualifications, five fruit preparations, two Ayurveda Aahara recipes and three vegetable puree/concentrate forms.

Key safeguards: dairy analogues do not inherit the Milks lead article based on naming; fungi are not all mushrooms and sterilized does not equal canned; sugar TOTAL ash does not substitute for SULPHATED ash; dried glucose syrup FSSR limit ≤1% sulphated ash excludes the CTR lead source requiring >1%; processed eggs need shell-free residue and legal processing basis; quinoa cannot borrow excluded cereal-grain metal articles; papad, custard powder, pasta and instant noodles permit different raw commodities; cocoa powder dry-fat-free limits are not raw cocoa bean/mass limits; coconut milk is not animal milk; FoSCoS Category18 Indian confections are not necessarily hard-boiled confectionery; unspecified vegetable puree does not equal tomato concentrate on dried tomato solids basis.

For every one of the 65 the manifest provides a distinct product/source boundary and affirmative proof needed. None is promoted by an inference or a note-taking checkbox. All named CTR rows remain **research-only** until actual source applicability is legally established.
