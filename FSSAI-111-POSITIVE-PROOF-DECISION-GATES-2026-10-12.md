## Verified core distinctions

- Cube sugar §2.8.1(5): **total ash at most 0.03%** is not a demonstrated sulphated ash result. The CTR refined-white-sugar Lead 0.5 mg/kg article is conditional on **sulphated ash at most 0.03%**. Chromium Refined sugar 0.02 mg/kg requires its own exact legal finished-article match. No automatic transfer.
- Dried glucose syrup §2.8.7: the required **sulphated ash at most 1%** rules out the high-ash sugar/starch-conversion Lead 5 mg/kg article demanding sulphated ash **over 1%**. This is **not a blanket Lead exemption**.
- Icing, Khandsari, Bura, Misri, Plantation White Sugar, Golden Syrup and edible lactose each keep their separate FSSR identity, source variants and chemical evidence rather than inheriting refined-sugar metal articles.
- Liquid, frozen, powdered and pickled eggs remain distinct products. The pesticide source row “Eggs” requires shell-free testing basis; applying it to processed egg foods needs regulatory interpretation and processing/recipe factors.
- Pasta §2.4.10 permits cereal or other flours, including tapioca/groundnut; instant noodles permit tuber flours; Papad §2.4.40 is a broad alternative-ingredient preparation; custard powder §2.4.9 allows maize OR sago/tapioca. No universal cereal aflatoxin or milled-grain pesticide transfer.
- All eight dairy analogue routes keep positive FSSAI FAQ Q7 corresponding-category safety applicability yet withhold the numeric 'Milks' article without actual corresponding subtype/product identification.
- Edible fungi remain broader than mushrooms; canned, preserved, heat-processed and as-sold formats remain distinct regulatory test matrices.
