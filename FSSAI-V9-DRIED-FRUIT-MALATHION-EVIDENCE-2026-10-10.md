# FSSAI Version IX — exact dried-fruit Malathion commodity source review

Reviewed 10 October 2026. **Partial official-source commodity evidence only. No complete compliance approval.**

## Official source rows

[FSSAI Contaminants, Toxins and Residues Regulations, Version IX, 3 February 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf), section 2.3.1 Malathion (pesticide no. 114), PDF page 30 (one-based):

| Official commodity | Malathion MRL | Residue definition |
|---|---:|---|
| Fruits | 4 mg/kg | Combined malathion and malaoxon, expressed as malathion |
| **Dried fruits** | **8 mg/kg** | Combined malathion and malaoxon, expressed as malathion |

These are distinct commodity source rows. Do **not** borrow the 4 mg/kg fresh-fruit value for dried fruit.

## Three exact dried-fruit identities

The [official FSSAI Chapter 2.3 food standards](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3%20%28Fruit%20%26%20Vegetable%20products%29.pdf) include these exact finished identities:

- `04-04-1-dehydrated-fruits` — **Dehydrated Fruits**, FSSR **2.3.35**.
- `04-04-1-dried-apricots` — **Dried Apricots**, FSSR **2.3.53**.
- `04-04-1-raisins` — **Raisins**, FSSR **2.3.47(2)**.

PMSV checks each catalogue ID, finished product name and FSSR clause against the source-pinned Malathion Dried fruits row. An altered article, MRL, unit, residue name, version, or catalogue identity withholds the numeric reference.

**Excluded by default:** Dates, generic dry-fruits-and-nuts mixed category, fruit bars/toffee, dehydrated vegetables and unlisted composites. Product composition or commodity classification must be independently reviewed before any additional inclusion.

**Limitations:** the matched source is one pesticide/commodity article, not an exhaustive pesticide panel. Other active pesticide MRLs, processing factors (where separately applicable), source-amendment effectivity, contaminant metals, mycotoxins, and laboratory results remain unassessed. The UI displays the limit as **partial source-verified commodity evidence; NOT a finished-product compliance PASS**.
