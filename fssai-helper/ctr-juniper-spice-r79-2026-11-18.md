# Juniper berry in Lajadi Kashaya

**Review 79 · 18 November 2026**

Recipe 12.1 names Hribera as its third ingredient and identifies *Juniperus communis* L. fruit at one-quarter part. The Spices Board Act lists juniper berry at Schedule entry 37. Codex identifies the same species in its standard for spices made from dried or dehydrated fruits and berries. Together these sources support considering the FSSAI spice articles when the actual supply is authenticated dried fruit of culinary-spice grade. This connection to the grouped articles is an inference. The recipe name Hribera and the archived API monograph name Hapusa still require reconciliation. ## Codex quality references

CXS 358-2024 covers spices offered for direct consumption, as food-processing ingredients or for repackaging. Section 1 excludes products intended for industrial processing. The 2026 reformatted publication identifies *Juniperus communis* L. in section 2.1. The style refers to the material as supplied. Grinding during tea preparation cannot establish the purchased style. The standard also allows other appropriately labelled styles, for which the Helper leaves the style selection empty.

| Supplied style | Maximum moisture, % w/w | Physical row in Table A2 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Whole, naturally dried | 22 | Whole |
| Whole, dehydrated | 16 | Whole |
| Cut or broken | 16 | Cut/broken |
| Ground or powdered | 14 | Ground/powdered |

For the listed styles, maximum total ash is 4% w/w, maximum acid-insoluble ash is 1% w/w and minimum volatile oil is 0.5 mL/100 g, each on a dry basis. Table A2 gives style-specific physical references. For whole fruit, the extraneous matter maximum excludes stalks. Separate maxima apply to stalks and to broken berries among the whole berries. The cut/broken row leaves “Other factors” blank. The ground row records six unevaluated characteristics, a live-insect maximum of zero per 100 g and an unevaluated “Other factors” cell. These distinct states are retained in the export and display. Section 9 refers to CXS 234-1999 for relevant methods and sampling. ## Indian draft and finished-product scope

The FSSAI draft dated 5 June 2024 proposed juniper berries at clause 2.9.44. The notification dated 21 October 2024 prints dried parsley at that number. The Helper records the June proposal as a draft. An operative Indian juniper-specific quality standard would require its own Gazette and commencement review.
