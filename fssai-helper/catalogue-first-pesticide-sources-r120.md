# R120 · First pesticide sources for three marketed forms

Reviewed 22 November 2026. Of the 533 catalogue entries, 498 already had a partial source match. Three more have a conditional route through a named commodity group in the 2,4-D table. The other 32 await a first documented route. The original October register retains its 35 open case identities and 175 evidence requests. A source match establishes neither a product approval nor a batch release.

| Marketed form | Gazette group and maximum | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Plain seedless tamarind | Fruits · 2 mg/kg | Identify mature ripe tamarind with the shell and seeds physically removed. Verify the marketed specification and process. Pulp extracts, concentrates and sauces need a separate assessment. [FPSFA 2.3.49, PDF p. 73](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=73) |
| Plain single-fruit frozen product | Fruits · 2 mg/kg | Identify the fruit and confirm solely physical preparation and freezing. ## Reading the source

CTR 2.3.1, serial 1, prints both values for 2,4-dichlorophenoxyacetic acid. The 0.01 mg/kg figure comes from the other-food-grains branch of the explicit food-grains entry. FSSAI's processing clarification extends raw-commodity MRLs to the foods resulting from physical processing and excludes thermal and chemical processing from that extension. The May 2026 final amendment leaves these two pesticide entries in place for the December assessment. [2018 Gazette, PDF p. 31](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=31) · [2021 clarification, paragraph 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/01/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf#page=1) · [May 2026 amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf)

## Using a worksheet

Select the tested marketed form and enter the sample reference, specification and process record. Then enter the report, result type and concentration in mg/kg, and confirm the analytical basis. The comparison applies to that sample and analyte. A separately sampled ingredient keeps its own result. The source row and decision can be traced in the product brief, the saved laboratory result and the individual testing plan. Bulk plans show the conditions for use and the official references.

The finder lets users select all 501 partial or conditional routes, just the three new group entries, the 498 earlier partial matches or the 32 entries still awaiting a first source. The last two columns of the 533-product register identify the current status and the evidence for the group route.
