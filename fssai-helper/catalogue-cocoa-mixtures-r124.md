# R124 · Dry mixtures of cocoa and sugars

The worksheet checks a proposed name for a dry mixture intended for direct consumption under FPSFA 2.7.6. The cocoa powder percentage comes from the recipe. The cocoa butter percentage comes from the specification or laboratory report for the input powder on a dry basis. The finished mix has a separate moisture maximum of 7% by mass. [2018 Gazette, PDF pp. 21–22](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification/Gazette_Notification_Pulses_Grains_Maize_14_08_2018.pdf#page=21) · [FSSAI Chapter 2.7, PDF pp. 10–12](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Chapter%202_7%20%28Sweets%20and%20Confectionary%29.pdf#page=10)

| Name family | Cocoa powder in the finished mix |
|---|---:|
| Sweetened cocoa, sweetened cocoa powder or drinking chocolate | At least 25% |
| Sweetened cocoa mix or sweetened mixture with cocoa | At least 20% |
| Sweetened cocoa-flavoured mix | Below 20% |

Within each row there are three classes. Cocoa butter in the input powder is at least 20% for the unqualified name, at least 10% and below 20% for fat-reduced, and below 10% for highly fat-reduced. The first two cocoa powder rows state minimums. The helper checks the proposed name without creating an upper limit for the middle row. [FSSAI naming table, PDF p. 11](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Chapter%202_7%20%28Sweets%20and%20Confectionary%29.pdf#page=11)

Chocolate powder has its own definition in paragraph (2)(c). The Gazette gives 32% by weight and a parenthetical 29% on a dry-matter basis. Enter each available figure on its reported basis. The helper flags disagreement and does not derive a dry-basis result from the recipe percentage. [2018 Gazette, PDF p. 22](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification/Gazette_Notification_Pulses_Grains_Maize_14_08_2018.pdf#page=22)

The standard permits spices. For a spiced mix, record the actual recipe in the crop contaminant worksheet. The existing food entry for naturally occurring saffrole names mace and nutmeg together. From 1 December 2026, the amended entry names foods and beverages containing mace or nutmeg kernel, or both. The maximum under these entries is 10 ppm. [Existing Gazette, PDF p. 4](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/Gazette_Notification_Crop_Contaminants_Natural_Toxic.pdf#page=4) · [May 2026 amendment, PDF p. 5](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=5)
