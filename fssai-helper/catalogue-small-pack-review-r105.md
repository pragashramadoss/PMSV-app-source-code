# R105 · Small-pack artwork declarations

Open **Product worksheet → Packaging, labelling and claims → Small-pack declarations**. Identify the SKU, artwork revision and pack. Enter the formed pack’s visible area, its measurement basis and the matching retail outer. Record where each applicable declaration appears. **Save small-pack check** adds this artwork to the product brief and register. **Export declaration placements as CSV** gives one line per field per saved retail artwork, including the observed location and any outstanding evidence.

## Measuring a wrapper

FAQ 24 uses all visible surfaces of the formed pack and excludes horizontal and vertical seals. Include visible sides or gussets. A single principal display panel or the total area of the unformed film cannot by itself establish eligibility. The worksheet accepts a measured total in cm² or mm². Each 100 mm² equals 1 cm². An assessment can be saved while a measurement or matching outer is still pending. [FSSAI FAQ 24, p. 4](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQ_Labelling_Display_01_11_2023.pdf#page=4)

| Visible area of an individual pack | Fields in regulation 8(1) | Manufacture and expiry/use-by dates under 8(1)(a) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 29.99 cm² | May be carried on the corresponding retail multi-unit pack | May also be carried on that pack |
| 30.00 cm² | May be carried on the corresponding retail multi-unit pack | Review on the individual pack under the ordinary date provisions |
| 100.00 cm² | May be carried on the corresponding retail multi-unit pack | Review on the individual pack under the ordinary date provisions |
| 100.01 cm² | Review on the individual pack | Review on the individual pack under the ordinary date provisions |

The two provisions use different boundaries. For dates, also check product-specific rules, the distinction between manufacture and packaging dates, and the short-shelf-life provision where relevant. [FSSAI compendium, pp. 21–22](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf#page=21) · [September 2022 Gazette amendment](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2022/09/631f1f96efc35Gazette_Notification_FSS_Label_12_09_2022.pdf)

## Reading the declarations

| Field | Review decision |
| --- | --- |
| Brand owner’s identity | Check the brand owner’s name and applicable role qualifier. The address has its own barcode option under 8(5)(a). The 100 cm² list does not include the brand owner’s name or address. |
| Separate manufacturer, packer, marketer or bottler | FAQ 34 places the ordinary name-and-address duty under 5(6)(a) on the brand owner. Check the different operator’s **licence number** under 5(7)(b). FAQ 34 continues onto p. 6 with a Legal Metrology cross-reference. Rule 6(1)(a), explanation III, refers food packages back to food law for this name-and-address clause. |
| FSSAI logo and applicable licence numbers | Regulation 8(1) lists the licence number and FSSAI logo and separately identifies the importer’s name and address. The placement worksheet applies the licence/logo provision to domestic as well as imported foods. The text runs the logo and importer items together without a comma. Its stated multi-unit condition still applies. |
| Imported food | Check the Indian importer’s name, address and licence. Review the country of origin independently. For food made abroad and packed or bottled in India, check the Indian packing or bottling premises separately under 5(6)(b). |
| Other fields | The 100 cm² list also covers ingredients, batch identification, nutrition, food additives, irradiation information and instructions for use, where applicable. Review the food name, separate allergen disclosure and other product-specific declarations on their own terms. |
| Barcode, GTIN or QR | Regulation 8(5) names the brand owner’s **address** and a **different operator’s licence number**. FAQ 43 includes QR codes. Record the actual decoded information or a scan reference. A batch prefix or generic homepage alone does not establish that a declaration is accessible. |

A retail multi-unit pack contains at least two individually packaged or labelled units of the same food. Their net quantities can differ, and the units may be sold separately or as a pack. Record the retail presentation used for this SKU. A shipping carton, an assortment of different foods or an unidentified outer does not by itself establish this route. An outer displayed for retail sale also has its own declaration duties. Regulation 4(8) addresses transparent outers and legible markings on an enclosed retail unit. [FSSAI definitions, p. 2](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf#page=2) · [Retail outer presentation, p. 5](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf#page=5)

The worksheet covers 20 identity, licence, date and other declaration fields. It records the reviewer’s observations. Other logos and symbols, Schedule II warnings, Legal Metrology quantity, price and consumer-care declarations, and material-specific marking require separate checks. The March 2026 final amendment covering notified logos commences on 1 July 2027. FSSAI also issued an earlier operational direction covering logos. Check the applicable instrument when reviewing an individual symbol. Non-retail containers follow regulation 10. Sources checked 21 November 2026.
