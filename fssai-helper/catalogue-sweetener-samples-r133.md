# Misri, dried glucose syrup and trehalose

Reviewed 24 November 2026. The three product standards provide eight numerical composition or purity checks. Enter the sample or lot, laboratory report reference, result as a percentage and confirmation of the analytical basis in the product worksheet. A numerical result applies to the selected parameter for that lot.

| Sample | Check | Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finished Misri | Extraneous matter | ≤ 0.1% by weight |
| Finished Misri | Total ash | ≤ 0.4% by weight |
| Finished Misri | Total sugar expressed as sucrose | ≥ 98% by weight |
| Dried glucose syrup as sold | Total solids | ≥ 93% by weight |
| Dried glucose syrup as sold | Reducing sugars | ≥ 20% by weight |
| Dried glucose syrup as sold | Sulphated ash | ≤ 1.0% by weight |
| Crystalline trehalose as supplied | Loss on drying | ≤ 1.5% |
| Crystalline trehalose as supplied | Total ash | ≤ 0.05% |

The original product standards identify the sampled material and basis for the first six entries. The 2021 amendment specifies the two trehalose checks and refers to the JECFA (2000) analytical methods. ## Lead in Misri

CTR 2.1.1(2) sets a lead maximum of 5.0 ppm by mass (5.0 mg/kg) for the sugar entry where sulphated ash exceeds 1.0%. Use this conditional check when the finished Misri is documented as sugar under that entry. Record the classification and the sulphated-ash result, method and lot. Match the lead report to that lot. At exactly 1.0% the condition is unmet. Misri's total ash, the grade of an incoming sugar and a hard-boiled candy classification cannot establish that this condition is met. Assess any failure of the separate composition criteria independently. The December amendment retains this sugar entry. [Sugar lead entry, PDF p. 18](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=18) · [December amendment, PDF p. 4](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=4)

For dried glucose syrup, sulphated ash above 1.0% already exceeds its product standard. For trehalose, the specification measures total ash. Both products remain in the queue for a first named finished-product CTR source. The focused CSV keeps the published conditional lead maximum in a separate column from the laboratory comparison for a documented sample.
