## Source-specific non-interchangeable quality criteria

**2.3.1 Fruits:** liquid-pack drained fruit **≥50% of net contents**, solid-pack **≥70% of net contents**; rigid container total fill **≥90% of water capacity**. Declare the actual packing medium and strength. Verify fruit species and packaging style.

**2.3.2 Fruit Salad/Cocktail/Mix:** for *sugar-added* juice or syrup, source cut-out strength classes: **10–13.9 °Brix extra light, 14–17.9 light, 18–21.9 heavy, ≥22 extra heavy**. Water-only packs do not automatically acquire these classes. Rigid container fill **≥90% of water capacity**, but **minimum drained fruit weight is 50% of distilled-water capacity**, not 50% of net contents. Separate defect allowances: blemished pieces **≤2 per 100 g drained fruit**, peel **≤6.5 cm² per 500 g total contents**, seed/extraneous vegetative matter **≤2 g per 500 g total contents**. Packing-medium-specific names and sweetening labels require source and current-law review.

**2.3.3 Vegetables:** liquid-pack mushroom, green beans, carrots, peas, corn and other vegetables **≥50% drained weight of net contents**; specifically **edible fungi/vegetables including baked beans packed in sauce ≥25%**; solid pack **≥70%**. Rigid container total fill **≥90% of water capacity**. Label must identify vegetables and the percentage range of each, plus packing medium and strength. **Do not use 25% for every vegetable or borrow fruit cocktail's water-capacity denominator.**

## Safety and regulatory holds

- These are source *quality* and identity criteria, not proof of heat-process lethality, microbiological safety, commercial sterility or pack integrity.
- Actual SKU fruit/vegetable species, recipe, thermal process, package type, declared style, measured drained weight, sampling and validated analytical method are unavailable.
- FSSR 2.3 identity does not automatically select any named finished-food CTR contaminant/pesticide/mycotoxin food article. Verify the currently effective original Gazette, amendments, additives, Appendix B, labels and full actual test panel.
