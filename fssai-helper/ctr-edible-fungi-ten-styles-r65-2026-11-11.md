### Defects and other primary conditions

- **Dried fungi:** source page 98–99 differentiates Shii-ta-ke organic impurities **≤1.0%**, other dried fungi organic impurities **≤0.02%**, and mineral impurities **≤2.0%**. Dried-fungi damage table records maggot damage **1.0% total including ≤0.5% serious**, crushed fungi **≤6.0%**, carbonized fungi **≤2.0%** and damaged fungi **≤20.0%** as *distinct defect classifications*, not a 1% combined defect budget.
- **Grits and powder:** grits means coarsely ground dried fungi of one species; powder passes a **200-micron sieve**. Unlike dried whole fungi, powder has a **9%** moisture ceiling.
- **Pickled fungi:** must be cleaned, washed, blanched, soaked in vinegar and **pasteurized in hermetically sealed packaging**. It may be made from one or more fungi species. The source vinegar as acetic acid criterion is a **maximum 2.0%** and must not be misread as an acidification minimum or pH control.
- **Fermented fungi:** preserved by **lactic acid fermentation and salt**, one species. The **4% maggot-damaged-fungi** allowance printed for this style is not the standard 1%/0.5% pair printed for several different styles. Actual starter/process/pathogen safety remains an independent condition.
- **Oil-packed fungi:** one species, fresh or previously salted, packed in **airtight containers**, heat treated to resist spoilage. **Do not assume 'in oil' is safe without a validated anaerobic-microbial/heat/pH/aw risk review.** The standard alone does not establish shelf stability or a kill step.
- **Quick-frozen fungi:** cleaned, washed and blanched before fast freezing. Process is **not complete until the thermal centre is −18°C after thermal stabilization**. Inorganic impurity limit is 0.2%; organic 0.02%; damage 1.0% total/0.5% serious.
- **Sterilized fungi:** one or more species, fresh/salted/frozen, packed airtight in **water and salt** and heat treated to prevent spoilage. The published salt maximum **2.0%** does not waive commercial-sterility evidence, actual retort validation or pack-integrity testing.
- **Extract / concentrate:** definition uses fungi juice or dried-fungi water with salt. **Extract: 7% saltless extract**; **concentrate: 24% saltless extract**. **Maximum 20% NaCl** is a different field, and mineral/organic impurities are printed **'none'**.
- **Dried fungi concentrate:** dried from fungi extract or concentrate, **moisture ≤9%; salt ≤5%; mineral and organic impurities 'none'**. It is not dried whole fungi and cannot borrow its moisture limits.
- **Salted fungi:** brine-preserved **semi-processed** fungi, from one species after cleaning/washing/blanching. **NaCl 15–18%** is a range, not just a maximum; different from the finished pickled/oil products.

Printed 'none' for certain impurity parameters must retain the source wording: **never turn this into an unsupported zero ppm, arbitrary LOQ, or a valid batch COA**. The original text defines mineral impurities as residual substances insoluble in HCl after ashing. Defect definitions, methods and units still need matching to representative tests.

## Labels, allergens and style eligibility

The source (PDF page 104) includes **processing term** disclosure, **common and scientific species name** for specified dried/salted/quick-frozen/fermented/pickled/canned forms, **'mixed'** when more than one species is present, prior fungal **processing history** if input not fresh, declaration when **salted fungi were used as input**, and **'stalks added'** when applicable. Composition limits stalk numbers unless an identified exception applies.

The fungi standard permits other specified ingredients, potentially **animal fat, butter, milk, cream and milk powder**. Thus a fungal product is not automatically vegetarian/vegan, allergen-free, or dairy-free. **Actual formulation** and effective allergen/claims law must be independently reviewed.

The official compendium cites 2011 packaging/labelling rules; that historic source wording does **not** establish that 2011 is the only current applicable label framework. Confirm all **currently effective labelling, packaging, additives, microbiology and contaminants requirements** against original Gazettes and amendments before any actual product determination.

## Non-inheritance and fail-closed safety gates

- **Dried whole/sliced:** Species/drying route drives moisture and impurity allowance.
- **Grits / powder:** Do not give powder the grits moisture allowance.
- **Pickled:** Vinegar MAX is not a safe-acidification pH threshold.
- **Fermented:** Fermentation route is not pasteurisation and has own defects.
- **Oil-packed:** Oil pack requires independent thermal/anaerobic hazard review.
- **Quick-frozen:** Air temperature ≠ product thermal centre and freezing ≠ kill step.
- **Sterilized:** Commercial sterility and package validation remain real SKU checks.
- **Extract / concentrate:** Do not confuse saltless extract % and salt %, or use dried variant's limit.
- **Dried concentrate:** Distinct from dried whole fungi and liquid extract.
- **Salted semi-processed:** 15% is a minimum and this is not RTE pickled fungi.

- Never inherit a specific *raw mushroom*, *other vegetable*, *canned vegetable*, or generic fungal CTR metal/toxin/pesticide limit from the FSSR identity alone. Confirm the legally effective **named finished-food CTR article**, processing, species, units, food matrix, method and all applicable current amendments.
- No licence, actual ingredient species, manufacturer/process records, real batch, validated COA or independent full-product legal matrix is embedded in the source screening. No automatic numerical CTR rules or legal PASS.
