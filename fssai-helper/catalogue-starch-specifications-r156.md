The arrowroot worksheet includes nine references from IS 1006:1984 for lots specifying that edition. Use Figure 1 for Maranta and Figure 2 for Curcuma. Under section 6.2.1 the laboratory tests moisture and starch on individual samples and the remaining requirements on the composite. The CSV identifies the reporting basis and distinguishes the published BIS values from the applicable FSSAI limit. [BIS preview](https://www.services.bis.gov.in/tmp/SR1006.pdf) · [Indian Standard, clauses 3 and 6](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S06/is.1006.1984.pdf)

The enzyme worksheet identifies six named uses from Table 6. The 100 mg/kg entry for alpha-amylase INS 1100(i) uses flour mass. The other five entries specify good manufacturing practice. The preparation itself requires a matching identity, activity and purity specification. [FSSAI Table 6](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A(4).pdf#page=189)

These sources cover additives, food identity and quality. Nine products still await their first product-specific contaminant, toxin or residue reference on 26 November. The 1 December plan has ten.
