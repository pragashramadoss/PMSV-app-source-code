The first three criteria assess process hygiene at the end of manufacture. The four food safety criteria apply at the end of manufacture and during shelf life. ## Pesticide residues in five crop products

The finished-product plans now identify the two materials to compare for coffee–chicory mixture, coconut milk powder, cocoa beans, cocoa mass/liquor/cake and arrowroot starch. For example, use the actual rhizome and purified starch for arrowroot. For cocoa, identify whether the tested fraction is nib, liquor or press cake. For the coffee blend, retain the coffee and chicory results separately and record the recipe and the extraction or roasting process.

FSSAI’s MRL guidance describes a processing factor as the residue concentration in the processed fraction divided by that in the corresponding raw agricultural commodity. The result depends on the pesticide, commodity and process. Record the residue definition, linked samples, analytical units, reporting limits, and the evidence supporting use of a factor. The guidance discusses how limits are set. It supplies no new numerical Indian limit for these five finished products. [FSSAI guidance, section 9, pages 45 and 53](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Guidance_Document_SOP_MRL_16_03_2022.pdf#page=45)
