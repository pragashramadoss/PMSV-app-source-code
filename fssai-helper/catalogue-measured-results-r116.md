# R116 · Measured results in individual product plans

Enter a measured composition value and its report reference in the product worksheet. The result appears beside the selected criterion in **Testing plan and source decisions** and in **Download this product’s testing plan**. The row identifies the sample or SKU when supplied, the reporting basis and the outcome. Changing the selected criterion or product variant clears the earlier measurement.

For metals, open **Check a reported metal result** and select the assessment date. Enter the reported value, unit and laboratory reference, then confirm that the report matches the sampled article and analytical basis. The worksheet and product plan use the same dated provision. Changing editions requires confirmation for the new basis. A report saved in an earlier version remains available for review.

| Sample and assessment | What the Helper shows |
|---|---|
| Besan, lead, 0.3 mg/kg on the documented mass basis | Under the reviewed November assessment the result is within the residual criterion. From 1 December 2026 it exceeds the 0.2 ppm limit for the named pulse flour. |
| Fish oil, arsenic, from 1 December 2026 | Select the reported analyte. Total arsenic above 0.1 mg/kg calls for inorganic arsenic testing. At exactly 0.1 mg/kg total, the notification does not give a screening disposition. A quantified inorganic arsenic result can be compared directly with its maximum. |
| Qualifier or unmatched unit | The reported value is retained with the outstanding comparison step. |

The published catalogue-wide plans contain generic regulatory rows for all 533 products. The product plan includes the measurements entered for that product. **Save worksheet on this device** retains entries for the next session.
