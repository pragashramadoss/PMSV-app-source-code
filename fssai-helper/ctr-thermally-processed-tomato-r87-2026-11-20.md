# Thermally processed tomato purée and paste

Reviewed 20 November 2026. Catalogue entry 189 covers both forms under FSSAI 2.3.14, food category 04.2.2.5. The 2011 notification and FSSAI’s consolidated Chapter 2.3 agree on the two table rows, their reporting basis and the rigid-container fill provision.

| Product declared on the label or specification | Row in 2.3.14(2) | Minimum total soluble solids in the finished product, % by weight |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Tomato purée | 1 | 9.0 |
| Tomato paste | 2 | 25.0 |

Select the declared form before entering a laboratory result. The table gives an inclusive minimum for each form. For example, 12.0% meets the purée minimum and falls below the paste minimum. A high result alone does not identify the product as paste. Changing the declared form starts a fresh lot check.

## Reading the laboratory report

The standard describes the product obtained by concentrating juice from ripe tomatoes, allows salt, and expresses total soluble solids as a percentage by weight. Ketchup and tomato sauce have a separate standard under 2.3.27 with a salt-free solids basis. For purée and paste, compare the laboratory’s final soluble-solids result on the original product basis. A salt-free result, total dry solids, or an instrument reading from a diluted test portion needs clarification before comparison.

The FSSAI fruit and vegetable products manual describes refractometry in section 1.6. It distinguishes sample preparation by consistency, addresses temperature correction and explains how the laboratory expresses a diluted measurement on the original sample basis. Section 3.1 directs the reader to section 1.6 for processed pulp and purée. The manual supplies analytical guidance. The numerical product minimums come from clause 2.3.14(2). ## Container fill and scope

Under 2.3.14(3), rigid containers must be at least 90.0% full. Compare the occupied product volume with the volume of distilled water at 20 °C that completely fills the same container. A 270 mL fill in a 300 mL container meets the minimum exactly. For a flexible pack, the brief records the pack type and omits the rigid-container comparison.

Clause 2.3.14 also addresses permitted additives and Appendix B microbiological requirements. The printable text brief records the declared form, lot reference, reporting basis, comparison, pack measurements and source pages. Product-specific contaminant decisions are tracked separately.
