# R118 · Multiple metal results in a product worksheet

In a product worksheet, open **Check a reported metal result**. Select the assessment date and metal, enter the value exactly as reported, and add the unit and laboratory reference. Use the optional sample or lot identifier to distinguish samples. Confirm the analytical basis after checking the cited article, sample, unit and preparation.

Switch metals to enter another reading. **Entered metal results** shows the readings together, including incomplete entries that still need evidence. Selecting a metal restores its value, unit, sample, reference and confirmation. Each product holds one current reading per metal. For fish oil, total and inorganic arsenic have separate entries. An older arsenic result with an unspecified species stays visible until the reviewer identifies or clears it.

Choose **Save worksheet** to retain the entries in this browser. The product brief and register downloaded from the worksheet include the entries. The selected product testing plan attaches them to the corresponding metal rows. The generic catalogue downloads contain the reviewed source data only.

To correct a reading, select its metal and, for fish-oil arsenic, its species. To remove it from the result table and exports, clear its reported value and save. To retain a previous lot before replacing its reading, download the product plan or brief first.

Changing to a different assessment edition retains the reported values and clears the analytical-basis confirmations. Changing the documented article for an individual metal requires reconfirmation for that metal. Recheck the sample against the applicable source before confirming again. Qualified results and unresolved article or species choices keep their review messages.

The worksheet reads the selected measurement saved by earlier versions. Measurements overwritten before R118 can only be recovered from a separate saved record.
