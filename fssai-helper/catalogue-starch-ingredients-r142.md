## Trehalose

FPSFA 3.3.4 has a total-ash maximum of 0.05%. A total-ash determination cannot be substituted for the sulphated-ash evidence required by CTR 2.1.1(2). The threshold excludes a sulphated-ash result of exactly 1.0%. A result assessed under the CTR group entry still requires evaluation against all applicable purity and purchase specifications. ## Dried glucose syrup

FSSAI category 11.1.3 includes glucose syrups produced from starch or inulin. CTR 2.2.1(2), serial 2, covers specified flours and their products. It does not expressly name dried glucose syrup. A generic declaration of tapioca starch, an inulin or maize source, or cassava origin alone leaves the group classification unresolved. The product standard and the flour-source classification remain separate checks.
