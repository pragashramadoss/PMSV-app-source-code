## Distinct legal source descriptions

- **2.3.13 thermally processed concentrate:** unfermented but fermentable fruit/vegetable juice, pulp or puree concentrated by water removal to **at least twice the original prescribed soluble solids** under **2.3.6/2.3.7**. Natural volatiles may be restored. Appropriate heat preservation before/after sealing is described. No universal fixed Brix is invented.
- **2.3.16 industrial juice/pulp/puree with preservatives:** mechanically obtained from sound ripe fruit/vegetables, unfermented but fermentable, may be pulpy/turbid/clear. Requires applicable **Appendix A additives** and **Appendix B microbiology**. **No twice-original soluble-solids condition is printed in this clause.**
- **2.3.17 industrial concentrated juice/pulp/puree with preservatives:** unfermented but fermentable, with water removal yielding **at least twice the prescribed original soluble solids** under 2.3.6/2.3.7. Requires applicable **Appendix A additives** and **Appendix B microbiology**.

All three specify **≥90% rigid-container fill as percentage of water capacity**, measured using distilled water at **20°C**. This is neither a net-quantity nor a microbial lethality or commercial sterility approval.
