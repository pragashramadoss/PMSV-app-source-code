# Salt substitutes, polyols, enzymes and cocoa processing aids

Reviewed 25 November 2026. Of the 533 catalogue products, 517 have a partial or conditional contaminant, toxin or residue source. Sixteen still need their first source. This review adds one qualifying finished-product route.

## Salt substitutes

The salt-substitute standard allows cereal flour as a diluent. When the approved recipe includes cereal flour, the finished mixture is a food containing a cereal product under CTR 2.2.1(1). An incoming flour sample belongs to the separate cereal row. Record the flour identity, recipe or supplier reference, sample role and lot. A mineral-only product or a formulation diluted solely with sugar needs a separate assessment. [Salt-substitute Gazette, p. 6](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Special_Dietary_Food_22_06_2017.pdf#page=6) · [Contaminant Gazette, p. 29](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=29)

| Documented sample | Total aflatoxins maximum | Aflatoxin B1 maximum |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Finished salt substitute containing cereal flour | 20 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |
| Separately sampled incoming cereal flour | 15 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |

These cereal and composite-food entries apply to the two tested assessment dates, 25 November and 1 December 2026. The December amendment changes the oil and oilseed wording in the aflatoxin table. [2026 amendment, p. 5](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=5)

The sodium maximum is 120 mg per 100 g of the finished mixture. The product standard also specifies the label name and declarations for sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, ammonium and choline per 100 g. [Salt-substitute Gazette, pp. 5–6](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Special_Dietary_Food_22_06_2017.pdf#page=6)

## Polyols

FoSCoS lists sorbitol separately in category 11.6 under regulation 3.2.16(1). The general 11.6 polyol entry excludes separately standardized products. For a confirmed sorbitol powder lot the published maxima are 1 mg/kg lead, 3 mg/kg arsenic and 2 mg/kg nickel. Its purity specification gives alternative minima of 91% or 99% depending on the method used. Check the method before interpreting a purity result. The general category still needs a source for the actual product. [FoSCoS product list](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product) · [Sorbitol specification, pp. 88–89](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Chapter%203_Substances%20added%20to%20food(1).pdf#page=88)

## Enzyme preparations and cocoa

FoSCoS 99.2 excludes enzyme preparations covered by the processing-aid or nutraceutical provisions. Appendix C, Table 11, row 51 names chymosin for milk coagulation in paneer and cheese at good manufacturing practice. The note gives a 5 mg/kg lead maximum for the covered enzyme preparation. Verify the named source and use before assigning that specification. The generic 99.2 entry still needs a matching source. [FoSCoS categories 99.2 and 99.4, p. 9](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/Compiled%20FPC_FoSCoS.pdf#page=9) · [Appendix C, p. 40](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20C(2).pdf#page=40)

For cocoa mass and cake, Appendix C, Table 12, row 2 lists ammonium carbonate (INS 503(i)) for pH control. The residue condition is GMP. The worksheet records the processing-aid provision separately from the open contaminant review. [Appendix C, p. 40](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20C(2).pdf#page=40)
