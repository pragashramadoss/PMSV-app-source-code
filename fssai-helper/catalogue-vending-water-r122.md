# R122 · Purified vending-water testing plan

Open **Product finder**, search for **purified vending water**, and open its worksheet. Under **Testing plan and source decisions**, select **Vending water · IS 10500**. Download the dedicated 74-entry CSV for a laboratory review. The same entries appear in the individual product plan and both full-catalogue plans.

## Sample scope

FSSR 2.10.9 covers water offered or sold from a decentralised system that purifies and dispenses it. It requires visually clear water free from sediment, suspended particles and extraneous matter, and incorporates IS 10500. Check the machine and outlet reference. Packaged drinking water, natural mineral water and captive installations fall outside this article. Ingredient water has its own evidence assessment. [Gazette, p. 4](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Drinking_Water_05_02_2019.pdf#page=4)

## Reading the plan

The 74 source entries cover six physical or sensory characteristics, 24 general chemical entries, 16 toxic-substance entries, two radioactivity entries, 18 pesticides, five bacteriological entries and three further clauses. One toxic-substance entry points to the pesticide table. Bacteriological entries distinguish drinking water, water entering distribution and water in distribution. Retain the indicated sampling volume. Each entry links to its source and applicable amendments. Use the acceptable limit first. A higher permissible value requires evidence that an alternative source is unavailable and that the row's other conditions apply. Free residual chlorine has a consumer-end minimum under its stated conditions. The iron and manganese rows retain the open question about their combined concentration.

## Comparing one laboratory result

Select the sampled outlet and enter the machine/sample reference and report reference. Choose one of the 15 Table 3 parameters with a numeric maximum and no relaxation. Enter the reported result or a numeric upper bound in mg/L or µg/L. Confirm the analyte and method on the report. A bound above the maximum yields an inconclusive comparison. The current reading and its references appear in the individual product export. Use **Save worksheet on this device** to keep the entry for a later session.

The bulk testing plans contain reference criteria only. This addition does not change the catalogue's separate count of 32 products awaiting a named CTR source or determine that a water supply is fit for release.
