# R115 · Worksheet evidence in the product testing plan

Open a product worksheet, enter the sample or formulation details, then open **Testing plan and source decisions**. **Download this product’s testing plan** exports the same decisions shown on screen. Use **Save worksheet** to retain the selections on this device. Pesticide results have their own save button and can be recorded for multiple samples and reports.

| Worksheet entry | Effect on the individual testing plan |
|---|---|
| Cocoa or gum variant | Composition shows criteria for the declared grade or form. For gum, the two conditional sugar criteria show their applicability for the selected sweetener formulation. |
| Sample identity, recipe or pack reference | Metals and toxins use the evidence accepted by the corresponding worksheet. A separately tested ingredient is identified as such. An unresolved source interpretation stays open. |
| Saved pesticide report | Each sample and analyte has its own row with the sample role, report reference, reported result, units and assessment. A saved ethylene oxide and 2-chloroethanol assessment retains its reported basis and review outcome. |
| Veterinary report | The plan includes the selected drug and sampled article, the report reference and the worksheet’s comparison. Changes to the assessment date, sample or source row require a fresh check. |

For example, select **Finished food containing a listed food article** for total aflatoxins in hard candy and enter a formulation reference identifying the almond ingredient. The individual plan shows the finished-food criteria for total aflatoxins and aflatoxin B1 along with that reference. With a separately sampled milk ingredient in dairy whitener, the plan identifies the milk sample against its own criterion.

Both catalogue-wide testing plan downloads retain the generic planning rows for all 533 products. The assessment date controls the metal, toxin and veterinary editions. Individual worksheet evidence neither changes those editions nor authorizes batch release.
