# Cocoa, dried egg and decaffeinated coffee

26 November 2026 · R145

In Product finder, **Export cocoa, egg and coffee checks** downloads 22 references for four catalogue entries. Each relevant product also has its own table and download. In the individual testing plan, selecting a regular coffee or regular coffee–chicory blend removes the decaffeination solvent rows.

| Product | References | Conditions that affect interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cocoa mass, liquor or cake | 14 | The category 5.1.1 entries include three exclusions for these standardized products. Several entries specify a final-product or combined-use basis. The Hindi Gazette confirms benzoates at 1,500 mg/kg. The Hindi and English units differ for INS 473. |
| Dried egg product | 4 | EDTA and triethyl citrate use dry yolk weight. EDTA is expressed as anhydrous calcium disodium EDTA. Sorbates are expressed as sorbic acid. |
| Decaffeinated coffee | 2 | Match the sampled coffee and the solvent used in decaffeination. Dichloromethane has a residual maximum of 10 mg/kg and butanone 2 mg/kg. |
| Coffee–chicory mixture | 2 | Use those solvent references for the independently sampled decaffeinated coffee input. A finished blend needs a separate provision. |

For standardized cocoa mass, liquor and cake, note XS141 excludes INS 475, INS 476 (PGPR) and sorbitan esters from their category 5.1.1 use provisions. The Helper leaves the operative maximum blank. The phosphate entry specifies 1,100 mg/kg as phosphorus. Natural phosphorus contributes to a total-phosphorus result. Check the recipe and basis before assessing added phosphates. [FSSAI Appendix A, pp. 167–168](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A(4).pdf#page=167) · [XS141, p. 348](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A(4).pdf#page=348)

The Hindi Gazette gives **1,500 mg/kg for benzoates** where the English text prints “15,00 mg/kg”. For sucrose esters of fatty acids (INS 473), the English Gazette and the current English compendium specify **10 g/kg**. The Hindi Gazette specifies **10 mg/kg**. Both readings appear in the Helper, with the operative comparison left open. [Hindi Gazette, p. 92](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Additive_Harmonization_16_09_2016.pdf#page=92) · [English Gazette, pp. 265–266](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Additive_Harmonization_16_09_2016.pdf#page=266)

For a whole-egg powder report, check which mass was used as the denominator. EDTA is limited to 200 mg/kg and triethyl citrate to 2,500 mg/kg on dry egg-yolk weight. Egg white alone provides no dry yolk basis. Check category 10.2.3 and the additive identity against the product and recipe. [FSSAI Appendix A, pp. 260–261](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A(4).pdf#page=261) · [Notes 21, 42 and 47, pp. 334–335](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A(4).pdf#page=334)
