## Confirmed source corrections
1. **Singhara / water chestnut flour** (FSSAI Chapter 2.3, clause 2.3.64, PDF p.105): manufactured from dried *Trapa bispinosa* or *Trapa quadrispinosa* **nuts**. R21's root/tuber-derived description was incorrect. This flour does not automatically inherit raw root/tuber limits, those for canned chestnuts, or the similarly named Chinese water chestnut.
2. **Purified vending-machine water** (Chapter 2.10, clause 2.10.9, PDF p.24): distinct from packaged drinking water and natural mineral water; references **IS 10500**. Do not inherit packaged/mineral-water CTR article limits based on FCS 14.1.1. Do not activate a 2025 draft vending-water amendment.
3. **Dairy analogues and vegetable-fat/protein frozen desserts** (Chapter 2.1, clauses 2.1.1 and 2.1.15): source/formulation evidence is required before selecting ordinary milk, dehydrated milk or ice-cream contaminant articles. This does not prejudge the legally correct 'similar frozen confections' scope.
4. **Coconut milk, cream and powder** (Chapter 2.3): kernel-derived food is distinct from coconut water/oil; the powder identity may contain maltodextrin and sodium caseinate, so the actual formula matters.
5. **Other guards:** cocoa beans ≠ cocoa powder, carob ≠ cocoa; isolated lactose ≠ dehydrated milk; royal jelly ≠ honey; colostrum ≠ generic milk; fruit pickles ≠ cucumber pickles; manufactured arrowroot ≠ raw roots/tubers; premixes, botanical seeds and Ayurveda recipes require ingredient/processing identity checks.
