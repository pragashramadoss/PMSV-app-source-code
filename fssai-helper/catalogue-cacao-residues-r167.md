# Cacao bean purchase specifications and Indian contaminant sources

Reviewed 28 November 2026. The Codex database lists nine pesticide entries directly against cacao beans (SB 0715). Codex Group 024 specifies the seed as the portion to analyse unless a provision says otherwise. The Helper uses these values for a documented contract or destination requirement. Enter the whole bean lot, specification, laboratory report and residue definition. For cocoa mass, liquor or cake, this check belongs to the separately sampled incoming beans.

## Outstanding Indian sources

The pending-source download records the assessment date, the Indian provision checked, the difference between its named sample and the finished product, and the evidence still needed. It covers nine products on 28 November and ten from 1 December. The additional December entry is drinking water sold through a vending machine. The revised saffrole provision covers beverages and foods containing mace or nutmeg kernel. The outlet water retains its own specification.

For example, the Indian lead and copper rows for cocoa powder specify different reporting bases. They cannot establish a limit for cocoa beans or pressed cake. The milk aflatoxin M1 rows name liquid milk and two milk powders, and the antibiotic performance limits for honey concern honey production. The pending-source file gives the corresponding checks for each remaining product. General contaminant provisions and checks for incoming materials are available in the product testing plans.
