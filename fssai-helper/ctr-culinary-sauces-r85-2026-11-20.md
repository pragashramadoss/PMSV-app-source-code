# Sauces and culinary pastes under FSSAI 2.3.28

Reviewed 20 November 2026. Clause 2.3.28(2) has four named composition rows. Five product-finder entries map to these rows because culinary sauce and culinary paste have separate catalogue entries. The 2011 Gazette and the 7 May 2025 consolidated chapter agree on the named products, both columns, their reporting bases and the rigid-container provision.

| Catalogue entry | Clause 2.3.28(2) table row | Minimum soluble solids, salt-free % by mass | Minimum acidity, % as acetic acid |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Chilli sauce | 1 | 8.0 | 1.0 |
| Fruit or vegetable sauce | 2 | 15.0 | 1.2 |
| Culinary sauce and culinary paste | 3 | 8.0 | 1.0 |
| Ginger paste | 4 | 3.0 | 1.0 |

Select the finished product that matches its identity. Tomato ketchup, tomato sauce and soybean sauce have their own standards. A recipe containing fruit, vegetables or ginger alone does not identify a particular row. The purchased ginger-paste ingredient in Krishara continues to use row 4 for the supply. The composition figures apply to the identified article.

## Comparing a laboratory result

Enter each reported value and its basis separately. The comparison includes equality at the minimum and works with unrounded decimal values. Blank, invalid and differently expressed results retain distinct outcomes. Uncorrected refractometer readings and pH do not establish the two specified results. Clause 2.3.28 also refers to permitted additives, colour and Appendix B microbiological requirements.

The FSSAI draft of 5 June 2024 proposes an acidity exception for retort or aseptic processing. The consolidated chapter retains the numerical acidity column. The Helper links the draft and uses the published row. A reviewer relying on an exemption needs an effective notification or direction.

## Container fill

Clause 2.3.28(3) specifies a minimum of 90.0% of water capacity for rigid containers. Water capacity is the volume of distilled water at 20 °C needed to fill the closed container completely. Compare the volume occupied by the product with the water capacity of the same container. The Helper accepts both volumes in millilitres. Net mass in grams and the nominal volume on the label cannot be substituted. The product brief includes the pack type, entered volumes and outcome.

For example, 270 mL of product in a rigid container with a 300 mL water capacity gives 90.0%, exactly at the minimum. The Helper keeps full precision for the decision. An ellipsis in a displayed percentage means more decimal places follow. Selecting flexible packaging or leaving the pack unidentified gives no fill verdict. The ordinary composition checks are available for every pack type.

The 533-product catalogue and the 137-case contaminant review register serve different purposes. This review adds composition and fill checks for named products. All 137 product-specific contaminant article decisions remain open.
