## Critical allergen, vegan and product identity guards

**The named FSSR headings "Coconut Milk (Non-Dairy)" and "Coconut Cream (Non-Dairy)" do not mean casein-free.** FSSR 2.3.51 and 2.3.52 expressly allow **sodium caseinate**, **maltodextrin**, and **coconut water**. 2.3.63 coconut milk powder permits sodium caseinate and maltodextrin. Sodium caseinate comes from milk; if present, **milk allergen declaration** must be reviewed against current Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) law, including its declared **Milk** allergen class. A vegan/milk-free claim on caseinate-containing product is unsupported. Do not infer actual caseinate is used in all coconut products: require complete individual SKU formulation and supplier documentation.

Where a label claims **vegan**, verify actual absence of animal-origin inputs/processing aids and necessary current FSSAI vegan logo endorsement separately. Recipe absence of caseinate alone does not prove compliant vegan claim.

**Product style evidence**: light coconut milk is not coconut milk; coconut cream is not cream concentrate; liquid fat % w/w is not powder fat % dry basis. The 90% nominal hermetic fill requirement belongs to the hermetically sealed liquid container condition, not every shipment format.

**CTR contaminant scope**: Product-standard identity and a source plant do not automatically assign exact current metal/pesticide/mycotoxin rows. Do not borrow cow milk, coconut oil, coconut water, raw coconut kernel, or generic fruit/vegetable rows. Current original Gazette and the actual legally applicable finished-product/ingredient panel must be checked, not marked exempt.
