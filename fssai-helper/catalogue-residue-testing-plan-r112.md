Each product receives a sampling decision for both residue areas. Additional rows represent candidate analytes for a named article, production group or sampled portion. A product assembled from several ingredients can have only a sample-selection decision. Record the actual recipe, process, species or sample when resolving it. The row count includes these decisions and conditional entries.

## Pesticides

The agricultural table is linked to the commodity, pesticide and printed residue definition. The processing clarification governs its application to agricultural commodities and physically processed foods. For boiled confectionery, the plan directs the reviewer to distinguish the finished candy from a separately sampled sugarcane ingredient. Source uncertainties, including the copper cross-reference and competing maize or rice entries, retain the source wording in the notes and leave the numeric comparison field empty. [2018 Gazette](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf) · [January 2021 scope clarification](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/01/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf)

The plain-tea plan includes the tea rows of the underlying table, the five operationalised MRLs, the six additional laboratory tests and the 20 entries in the banned-insecticide annexure. Later directed values take precedence for their specified tea entries. The annexure sets an extraneous MRL of 0.05 mg/kg for DDT and 0.01 mg/kg for the other 19 entries. Confirm the residue definition when interpreting a laboratory result. Green-tea entries retain an article-specific review. The October 2026 tea proposal supplies no operative value in this edition. [2023 MRL direction](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2023/04/644b6627e7923Direction_Fixation%20of%20MRL%20of%20Pesticides%20in%20Tea.pdf) · [Banned-insecticide annexure](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2023/12/656dbd1d58845Order%20for%20testing%20banned%20pesticides.pdf#page=2) · [March 2024 direction](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2024/03/65e6f53962cfbDirection_04.03.2024.pdf) · [FSSAI WTO/SPS notifications](https://www.fssai.gov.in/notifications.php?notification=wto-sps-notification)

For 36 spice and culinary-herb identities, the plan calls for a documented pesticide-specific decision under the FSSAI, registration and Codex hierarchy. [April 2024 spice order](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2024/04/6616351c775b5Order%20MRL%20Spices%20and%20culinary%20herbs.pdf)

## Veterinary drugs

The plan distinguishes tolerances for named foods and tissues, extraneous limits for substances prohibited in production, and honey thresholds for detecting antibiotic misuse. For honey, the export preserves all 12 analyte entries, units in µg/kg, and both individual-or-collective qualifications. Seafood production prohibitions require farmed origin. Each conditional fishery-product row carries its classification question. Identical source rows for alternative eligible species are grouped while retaining the precise article and sampled portion. [Seafood table](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Contaminants_Regulations.pdf#page=19) · [October 2024 Gazette](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=17)

The assessment edition runs through 30 November 2026. The seafood entries include a dated reminder to consult the amendment that takes effect on 1 December. Overlapping provisions retain the printed values in the notes pending interpretation. [Commencement and amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=4)
