# Carob powder and raw-material residue checks

Reviewed 26 November 2026. Of 533 catalogue products, 523 have at least one partial or conditional finished-product reference under the Indian Contaminants, Toxins and Residues Regulations (CTR). Ten still need a first such reference.

## Carob powder

FSSAI product standard 2.11.7 identifies carob powder as a husk-free powder from roasted carob pods. The product name alone does not establish which part of the pod was milled. Batlle and Tous describe a process in which the processor separates the seeds, then roasts and mills the pulp for sale as carob powder. The seeds enter a separate gum-manufacturing process. [FSSAI product standard, 2.11.7](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Food_Additives_Regulations.pdf#page=129) · [Batlle and Tous, section 12.11](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/f8b6bb44-ec81-4f3b-b45b-e6e03c2a6faf/content)

Select the pulp-only form. Record the supplier specification and manufacturing process, the basis for the classification, the sampled powder lot and the as-supplied reporting basis. The laboratory comparison also requires the matching sample identifier and mass-based units. Whole-pod powder containing seed, blends, isolated seed material and locust bean gum need their own assessment. The reference CSV shows the published values. The unspecialised testing plan leaves the applied limits blank until the worksheet records the evidence.

## Sampling before processing

Four added entries cover separately sampled raw *Maranta arundinacea* rhizomes under the vegetable entries for the combined benomyl and carbendazim residue, carbendazim, dichlorvos and malathion including malaoxon. Confirm the species, commodity group, raw intake lot and sampling point. Purification separates arrowroot starch from the rhizome. [FSSAI pesticide Gazette, pp. 32–42](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=32) · [FSSAI arrowroot standard, p. 83](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Food_Additives_Regulations.pdf#page=83)

Six added egg entries cover 2,4-D, the combined benomyl and carbendazim residue, carbendazim, edifenphos, ethion including its oxygen analogue, and phenthoate. All six specify the shell-free basis. Take the whole-egg sample before processing and retain its intake lot. A result for a dried powder, separated yolk or separated white requires a provision applicable to that sample. [FSSAI pesticide Gazette, pp. 31–45](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=31)

Serials 13 and 23 overlap. Select the entry corresponding to the laboratory's reported residue definition. Do not add the limits. The residue entries also serve surveillance purposes. India lists benomyl and dichlorvos as banned for use. [Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage, pp. 1–2](https://ppqs.gov.in/sites/default/files/list_of_pesticides_which_are_banned_refused_registration_and_restricted_in_use_0.pdf)
