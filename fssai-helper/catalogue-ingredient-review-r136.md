# Silver leaf, edible catechu and cocoa beans

The 24 November 2026 review brings the catalogue to 515 of 533 products with at least one partial or conditional contaminant, toxin or residue source. Eighteen products still need their first such source.

## Silver leaf

The supplied leaf must meet the silver-leaf standard and be documented as colour INS 174 for the intended use. The colour listing in Appendix A identifies silver in a specific food category. Check the permission for the actual receiving food. The worksheet requires the supplier specification, use reference, lot and dry colouring matter basis. A reported value must identify the same lot. A decorated sweet, carrier preparation or leaf weighed with its backing requires a separate assessment.

| Test | Maximum on dry colouring matter | Additional evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | 10 mg/kg | Colour other than caramel |
| Copper | 30 mg/kg | Colouring matter |
| Arsenic | 5 mg/kg | Independent reference establishing the non-synthetic classification |

The leaf standard specifies a maximum mass per area of 2.8 g/m², minimum fineness of 999 parts per 1,000, uniform sheets free of creases and folds, and manufacture without material of animal origin.

## Catechu and cocoa beans

Catechu has four common comparisons. Its fifth depends on the subtype. The acid-insoluble ash maximum on a dry basis is 0.5% for ordinary catechu and 1.5% for Bhatti Katha. Switching the subtype clears the earlier measurement. [FPSFA 2.11.2, p. 128](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Food_Additives_Regulations.pdf#page=128)

For whole cocoa beans the maximum moisture is 8% by mass. The maxima by count are 4% mouldy, 8% slaty, 2% insect-damaged and 4% germinated and flat combined. The worksheet links each criterion to its Gazette page. Both cocoa beans and catechu still need a matching contaminant, toxin or residue source. [FPSFA 2.3.54, pp. 28–29](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Standards_Fruits_FPSFA_09_08_2017.pdf#page=28)
