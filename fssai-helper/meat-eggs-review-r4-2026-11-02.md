## Corrections and applicability controls

- Removed the unconditional exact mapping of smoked sausage to semi-cooked meat. Final heat treatment and whether further cooking is required determine the candidate route; smoking alone is insufficient.
- Removed the unconditional exact mapping of whole-cut fermented meat to the Appendix-B fermented definition, which specifies chopped/ground meat. The candidate remains visible, subject to scope assessment.
- Flagged the Chapter 2.5 ready-to-eat pickle wording versus Table 5 categories 1–5 cooking instruction rather than silently treating those provisions as equivalent.
- Restricted the seven-day liquid storage sentence to egg whites at 4°C or below. It is not a seven-day permission for liquid yolk or whole egg.
- Restored boneless-meat scope, separate bovine fat alternatives and chilled/frozen definitions alongside storage provisions. The two-to-four-day consumption phrase is not a minimum product age.
- Scoped pickled-meat and low-moisture-meat storage exceptions without implying a validated ambient shelf life.
- Preserved species-dependent fat caps, lean-meat/MRM denominators and claim thresholds. Sausage inherits comminuted requirements alongside its own limits.
- Restored fresh-egg collection/packing timing and unwashed/not-dry-cleaned identity requirements.
- Kept frozen-egg maintenance at -40 to -23.3°C as printed, separate from generic meat storage, and treated pasteurisation temperature/time as paired conditions.
- Separated pickling-solution pH and % w/v preparation salt from finished-egg parameters.
- Separated process-hygiene from food-safety stages. Sampling checks use individual units and complete n/c/m/M plans, not a mean or single result.
- Retained qualitative test portions and the poultry Salmonella-serovar note. No unsupported poultry-only Campylobacter exception was created.
