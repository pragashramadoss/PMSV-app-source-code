# Incoming ingredient checks and outstanding finished-product evidence

25 November 2026 · R139

The register covers 533 marketed products. Of these, 517 have at least one partial or conditional contaminant or residue source. For the remaining 16, the product finder and pending-source export identify the sample or classification issue and the evidence needed to resolve it. An ingredient report can support intake acceptance while the finished-product assessment stays open.

| Product worksheet | Sample collected before processing | Reference for the intake lot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee–chicory mixture | Raw coffee beans | Copper 30 mg/kg, ochratoxin A 5 µg/kg and monocrotophos 0.1 mg/kg |
| Coffee–chicory mixture | Dried or roasted chicory | Copper 30 mg/kg and arsenic 4 mg/kg |
| Egg powder | Whole hen egg contents, sampled shell-free before drying | Monocrotophos 0.02 mg/kg, phorate 0.05 mg/kg and pirimiphos-methyl 0.05 mg/kg. For phorate, the residue definition includes its oxygen analogue and their sulphoxides and sulphones, expressed as phorate. |
| Coconut milk, cream and milk powder | Coconut sampled before processing | The copper oxychloride and copper sulphate rows direct the analyst to CTR 2.1 for elemental copper. Identify the applicable metal article and basis before comparing. |
| Coconut milk and cream | Separately sampled raw coconut water, if added | Fenpyroximate 0.02 mg/kg. Use a mass-based result for the sampled coconut water. |

The copper and arsenic entries are on pages 23–24 of the 2020 metal Gazette. The coffee ochratoxin A entry took effect on 1 April 2025. The December 2026 amendment leaves these entries in place. [Metal Gazette](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=23) · [Ochratoxin A amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=17) · [December amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=4)

The pesticide references are on Gazette pages 35, 39 and 44–46. Page 51 explains the copper cross-reference. Record the active substance, sampled lot, preparation, reporting unit and residue definition. Assess a roasted blend, dried egg powder or processed coconut product on its own legal and analytical basis. [Pesticide Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=35) · [FSSAI processing clarification](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/whatsnew/advisories-orders/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf#page=1)

The coconut milk and cream standards allow coconut water as an ingredient. The separate powder standard describes drying coconut milk extracted from kernels and identifies maltodextrin and sodium caseinate as optional additions. [Coconut milk and cream standards, clauses 2.3.51–52](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Food_Additives_Regulations_01_11_2022.pdf) · [Coconut milk powder standard](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/5f362b888233bGazette_Notification_FSS_Additives_Third_Amendment_14_08_2020.pdf#page=17)
