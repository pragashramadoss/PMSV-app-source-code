## Botanical and recipe boundaries

The monograph describes natural camphor obtained by hydrodistillation, naming **Cinnamomum camphora (L.) Nees & Eberm.**, leaves/chipped wood/roots, and **Ocimum kilimandscharicum Guerke**, whole plant. The synthetic-camphor note describes a racemic, optically inactive form; it is not a substitution permission for an FSSAI recipe.

- **14.5 Dugdha Kupika**, PDF page 95 / printed page 59, ingredient 5: Karpura; `Camphor/Cinnamomum camphora (L.) J.Presl`; part `Sub. extract`; Q.S. The method uses Karpura in the sugar syrup. The shared species name is a source lead only: botanical-authority reconciliation, actual provenance, extraction, food-use grade, adopted criteria, use quantity and safety still need review. The monograph's Ocimum entry does not expand this recipe.
- **14.6 Sugandhit Ksheera**, PDF pages 96–97 / printed pages 60–61: Karpura is method-only and absent from the 13-row ingredient table. No ingredient is inserted into that table. Every material choice retains the existing official-recipe alignment hold; this reference cannot repair it.

The medicinal dose and therapeutic claims on page 211 are deliberately not imported into food-use rules. The published Q.S. recipe quantity and the assay minimum are not converted into a safe food dose. Under Ayurveda Aahara regulation 3(5), adopted ingredient purity criteria and their disclosure remain a distinct FBO review.
