# Import records for egg powder, cocoa beans and coffee beans

Reviewed 26 November 2026. In the product worksheet, open **Import documents for this consignment**. Select whether the article matches, enter the lot and country of origin, and record the references checked. Save the worksheet to retain them on the device. The individual product testing plan includes the entered references. The full-catalogue plans and the dedicated import CSV contain the general requirements.

| Article imported into India | Receiving check |
| --- | --- |
| Egg powder | Match the overseas manufacturing site and its egg powder registration to the FSSAI ReFoM entry. Match the sanitary import permit to the shipment. Verify the Bill of Entry port code against the designated high-risk food port list applicable on the shipment date. |
| Fermented and dried cocoa beans for processing or industrial use | Schedule VI, serial 644 covers these beans from any country. Verify the five pest and disease declarations and the treatment endorsement on the phytosanitary certificate for the lot. Record the approval reference if an alternative treatment was used. |
| Coffee beans for consumption or processing | Schedule VI, serial 90 covers beans from any country. Verify both coffee berry borers in the declaration and the treatment endorsement on the certificate issued at origin or re-export. |

The cocoa mass, liquor and cake worksheet applies the cocoa checks to its incoming imported bean lot. The coffee–chicory worksheet does the same for incoming imported coffee beans. For the egg powder worksheet, select the import option when the powder itself is imported. When a different article or domestic consignment is under review, select **Outside this import route**.

The dedicated export has 11 rows across five catalogue worksheets. A recorded document number identifies evidence for review. It does not establish shipment clearance or the residue level in a finished food. For the 533-product contaminant review, nine products on 26 November and ten on 1 December still await a first product-specific domestic contaminant, toxin or residue provision.
