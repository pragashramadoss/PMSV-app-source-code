# Supplier evidence for flowers in recipes 14.6 and 14.7

Reviewed 19 November 2026.

## What the recipe identifies

Recipe 14.7 lists aromatic flowers at Q.S. Its footnote gives Ketaki (*Pandanus tectorius*) and Jati (*Jasminum officinale*) as examples. The method removes the flowers after infusing them in water, before adding buffalo milk. The footnote leaves the actual species and the supplied form open. Recipe 14.6 separately names Jati at ingredient 9. The published titles and preparation methods of these two recipes still need authoritative clarification. ## When the raw Jati references are relevant

The supplier must identify a separately supplied flower of *Jasminum officinale* and confirm naturally dried, food-grade material supplied as a culinary herb for this comparison. Codex lists this species at HH 3231. Its herb group covers certain plants used in small amounts to flavour food, including flowers in naturally dried form. The Indian metal entries refer to dried herbs. This combination supports checking these entries against the supplied ingredient. The exact Indian article applicable to a specific supply still requires assessment. ## Using the product brief

For recipe 14.7 select the identity and form on the supplier record. A botanical name can be entered for another individually identified flower. An optional document or lot reference travels with the downloaded individual brief. Changing the identity clears the prior form and reference. Selecting fresh flowers, oil or extract, a different species, mixed flowers or unknown material leaves the raw reference unassigned. The brief for recipe 14.6 also records the selected named flower and whether the dried culinary Jati form was confirmed.

The laboratory and product reviewer must assess transfer into the infusion and the finished milk separately.
