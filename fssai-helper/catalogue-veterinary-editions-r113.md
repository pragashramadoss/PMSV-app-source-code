# R113 · Veterinary assessment dates and seafood tolerances

Reviewed 22 November 2026. The effective date for the May 2026 amendment is 1 December 2026. An assessment dated 30 November uses the earlier provisions, including the four seafood tolerances in clause (1). On 1 December, that clause is omitted. Clause (4) then lists seafood under trimethoprim, serial 45, at 0.05 mg/kg and oxolinic acid, serial 50, at 0.3 mg/kg. ## Using the plans

- **Catalogue export.** Set the veterinary assessment date and choose **Export veterinary plan for all 533 products**. Every record and the filename carry the selected date. The complete six-area catalogue export uses the November review edition.
- **Individual product.** Set the date in **Veterinary drug residues** or in the veterinary area of **Testing plan and source decisions**. Both controls use the same product date. The worksheet, source index, product brief, product plan and register with saved assessments use that date. The other five testing areas retain the November review. A saved sample and its report stay associated with the originally selected source row.
- **Seafood sample.** Confirm the marketed form, species and sampled portion. An aquaculture prohibition requires farmed origin. From December, the previous general tetracycline and oxytetracycline rows drop out. A species- or tissue-specific row may still apply, such as oxytetracycline in fish. The comparison still requires a matching laboratory unit and documented sampling basis.
