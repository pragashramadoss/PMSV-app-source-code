# R99 pesticide residues in product worksheets

Reviewed on 21 November 2026 for the 533-product FSSAI catalogue.

The worksheet identifies the sample before showing a pesticide result. A report on incoming sugarcane, for example, can be recorded alongside a hard-candy worksheet. The comparison and the exported brief name the sugarcane lot. A finished candy result has no sugarcane limit in this tool.

## Gazette, processing and purpose of the sample

The offline index contains all 1,098 expanded commodity entries under 213 pesticide serials checked against the 24 December 2018 Gazette. The index preserves original headings, printed limits, page references, qualifiers and compound cells. It distinguishes a maximum at the limit of quantification (asterisk), a fat or carcass-fat calculation, a shell-free sample and a reference to the copper provisions (dollar sign). Searching by pesticide or food also returns source entries outside the sample choices offered in a worksheet. [2018 Gazette, pp. 30–51](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=30)

FSSAI's January 2021 clarification limits the 213-entry table and its general 0.01 mg/kg note to agricultural commodities and the foods produced by physical processing. It excludes thermally and chemically processed foods from that table and note. Other regulations and product-specific provisions still require review. A missing match, an unidentified process, and a result on a different sample each leave the assessment open. [FSSAI processing clarification, paragraph 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/01/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf#page=1)

For spices and culinary herbs, first check the domestic pesticide–commodity entry. Under the 8 April 2024 order, registration with CIB&RC and an FSSAI MRL for another food lead to the applicable Codex MRL. If Codex has none in that branch, the order sets 0.1 mg/kg. The order also sets 0.1 mg/kg for an unregistered pesticide. Registration, scope and the Codex search must be documented. Page 1 contains the revised order. The 2022 documents in the annexures were superseded. [FSSAI spice order, paragraphs 1(a)–(c) and 2](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2024/04/6616351c775b5Order%20MRL%20Spices%20and%20culinary%20herbs.pdf#page=1)

## Laboratory evidence

Choose the marketed product or a separately sampled ingredient, its actual article, and the documented process. Record the sample and laboratory report references. The comparison requires the printed unit and basis. A starred row also needs a validated method LOQ at or below the printed value. Less-than reports compare the upper bound against the limit. A bound above the limit is inconclusive. A bare ND cannot establish a numerical result.

The duplicate carbaryl/maize and carbendazim/rice entries, five chemical-name discrepancies, ambiguous group or subgroup rows, copper references and unresolved sample bases retain explicit review messages. Gazette thresholds describe residues in the food. Separate evidence is required for pesticide-use restrictions, specific import directions, analytical interpretation and a lot-release decision.

The CSV adds eight fields to the 99 R98 fields. It keeps the original columns in order for all 533 products. A saved device worksheet records one selected pesticide comparison for that product. The supplied blank register contains no sample or laboratory entries.
