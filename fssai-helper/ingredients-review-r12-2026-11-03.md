## Substantive controls

1. Ordinary catechu acid-insoluble ash is at most 0.5% dry basis, with a separate Bhatti Katha exception of 1.5%. The ordinary and exception rows are not simultaneously imposed on every sample.
2. Pectin's methanol/ethanol/2-propanol total is at most 1%; carrageenan's named solvents singly or combined are at most 0.1%. Every component and the sum are required; a compliant selected solvent is insufficient.
3. Ingredient purity is not finished-food permission. Metabisulphite thiosulphate limits differ; the ammonium-hydrogen-carbonate iron row is percent, not mg/kg; guar's protein factor is 5.7, not hemp's 6.25. These distinctions are explicit.
4. Detection portions are preserved: for example, Salmonella negative per g for acacia/tragacanth/ghatti/karaya differs from absent in 10 g for alginic acid/guar. Carrageenan's negative-per-test row has no portion and is blocked. No arbitrary 25 g portion or five-sample plan is invented.
5. Hemp seed, oil, flour and finished-beverage THC matrices remain separate. Natural cannabinoid origin, the inclusive 24-month infant restriction and presentation conditions are separate controls. The CBD ceiling is not a fortification permission.
6. Retrieved Claims Schedule I thresholds for solids, liquids and energy basis are retained separately from the conflicting legacy dextrin high-source wording. Six reference rows do not constitute label approval.
7. Previously machine-complete interpretations are blocked for tartaric-acid sulphate units/operator, tragacanth viscosity units, calcium-alginate ash heading/range conflict, carrageenan Salmonella portion and sorbitol method-dependent purity. Prior interpretations remain traceable.
