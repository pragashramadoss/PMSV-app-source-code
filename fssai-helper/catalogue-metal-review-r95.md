The melamine total includes 514 other-food entries and one qualifying powdered Infant Milk Food entry. R95 adds 31 named food-grade ingredients. The 18 open entries comprise seven infant formula or specialised infant products and eleven ingredient, preparation or processing-stage identities. For an actual sample, the worksheet records the label, specification or other source for a conditional selection.

The mercury total includes 12 specific articles and 418 reviewed foods under the residual entry on Gazette page 27. The 103 remaining worksheets identify the food, species, process or sample basis to check. They provide the relevant printed choices and retain the supporting reference on the device and in exports.

## Cocoa and confectionery

All eleven catalogue entries in category 05 now show every metal heading in the CTR 2.1.1 table. The finished-product identity, packaging condition and analytical basis appear beside the source row.

| Example | Product decision |

| Hard-boiled sugar confectionery | The four named entries are lead, copper, arsenic and tin. The worksheet flags overlapping tin descriptions for a canned product. |
| Soft candy and lozenges | Each has its own finished-product assessment. A hard-candy result cannot establish the applicable maximum for either identity. |

For 25 named food-use ingredients the worksheets also identify specific Gazette metal rows or competing entries. Pectin retains the separate liquid, solid and generic descriptions. Fish-derived gelatin and seaweed-derived gelling agents retain competing group entries where relevant. The tartaric acid standard and Gazette name the L(+) form. The catalogue's L(+/−) wording requires confirmation of the supplied stereoisomer.

## Source edition and interpretation

The Gazette dated 7 August 2020 replaced CTR 2.1.1. Its table gives ppm as mg/kg or mg/L and includes explicit per-litre entries for water. The original table was checked against Version IX and the subsequent amendment register. The May 2026 amendment takes effect on 1 December 2026. Its later provisions are excluded from the 20 November decisions.

For the 31 food-grade ingredient determinations, the worksheet links the specific standard and the Food Safety and Standards Act definitions in section 3(1)(j), (k) and (y). The melamine assessment concerns the ingredient itself when supplied for incorporation into food. A downstream infant formula has a separate identity and sample. Food-use chemical monographs and the specific tables in CTR 2.1.1 also require attention.

The published register contains the catalogue decisions. An export made inside the Helper also includes the user's recorded sample, form, pack and evidence. A statutory maximum, a testing frequency and approval of a commercial lot are separate decisions. No product has been marked fully verified across all applicable FSSAI provisions.
