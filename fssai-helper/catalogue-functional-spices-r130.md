# Functional spice ingredients and premixes

Reviewed 24 November 2026. FoSCoS category 99.7 includes individual listed functional ingredients and preparations containing functional ingredients. The product specification and classification must establish that the sampled article belongs to the selected catalogue entry. This worksheet covers three ground spice forms. It does not establish permission for a food use, claim, dosage or retail supplement classification.

| Specified powder | Ingredient list, Schedule VI Part B | Ground spice identity in FPSFA 2.9 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinnamon, ground inner bark of Cinnamomum verum | Item 44, PDF p. 113 | Cinnamon powder, PDF p. 8 |
| Clove, ground dried unopened flower buds | Item 49, PDF p. 113 | Clove powder, PDF p. 10 |
| Turmeric, ground dried Curcuma longa rhizome | Item 58, PDF p. 114 | Turmeric powder, PDF p. 27 |

| Sample and documented classification | Total aflatoxins (µg/kg) | Aflatoxin B1 (µg/kg) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Single ground spice sold as the functional ingredient | 30 | 15 |
| Finished compound food premix containing the ground spice, within the food-containing entry | 20 | 10 |
| Ground spice sampled individually on receipt for the premix | 30 | 15 |

Record the specification, plant part, process, sample lot and classification. For a compound premix, identify the ground spice in its finished formulation. An all-spice blend needs assessment under the spice mix entry. Oils, extracts, curcumin isolates, other ingredient forms and ambiguous product classifications remain unresolved in this worksheet. A measurement from a separately sampled spice cannot establish the premix result.

The individual product downloads and the full testing plan retain the sample role and applicable sources. The worksheet requires the reported lot to match the documented sample lot before a numerical comparison. Both the 24 November and 1 December 2026 plans retain the same maxima for these entries.
