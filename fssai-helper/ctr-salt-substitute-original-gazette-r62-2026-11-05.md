## Exact source clause established

One original R50 source-screened catalogue identity, `12-12-1-salt-substitutes`, has FoSCoS FPC **12.1.2** and FSSR clause **2.9.30(6)**. The source is not just a FoSCoS entry: **FSSAI's Fifth Amendment Gazette dated 19 June 2017** notified the clause, effective on publication. The original Gazette's **English printed page 5** (PDF page index 5, 0-based) and the official Chapter 2.9 Version 3 (7 May 2025) PDF pages **37–39** were visually inspected. <Source links below.>

## Substance/ratio classes must not be flattened

| 2.9.30(6)(1) group | Substance scope | Exact published condition |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | Certain potassium, calcium or ammonium salts of listed acids / potassium sulphate | GMP, but phosphorus **≤4% m/m** and ammonium ion **≤3% m/m** of mixture |
| **(b)** | Listed magnesium salts *mixed with Mg-free substitutes* | Magnesium **≤20% of the sum of K, Ca and ammonium cations**; phosphorus **≤4% m/m** |
| **(c)** | Listed choline salts in choline-free substitute mixtures | Choline **≤3% m/m** of mixture |
| **(d)** | Listed free adipic, glutamic, citric, lactic or malic acids | **GMP**, not a single numeric ppm limit |

**Critical basis:** the magnesium 20% criterion is **not 20% of the finished salt-substitute mixture's mass**. Some percentage limits belong to a particular chemical group; they must not be assigned indiscriminately to an unidentified formula.

## Distinguish FSSR 2.11.9 from 2.9.30(6)

The **2017 Gazette simultaneously introduced** a *separate* FSSR **2.11.9** for special dietary low-sodium/very-low-sodium **food**. The limits under that food standard address the final food **as normally consumed** and include a comparison with normal comparable food:

- Low sodium food: **≤120 mg sodium per 100 g of final food as normally consumed**.
- Very low sodium food: **≤40 mg sodium per 100 g of final food as normally consumed**.
- Both definitions require the specified sodium reduction relative to comparable normal food.

The numerical coincidence of **120 mg/100 g** is **not permission to substitute the analytical matrix**: 2.9.30(6) is the salt replacement mixture, whereas 2.11.9 is a finished dietary food. Neither automatically licenses a health claim or an exemption from other food-safety requirements.
