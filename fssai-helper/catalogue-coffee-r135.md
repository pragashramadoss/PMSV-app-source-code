# Coffee, chicory and coffee–chicory blends

The coffee worksheet groups the tests by the sample presented. Choose the product form before recording a result. The downloadable plan for all products shows every form. The product-specific download reflects a selected form, the recorded report and its comparison. The reference register excludes device entries.

| Product | Scope of review |
| --- | --- |
| Coffee | Green or raw beans, roasted or ground coffee, decaffeinated roasted and ground coffee, soluble coffee and decaffeinated soluble coffee |
| Coffee–chicory mixture | Regular and decaffeinated versions of both the roasted-ground and instant blends |
| Chicory | Roasted chicory powder, including the 2% aggregate allowance for added edible fat/oil or sugars covered by the formulation provision |

## Formulation and laboratory report

The minimum coffee content for each blend is 51%. Record the percentage by mass for ground blends and by mass on dry matter for instant blends. Caffeine also depends on the product form. For regular ground and instant blends, the dry-basis minima are 0.6% and 1.4%, respectively. For decaffeinated ground and instant blends, the dry-basis maxima are 0.1% and 0.3%. Instant blends also have two solubility checks. A formula document can support a recipe percentage. A laboratory result requires the sample, report, unit and analytical basis. [Product standards 2.10.2–2.10.4, PDF pp. 4–10](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_10_BEVERAGES_Other%20than%20Dairy%20and%20Fruits%20Vegetables%20based.pdf#page=7)

For water-soluble ash in decaffeinated roasted and ground coffee, the 2019 Gazette gives a 65% minimum and omits the denominator. FSSAI method 04A.006:2023 gives expressions on both a sample-weight and a total-ash basis. The worksheet flags this measurement for manual review. [2019 Gazette, PDF p. 6](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/5dc10699f2318Gazette_Notification_Standards_Coffee_Water_05_11_2019.pdf#page=6) · [FSSAI methods manual, method 04A.006:2023](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual%20of%20Methods%20of%20Analysis%20for%20Beverages%20Tea%2C%20Coffee%20and%20Chicory.pdf)

## Front of the pack

The August 2025 Gazette specifies declarations for ground and instant coffee–chicory packs with effect from 1 July 2026. FSSAI's direction of 21 July 2026 also permits a coffee-to-chicory percentage split or a clearly discernible graphic showing both percentages on the front. The direction applies pending final notification. The worksheet previews the split using the entered two-ingredient recipe. A sample or lot result and an artwork percentage are recorded independently. [Gazette, PDF pp. 3–4](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2025/08/689d8d163d422coffee%20chicory_gazette.pdf#page=3) · [FSSAI direction, PDF pp. 1–2](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/07/6a604e4037173Direction_Coffee%20Chicory.pdf#page=2)

## Raw coffee-bean sample

CTR 2.3.1, serial 129, specifies 0.1 mg/kg for monocrotophos in raw coffee beans. The coffee–chicory worksheet shows this under the incoming raw-bean lot. The manufactured blend remains on the list of products awaiting a first product-specific contaminant, toxin or residue source. For pure coffee, the marketed raw-bean comparison is available when the green or raw form is selected. [Pesticide Gazette, PDF p. 44](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=44)
