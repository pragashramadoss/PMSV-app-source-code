## Newly clarified official-source detail

**14.7 Sugandhita pushpa** has a footnote that names **Ketaki (Pandanus tectorius)** and **Jati (Jasminum officinale)** followed by **“etc.”**. These are **non-exhaustive examples** in the FSSAI source; they do **not** identify the flower actually used in a manufactured SKU, prove a specific plant is permitted for food use, or assign a pesticide/CTR limit.

The official 25 July 2025 source lists **14.6 Sugandhit Ksheera** as **Lidha (lickable), 13 ingredients**, and describes four separately collected milk reductions with different additions. **Karpura and Kasturi** appear in the method, but not the 13-row table; **Ketaki** is in the table but not independently assigned to a specific stage. The official **14.7 Ksheerapaka**, **Pana (drinkable), 4 ingredients**, has a single floral-water infusion and milk-heating method. Do not swap titles or combine ingredients to fix the apparent alignment inconsistency.

The FSSAI **19 June 2026 Part-2** publication starts its index at **23.1** and is not a verified correction to the prior 14.6/14.7 recipe entries. Other orders could exist and the notification search was not exhaustive.

Flowers removed after contact cannot be presumed to leave zero transferred compounds. Neither the method-only substances nor the broader floral examples have been independently approved for a real product.
