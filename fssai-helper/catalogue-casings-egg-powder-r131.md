# Natural animal casings and dried egg products

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Animal casings now have a conditional first source under CTR 2.3.2(4). Egg powder remains in the finished-product residue queue. The two dates in the helper retain these criteria.

## Natural animal casings

FPSFA 2.5.2(14) specifies dried or wet-salted casings made from the small or large intestine, oesophagus or urinary bladder. Twenty drug entries in CTR 2.3.2(4) name all edible animal tissues or all edible animal tissues except fish. The worksheet covers a documented non-fish natural casing. It requires the species reference, tissue, preservation, sample lot and the reference establishing the reporting basis for the preserved casing. A result from salt or brine, sausage filling, reconstituted collagen, or a synthetic casing requires a separate assessment.

Nineteen entries allow comparison at 0.01 mg/kg when the article and lot match. Sulfamethazine at serial 41 is visible for review. The helper withholds a numeric comparison until the relationship with the sulfadimidine entry at serial 39 and the sampled tissue has been resolved. The focused export excludes organ-specific limits for muscle, liver, kidney and fat.

## Egg powder

FPSFA 2.5.3(3) distinguishes dried white, yolk and whole egg. The testing plan contains separate pH minima of 3.9 for dried yolk and 4.2 for dried whole egg. The table gives no pH minimum for dried egg white. For dried whole egg protein, the English Gazette lists 45% on PDF page 38 and the Hindi Gazette lists 47.1% on PDF page 15. The English compendium also lists 45%. The plan shows both Gazette references with the operative limit blank pending clarification.

The residue plan lists the seven egg entries at serials 2, 7, 17, 28, 35, 46 and 47 of CTR 2.3.2(4) for a separately sampled incoming whole hen egg. No finished powder tolerance or conversion factor was established. The December 2026 changes to the seafood entries do not change these egg or casing rows.
