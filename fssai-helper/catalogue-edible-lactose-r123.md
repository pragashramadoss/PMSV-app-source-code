# R123 · Edible lactose and pesticide residues

**Reviewed 23 November 2026.** FSSAI names lactose among milk derivatives in its definition of a milk product. Its edible lactose standard specifies whey as the raw material and expressly refers back to that definition. This establishes a conditional link to the pesticide table's milk-product group for plain edible lactose. [Dairy Gazette, definition on PDF p. 75](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Milk_Products_24_10_2017.pdf#page=75) · [Edible lactose, PDF p. 134](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Milk_Products_24_10_2017.pdf#page=134)

## Determine whether the sample qualifies

Record the finished lactose lot, supplier specification and process from milk through whey to the finished crystals. The 2021 clarification limits the MRLs to agricultural commodities and physically processed foods, and excludes thermally and chemically processed foods. The helper leaves the comparison open if any step involved heat or chemical treatment, the process is unknown, or the sample was a different food. A heat-treated commercial lactose lot therefore needs an independent assessment. [FSSAI clarification, paragraph 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/01/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf)

Each of the six rows names the milk-product group. They are included in the 23 November and 1 December catalogue plans. The final 2026 CTR amendment addresses other entries. [Pesticide Gazette, p. 31](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=31) · [Pesticide Gazette, p. 32](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=32) · [May 2026 amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf)

In **Product finder**, open **Edible Lactose** and choose **Download these pesticide entries** for the six source rows. In the worksheet, select **Pesticide residues**, the marketed lactose sample and its documented process. A saved result retains the sample, process reference, laboratory report, analyte and individual comparison.
