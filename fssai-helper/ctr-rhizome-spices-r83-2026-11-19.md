# Turmeric and dried ginger in the Product Helper

Reviewed 19 November 2026.

## Choosing the raw material

The turmeric and dried ginger catalogue entries now let a reviewer record the form received from the supplier. The same controls appear beside Ardraka and Haridra in recipe 13.1 and beside Ardraka in recipe 14.6. Recipe 13.1 adds ginger paste to the ghee. Recipe 14.6 adds pieces of ginger to the first milk preparation. These preparation instructions leave the form received from the supplier open. Check the raw input if paste was made on site. A purchased paste needs assessment in its own form. [FSSAI recipe 13.1, PDF p. 88](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf#page=88) · [Recipe 14.6 ingredient table and method, pp. 96–97](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf#page=96)

Regulation 2.9.11 identifies dried whole ginger rhizomes and ginger powder from *Zingiber officinale* Roscoe. Regulation 2.9.18 describes whole *Curcuma longa* turmeric bulbs or fingers cured by boiling-water treatment and drying. Turmeric powder is ground from the dried material. The two turmeric tables also require a negative lead chromate test. Record its qualitative result separately from the measured lead concentration. [Dried ginger standard, pp. 17–18](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_9_Salt%20Spices%20Condiments%20and%20related%20products.pdf#page=17) · [2018 amendment to 2.9.11, p. 31](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/notice-comment/5bfd0937e84e3Gazette_Notification_DatePasta_27_11_2018.pdf#page=31) · [Turmeric standard, pp. 27–28](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_9_Salt%20Spices%20Condiments%20and%20related%20products.pdf#page=27)

Both arsenic entries leave speciation and a dry-matter basis unspecified. The five named turmeric metal rows determine the metal references displayed for this commodity. The two aflatoxin rows identify spices and spice mixes. Their scope and units are retained in both product briefs. ## Recipe coverage and export

The turmeric ingredient previously had one named lead reference. The four additional named metals and two aflatoxin rows add six links. Arsenic adds one link to each of the two ginger entries. This brings the recipe register from 104 to **112 ingredient-to-article links**, with **35 of 46** ingredients having at least one candidate reference. All 137 product-specific finished-food article decisions are open. No numerical results from a raw spice are transferred to the cooked recipe, finished milk, fresh roots, purchased paste, extracts or blends.
