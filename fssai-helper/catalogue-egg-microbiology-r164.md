# Egg microbiology and laboratory report checks

Reviewed 27 November 2026 against Appendix B Tables 8A and 8B and the Chapter 2.5 product identities. Both dated plans cover all five egg entries.

| Catalogue product | Process hygiene, Table 8A | Food safety, Table 8B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh table eggs | Row 1 gives no numerical criterion. Schedule 4 hygiene and GMP apply. | Row 1 gives no numerical criterion. |
| Liquid egg products | Apply row 2 to a documented pasteurized liquid sample of whole egg, yolk or white. | Salmonella and Listeria monocytogenes each require absence in 25 g in every unit. |
| Frozen egg products and egg powder | Row 3 applies to the frozen or dried product. | Every unit must meet the Salmonella absence criterion in 25 g. For Listeria monocytogenes the published plan is n = 5, c = 0, m = 100 cfu/g. |
| Pickled eggs | The product standard specifies hard-boiled eggs. Apply the cooked or ready-to-eat row 4. | Salmonella and Listeria monocytogenes each require absence in 25 g in every unit. |

For each of rows 2–4, Table 8A specifies five units and allows up to two above m provided none exceeds M. The aerobic plate count limits are m = 10,000 and M = 100,000 cfu/g. For Enterobacteriaceae they are m = 10 and M = 100 cfu/g. Five values at or below m are satisfactory. One or two above m and at or below M are acceptable. More than two above m or a value above M is unsatisfactory.

Table 8A applies at the end of manufacture and informs process-hygiene action. Table 8B addresses lot acceptability at the end of manufacture and during market shelf life. In the regulatory procedure, three sets from the same lot go to three accredited laboratories. Each laboratory must meet the food safety criterion. The worksheet interprets a single laboratory set for a single organism. It requires the lot identifier, report identifier and all five results. A report that uses a qualified value such as “<10” requires separate interpretation.

The individual worksheet, product brief and product microbiology CSV retain the selected result. Shared catalogue files contain the published criteria and conditions only. For a liquid product, enter the pasteurization record before using row 2. Changing the process or organism clears the affected results.

The contaminant review for finished egg powder remains open. Seven incoming whole-egg veterinary tolerance entries and the product composition requirements remain available separately. The applicable finished-powder chemical limit still requires a source that covers the dried matrix.
