# Thermally processed fruit salad, cocktail and mix

**R91 · Reviewed 20 November 2026 · Catalogue row 147 · FCS 04.1.2.4 · FSSAI 2.3.2**

## Which product qualifies

Section 2.3.2 covers a heat-processed mixture of fruits packed in a suitable liquid. The fruit may be fresh, frozen or canned. Named formats include cans, bottles, flexible packs and aseptically packed product.

The Fourteenth Amendment dated 19 November 2018 replaced the entire product standard. It was published on 20 November 2018 and specified compliance by 1 July 2019. The 2025 consolidated chapter carries the replacement text.

## Weighing and fill

| Provision | Enter from the same container | Minimum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.3.2(2)(B)(vi) | Drained fruit in grams and the measured mass of distilled water at 20 °C that fills the sealed container | 50% of that water mass |
| 2.3.2(2)(B)(v) | Occupied volume of fruit plus packing medium in mL and full distilled-water capacity at 20 °C in mL | 90% of that water volume |

The two measurements are independent. Each requires an actual measurement on its specified basis. Both provisions use the sealed container's full capacity, including for a covered flexible pack. This corrects the rigid-pack wording in the R66 summary. The 2011 text used net contents for the original liquid and solid pack rules. The 2018 replacement supplies the operative denominator for this product.

For 500 g of reference water, the required drained fruit is 250 g. A measured 240 g gives 48% and a shortfall of 10 g. Separately, 450 mL of fruit plus medium in a measured 500 mL container meets the 90% volume minimum.

## Packing medium and declaration

Sections 2.3.2(2)(A) and (3)(b) link the liquid composition to the declaration accompanying the product name. Fruit juice is from fruit used in the product. For water or a water-predominant mixture without added sugar, use the water declaration. A mixed medium with at least 50% juice by volume uses named juice(s) and water. For pure juice, name its fruit or fruits.

Where sugars have been added, classify the finished liquid medium by its cut-out strength. The Helper uses the reported precision. A value in a gap between the printed bands requires review of the report's precision.

| Cut-out °Brix | Sugar added to fruit juice alone | Sugar added to water, with or without fruit juice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10–13.9 | Extra light sweetened juice | Slightly sweetened water / extra light syrup |
| 14–17.9 | Lightly sweetened juice | Light syrup |
| 18–21.9 | Heavily sweetened juice | Heavy syrup |
| 22 and above | Extra heavy sweetened juice | Extra heavy syrup |

Sugared juice has a stated minimum of 10 °Brix. For sugared water, the first listed syrup band starts at 10 °Brix. The designation belongs in or close to the product name. Use the actual fruit names wherever called for.

## Canned tropical fruit salad

The three numerical defect limits in 2.3.2(2)(B)(iv) name canned tropical fruit salad. The peel result is an average and counts only peel from fruit prepared as peeled.

| Defect | Maximum |
| --- | --- |
| Blemished pieces | 2 pieces in 100 g drained fruit |
| Peel from fruit prepared as peeled | 6.5 cm² per 500 g total contents, averaged |
| Seed material plus extraneous vegetative matter | 2 g per 500 g total contents, combined |

Review characteristic colour, flavour and odour of the blend and the texture appropriate to the individual fruits under 2.3.2(2)(B)(i)–(iii). Each result in the lot brief addresses the stated criterion. The contaminants article decision for row 147 remains open.
