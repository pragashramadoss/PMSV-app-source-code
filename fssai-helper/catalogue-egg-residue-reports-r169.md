# R169 · Reading an egg residue report

Reviewed 28 November 2026. Open **Egg powder → Egg residues · incoming eggs and finished export powder**. Enter the sample lot, laboratory report, unit and reported readings for each sample. Record a numerical upper bound exactly as reported. The public CSV lists ten references. The individual CSV includes the two sets of sample details and comparisons.

## Incoming eggs

FSSAI CTR 2.3.2(2) prohibits the listed drugs during egg production and specifies extraneous maximum residue limits. The panel compares a separately sampled incoming hen egg with the following values. The prohibition on use applies independently of the reported concentration.

| Laboratory analyte or marker | Linked drug | EMRL in µg/kg |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Chloramphenicol | Chloramphenicol | 0.3 |
| Colistin | Colistin | 1 |
| AOZ | Furazolidone | 1 |
| SEM | Nitrofurazone | 1 |
| AMOZ | Furaltadone | 1 |
| AHD | Nitrofurantoin | 1 |

On an incoming egg report, **<0.5 µg/kg** is inconclusive for chloramphenicol. Its reporting bound exceeds the 0.3 µg/kg EMRL. The same bound is within the 1 µg/kg comparator for AOZ. A bare “ND” leaves the comparison open until the laboratory supplies a numerical reporting bound. The reviewed source provides no drying factor for applying these egg values to powder.

For colistin, the FSSAI egg-production EMRL is 1 µg/kg. The export order separately prints 300 µg/kg against finished egg powder. A powder result cannot establish compliance of the eggs used to make it, and the export table does not authorise use in production.

## HCH in finished egg powder for export

Clause (e)(3) of India’s export order lists separate limits for total HCH, alpha-HCH, beta-HCH and lindane. Record the all-isomer total reported by the lab and the three individual measurements from the same lot. If exact individual results add up to more than the reported total can contain, the worksheet flags the discrepancy for review with the laboratory. A missing or insufficiently sensitive result keeps its comparison open. The order also requires compliance with any stricter importing-country limits.

| Measurement | Export-order maximum in mg/kg |
| --- | ---: |
| HCH, all isomers combined | 0.03 |
| Alpha-HCH | 0.02 |
| Beta-HCH | 0.01 |
| Lindane (gamma-HCH) | 0.1 |

For example, a report with **0.05 mg/kg gamma-HCH and 0.06 mg/kg total HCH** is within the separate lindane maximum but exceeds the combined HCH maximum. The worksheet displays both findings. For the export order’s separate pyrethroid table the unit is unspecified. That table supplies no numerical comparison in this worksheet.
