# R74 — Water source and documentation review

Reviewed 17 November 2026. Covers one vending-water identity and four Ayurveda recipe-water instances. No numerical water limits are added. All 137 product-specific CTR decisions remain open; all 14 Ayurveda ingredient source gaps remain, including these four water instances.

## Verified source anchors

- Original 2019 FPSFA notification, PDF page 4: 2.10.9 incorporates IS 10500 for purified water offered or sold through vending machines. Packaged drinking water, natural mineral water and installations for captive consumption are excluded from that definition.
- BIS public metadata identifies IS 10500:2012, second revision, reaffirmed August 2023, and four amendments. The public detail page lists 2015, 2018, 2020 and 2021. This checks the source edition and amendment inventory, not their complete normative content or automatic applicability of any particular limit.
- June 2025 FPSFA proposed replacement, PDF pages 10–12: draft water tables. The official comments page still describes this document as a draft. This review has not established a final replacement or commencement; none of those proposed values are imported.
- FoSCoS licensing FAQ, updated 21 July 2022, PDF page 18, questions 121–123: ingredient-water documentation and testing guidance. These are dated administrative guidance, not an exhaustive current regulatory or KOB-specific review.

## Ingredient-water document aid

| Supply | Licensing evidence described in the FAQ | Continuing evidence described in the FAQ |
|---|---|---|
| Municipal Corporation / government body | Commercial-use water bills may replace the mandatory water report | Bill records support the stated testing concession |
| Mall, commercial hub, market or other private authority | Supplier water report, no more than one year old, meeting KOB document requirements | Obtain supplier reports at the frequency in FAQ 123; retain for inspection |
| Own or another supply | Check the applicable KOB water-report requirement; no municipal concession inferred | FAQ 123 calls for IS 10500 testing, excluding radioactive parameters, at least once each financial year and a minimum six-month gap between tests |

The private-authority route also retains FAQ 123's financial-year and minimum-gap wording. The aid does not calculate a due date or decide whether a report is current. The radioactive-parameter exclusion is kept within this ingredient-water FAQ context; it is not applied to vending or packaged-water standards. Municipal bills are documentation evidence, not analytical proof that water at the point of use is safe. Mixed or unknown supply requires clarification, with no concession inferred.
