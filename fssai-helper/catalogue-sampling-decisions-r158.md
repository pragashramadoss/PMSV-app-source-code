## Canned and alternatively packed foods

Item 51 specifies six sealed cans. The Helper now asks for the pack on canned tomatoes, chestnuts, chestnut purée and fishery products. It also offers the canned route for twelve fruit and vegetable products. These include thermally processed fruit, vegetables, soups, purées and juices. Selecting glass, pouch or other packaging leaves the quantity open for the actual article. For example, the canned-tomato standard expressly includes glass containers. ## Record a sampling decision

Open the product worksheet and expand **Regulatory sample quantity**. Choose the purpose and enter the actual article and lot. The worksheet gives the applicable number of parts or identifies the plan that determines it. For an unspecified article, confirm the packaging and table review, enter the quantity drawn and record the supporting reference. Through 31 March 2027 enter the Food Analyst consultation. From 1 April 2027 enter the applicable Food Authority guideline and clause. Use **Save worksheet on this device** to keep the entry. Changing the lot, purpose, form or assessment date clears the previous quantity decision. The product CSV carries the lot entry. The 533-product catalogue export stays independent of entries on a particular device. [Regulation 2.3.1(4)](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Laboratory%20and%20Sampling%20analysis%20(IV).pdf#page=4) · [2026 amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/notice-comment/Notification%20dated%2014.09.2026_amendment.pdf#page=3)

| Purpose | Number of parts or governing document |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary domestic enforcement | Four parts or four already sealed packages under rule 2.4.1(9)(i) |
| Microbiological analysis | Applicable Appendix B plan for the food and criterion |
| Surveillance | The Food Authority or State Food Authority surveillance plan |
| Import clearance | Two parts under regulation 9. Sealed bulk containers requiring aseptic or hygroscopic conditions have a specific representative-sample procedure. |
| Complaint investigation | Check the applicable case and document reasons and evidence for a reduced count. |
| Food safety emergency | Document reasons and supporting evidence in writing for any reduced count. |

## Cocoa beans sampled for quality

The cocoa bean defect worksheet and its export now include the Cacao of Excellence sampling protocol. For physical or sensory quality assessment it targets a 2 kg reference sample, from which a test sample of at least 500 g is taken. The worksheet keeps the commercial protocol separate from the quantity agreed for regulatory dispatch. [Cacao of Excellence quality guide, section 1](https://www.cacaoofexcellence.org/fileadmin/Websites/CocoaOfExcellence/Resources_items/Guides_for_the%20Assessment_of_Cacao_Quality_Flavour/14Feb2025_WEB_CoEx_Guide_Cacao_Quality_Flavour-ENGLISH-March_2024.pdf) · [ISO 2292 scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/68203.html)

Nine catalogue products still require a first specific Indian contaminant, toxin or residue source on 27 November. The 1 December legal edition adds purified vending water to that queue. Sampling references are counted separately.
