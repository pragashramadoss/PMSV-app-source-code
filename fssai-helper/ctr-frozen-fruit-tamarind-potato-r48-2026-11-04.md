| **2.3.37 Frozen Fruits/Fruit Products** | Block/IQF frozen; fruit sound/clean; wash; blanch **if required**; **−18°C at thermal centre after thermal stabilization**; Appendix B | No automatic peroxidase-negative rule copied from 2.3.38 vegetables; not canned/heat-processed fruit |
| **2.3.49 Seedless Tamarind** | Moisture **≤20%**, organic extraneous matter **≤5%**, total ash (dry) **≤6%**, acid-insoluble ash (dry) **≤1%**, crude fibre (dry) **≤9%**, seeds **≤0.5%** | Whole deseeded ripe *Tamarindus indica*, not pulp/puree/concentrate 2.3.18 |
| **2.3.60 Quick Frozen Fried Potatoes** | **−18°C** thermal centre; moisture **≤78%**, FFA in extracted oil as oleic acid **≤1.5%**, burnt units **≤0.5% mass**; frozen four-sided strip ≥4mm | Includes *Solanum tuberosum*, *S. andigena*, *Ipomoea batatas*; edible oil fry/precook **or water-blanch**; do not inherit raw potato limits |

For potato strip-width styles, shoestring **4–8mm**, medium **>8–12mm**, thick **>12–16mm**, extra-large **>16mm**. The printed 10% by-length tolerance where specific lengths are not indicated must be separately interpreted for actual product/label. Actual freezer-air temperatures cannot substitute for measured stabilized thermal-centre temperatures.

## Important controls
- **2.3.37** may contain milk solids: evaluate actual formula and milk-allergen artwork. Do not infer milk is present in every frozen fruit product.
- **2.3.49** total ash, acid-insoluble ash and crude fibre are expressed on a **dry basis**; do not substitute tamarind pulp/concentrate Brix/acidity, or total ash measured as-sold.
- **2.3.60** FFA is in **extracted oil, expressed as oleic acid**; this is not interchangeable with total oil content or ordinary product acidity.
- Each exact SKU needs correct FSSAI category, formula/species, approved test method and actual analytical results, competing article check, microbiology, additive/pack/label, and current original-Gazette contaminant provisions before a regulatory conclusion.
- **2025 edition flag:** Prior R46 and R47 audits used a 2024 compendium reference; audit references require cross-edition verification before production activation. This note does not assert their 2024 numbers changed.
