## Fermented Soybean Paste — 2.3.57

The source product is fermented soybean paste from soybean with **inactivated trypsin inhibitor**. The basic ingredient list includes soybean, salt, water and non-pathogenic, non-toxin-producing **Bacillus and/or Aspergillus** organisms. It permits specified optional grains/flour, yeast, lactic-acid organisms, agricultural ethyl alcohol, sugars/honey, starch syrup and permitted natural flavourings.

| FSSR selected test | Soybean only | Soybean with grains |
|---|---:|---:|
| Moisture maximum | **60.0%** | **60.0%** |
| Total nitrogen minimum, by mass | **1.60%** | **0.60%** |
| Amino nitrogen minimum, by mass | **0.30%** | **0.12%** |

The source also calls for **minimum container fill of 90% of water capacity**, allowing necessary GMP headspace. It does not prove the actual pack fill, microbial culture safety, soybean trypsin inhibitor destruction or finished food testing.

**PDF adjacency trap:** An earlier "no additives allowed" sentence applies to **Date Paste 2.3.56**, and another follows **Harissa 2.3.58**. Do not accidentally assign either sentence to fermented soybean paste. Actual additive permissions still require applicable separate law.

## Vegetable Protein Products — 2.3.59

This source standard concerns vegetable material excluding single-cell protein sources, from which non-protein components are reduced/removed to yield a protein product.

| FSSR selected parameter | Published source criterion |
|---|---|
| Moisture | **≤10%** |
| Crude protein, **N × 6.25**, on **dry weight basis** | **≥40%** |
| Total ash, dry-weight basis | **≤10%** |
| Crude fibre, dry-weight basis | **≤10%** |
| Residual fat | **Compatible with GMP**, not numeric zero |

Optional carbohydrate, edible fat/oil, other protein ingredients, vitamins/minerals, salt, herbs and spices are referenced in the source. The actual plant/protein species, extraction process, dry-mass conversion, allergen disclosures and full analyte/microbiology requirements are **not** confirmed for any real SKU.

## Distinct contaminant and safety requirements

- A soybean or cereal **raw commodity contaminant value** cannot automatically be assigned to a fermented paste or mixed protein-concentrate **finished food**.
- The soybean-only nitrogen values are **not** an alternative favourable standard for a grain-containing product. The actual recipe decides which column to assess.
- Vegetable Protein Products **N × 6.25** dry-weight crude protein is a different analytical parameter from **total or amino nitrogen** in fermented soybean paste.
- Species label alone does not prove the fermentation organisms are non-toxigenic; strain, safety, process and actual microbiological test evidence are required.
- Do not transfer fermented soybean curd / soy beverage standards or unrelated product-label data.
- No actual legal product CTR article, full metal/mycotoxin/pesticide/veterinary contaminant set, applicable Appendix B microbiology, additives, packaging and labelling has been adjudicated.
