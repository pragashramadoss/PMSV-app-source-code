## Arrowroot

For raw *Maranta arundinacea* rhizomes, the Indian root-and-tuber entries give a maximum of 0.1 mg/kg each for lead and cadmium. The cadmium entry excludes potato and celeriac. Codex lists this species as a tuberous vegetable under VR 0573. Record species, raw lot and mass basis. FSSR 2.4.14(1) defines the finished arrowroot as purified starch. A finished starch sample and the alternative Curcuma species need their own evidence. [FSSAI metal Gazette, lead p. 22 and cadmium p. 27](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=22) · [Codex classification, p. 82](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/fr/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXA%2B4-1989%252FCXA_004e.pdf#page=82) · [FSSAI arrowroot standard](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Food_Additives_Regulations.pdf#page=83)

## Lysozyme

For a separately supplied lysozyme preservative, the arsenic group maximum is 3 mg/kg on dry matter. Check the supplier specification and reporting basis. Appendix A lists use at GMP in ripened cheese under category 1.6.2. Appendix C, Table 5, lists a separate microbial-control use at GMP in alcoholic beverages and their low-alcohol or alcohol-free counterparts. Category 99.2 excludes enzymes already covered by the processing-aid or nutraceutical rules. [Indian arsenic table, p. 25](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=25) · [Appendix A](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Appendix%20A%284%29.pdf) · [Appendix C, pp. 6 and 75](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20C(2).pdf#page=6) · [FoSCoS categories, p. 9](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/Compiled%20FPC_FoSCoS.pdf#page=9)
