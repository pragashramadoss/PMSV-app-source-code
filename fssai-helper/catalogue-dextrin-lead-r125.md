# Lead in dietary fibre (dextrin) · R125

Reviewed 24 November 2026. FPSFA 2.11.8 defines soluble dietary fibre (dextrin) through the conversion of acidified starch followed by purification. The lead table in CTR 2.1.1(2) includes a group for starch-conversion products whose sulphated ash exceeds 1.0%. The helper links these provisions for the purified ingredient as supplied. [Dextrin definition, Gazette p. 5](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Plant_Sterol_HFD_25_07_2016.pdf#page=5) · [Lead table, Gazette p. 20](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=20) · [FSSAI contaminant compendium, p. 4](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf#page=4)

| Sample and evidence | Decision in the helper |
|---|---|
| Purified dextrin with documented sulphated ash above 1.0% | Compare the lead result for the same lot against 5.0 mg/kg. |
| Sulphated ash exactly 1.0% or lower | The specific entry does not apply. Review other lead provisions. |
| Total ash only, an unconfirmed analytical basis, or a missing lot report | Keep the lead threshold open. |
| Finished food or blend containing dextrin | Assess the finished food under its own identity. |

Record the ingredient specification, lot identifier, sulphated-ash percentage, method and report reference. For a lead comparison, record the lead result, unit, lot and laboratory reference, and confirm the basis. The bulk plan lists the conditional source. The individual worksheet can export the evidence for its lot.

Dried glucose syrup under FPSFA 2.8.7 has a separate sulphated-ash maximum of 1.0% by weight. A lot that meets that product standard cannot exceed the trigger above. A result above 1.0% requires a product-standard review. The applicable lead provision for dried glucose syrup remains open.
