# Tomato ketchup, tomato sauce and soybean sauce

Reviewed 20 November 2026. The product finder has one combined entry for tomato ketchup and tomato sauce (catalogue 424) and one entry for soybean sauce (429). Each uses its own FSSAI clause, amending notification and chapter page.

| Product | Clause | Minimum total soluble solids, salt-free % by mass | Minimum acidity, % as acetic acid | Minimum total nitrogen, % by mass |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Tomato ketchup and tomato sauce | 2.3.27(2) | 25.0 | 0.2 | — |
| Soybean sauce | 2.3.29(2) | 15.0 | 0.6 | 1.0 |

The dash means this composition table has no total-nitrogen criterion for tomato ketchup or sauce. Each result is compared independently on the laboratory reporting basis. Protein content, amino nitrogen and nitrogen expressed per unit volume cannot be entered as total nitrogen by mass. Acidity requires a result expressed as acetic acid. Raw refractometer readings need the correct salt-free reporting basis.

## Amendment history

The original 2011 tomato table gave a minimum acidity of 1.0%. The Fourteenth Amendment Regulations, notified on 19 November 2018, substituted 0.2%. The original tomato standard and the current consolidated clause both give 25.0% salt-free soluble solids.

The original soybean sauce table gave 25.0% salt-free soluble solids and 0.6% acidity. The Third Amendment Regulations, notified on 26 July 2021, replaced clause 2.3.29. The replacement set the solids minimum at 15.0%, retained 0.6% acidity and added 1.0% total nitrogen by mass. The definition describes fermented soybean paste and the inactivation of trypsin inhibitors.

Both standards specify a rigid-container fill of at least 90.0% of water capacity under their own paragraph (3). Use actual occupied product volume and the water capacity of the same container measured with distilled water at 20 °C. Both clauses also refer to permitted additives and Appendix B microbiological requirements.

## Use and export

Search for tomato ketchup, soy sauce, soya sauce or soybean sauce in the product finder, or choose the article under **Review progress → Sauce and paste standards**. Enter a lot reference and any available measurements. The brief includes the article, test basis, results, rigid-pack assessment and dated source links. Composition results alone do not establish product release.
