# R170 · Milk-logo artwork and coffee–chicory testing

## Milk-logo artwork

The artwork check covers 92 catalogue entries. FSSAI identifies eligible dairy products and exclusions in FAQ 14–15. The Helper maps 70 entries to eligible standardised products, 19 to exclusions, and three to a decision that depends on the finished SKU. The three are fat spread, fermented milk products based Indian sweets and other composite milk products based Indian sweets. For these entries, document the actual standard, recipe and classification. The product’s name or presence of milk powder alone cannot establish eligibility.

For example, standardised chocolate ice cream and colostrum are eligible. Milk chocolate, milk biscuits, confectionery containing dairy merely as an ingredient, and dairy analogues are outside the eligible categories. Proprietary status also matters. A milk-allergen declaration and the milk logo answer different questions.

[FSSAI dairy FAQ, pp. 6–7](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQs_Diary_12_07_2022.pdf#page=6) · [FSSAI dairy product definitions](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_1_Dairy_products_and_analogues.pdf#page=1)

### Dates and measurements

The Labelling and Display Second Amendment notification is dated 6 November 2026. Its graphic specifications come into force on **1 July 2028**. A separate December 2022 direction tied compliance with the underlying milk-logo provision to six months after FSSAI issued the specifications. The November and December 2026 plans show the measurements for artwork planning. The Helper retains the earlier direction as a separate reference for implementation of the underlying logo requirement.

| Actual principal display panel area | Minimum square side A | Minimum drop height B | Minimum drop width C |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Up to 100 cm² | 6 mm | 4.56 mm | 3 mm |
| 100 to 500 cm² | 8 mm | 6.08 mm | 4 mm |
| 500 to 2,500 cm² | 12 mm | 9.12 mm | 6 mm |
| Above 2,500 cm² | 16 mm | 12.16 mm | 8 mm |

The printed graphic is a white drop in a blue square. The blue is **C 89, M 58, Y 0, K 0**. The Gazette repeats 100 and 500 cm² in adjacent bands. At either exact area, the Helper shows both sets of dimensions. At exactly 500 cm², for example, it flags an 8 mm square for confirmation and checks a 12 mm square against both bands. The fourth band starts above 2,500 cm².

Open **Product finder → product worksheet → Milk-logo artwork check**. Enter the revision, assessment date, finished-product classification, verified PDP area and measurements at actual print scale. Record both square sides and the drop height and width. Use the CMYK values from the print proof. A blank measurement stays pending. Check the drop design against the Gazette drawing. The individual download includes the observations. The public scope file contains no private measurements.

## Ochratoxin in coffee–chicory blends

FSSAI’s 2016 laboratory parameter order lists ochratoxin for the regular ground blend and for the regular instant blend. These are finished-sample references for articles 2.10.4.1 and 2.10.4.2. The tables give neither a numerical maximum nor a specific ochratoxin designation. The Helper therefore includes the references with the numerical limit blank. The CTR schedule separately specifies ochratoxin A for coffee. A marketed blend needs its own basis for a numerical compliance decision.

[FSSAI laboratory order, ground blend p. 588](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2018/08/5b7fa9ef0a9e3Order_Lab_Parameters_18_04-2016.pdf#page=588) · [Instant blend p. 590](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2018/08/5b7fa9ef0a9e3Order_Lab_Parameters_18_04-2016.pdf#page=590)

Both plans cover 533 products. Nine await their first product-specific domestic CTR source on 28 November 2026, and ten await one on 1 December 2026.
