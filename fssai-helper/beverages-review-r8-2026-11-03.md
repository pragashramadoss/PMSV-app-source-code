## Main corrections and controls

1. Kangra tea alkalinity is a true 1.0–2.2% range, expressed as K2O on dry basis. The old >= operator could ignore the separate maximum. The unusual printed aqueous-extract wording is retained and blocked, not silently corrected.
2. Restore the omitted decaffeinated roasted/ground coffee alkalinity requirement and the missing solubility requirements for decaffeinated soluble coffee and instant coffee–chicory variants. Keep mass versus dry-mass formulation bases distinct.
3. Restore omitted mineral-water chemical/radioactivity requirements and packaged-drinking-water MBAS/radioactivity requirements. Retain natural-spring TDS scope and the limited calcium/magnesium minimum exception for referenced ingredient-water use, not retail PDW.
4. Distinguish finished beverage, ester-gum ingredient, packaging migration test and daily-intake matrices. A method detection capability is not a permitted contaminant concentration.
5. Model all 21 direct microbial criteria without inventing Appendix B n/c/m/M plans. Retain actual test volumes, incubation conditions and individual named-organism results. An enumeration of zero is not substituted for an absence result. Missing mineral-water portions and the printed A x 250 ml total-coliform row remain unresolved.
6. Total caffeine is 145–300 mg/L for the specific caffeinated-beverage category. Optional ingredient maxima expressed in mg/day are not mg/L limits; the ordinary carbonated-water caffeine ceiling is not inherited by this category.
7. Recheck the water testing scheme: five satisfactory consecutive batches after microbiological failure is a different provision from the three-batch rule for increased monitoring after a six-monthly-test failure. No comparison authorizes restart or release.
