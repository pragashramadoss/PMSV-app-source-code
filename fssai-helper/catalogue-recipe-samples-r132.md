# Ayurveda recipes and incoming ingredient samples

The review covers four catalogue entries on 24 November 2026. It distinguishes the food as sold from the material sampled on receipt. Each comparison needs a lot identifier, a supporting specification or recipe, and a laboratory report for that same lot.

| Product | Sample eligible for comparison | Analyte and maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Narikela Ksheerika, recipe 14.3 | Finished recipe with documented retained coconut kernel | Total aflatoxins 20 µg/kg and aflatoxin B1 10 µg/kg |
| Narikela Ksheerika | Incoming coconut kernel for processing | Total aflatoxins 15 µg/kg and aflatoxin B1 10 µg/kg |
| Narikela Ksheerika | Incoming liquid cow milk | Aflatoxin M1 0.5 µg/kg |
| Ksheerapaka, recipe 14.7 | Authorised variant sold as a non-alcoholic beverage, through 30 November 2026 | Saffrole 10 ppm |
| Ksheerapaka | Incoming liquid buffalo milk | Aflatoxin M1 0.5 µg/kg |
| Coconut milk powder | Incoming coconut kernel for processing | Total aflatoxins 15 µg/kg and aflatoxin B1 10 µg/kg |
| Water chestnut flour (Singhare ka Atta) | Incoming dried Trapa nuts before milling | Total aflatoxins 15 µg/kg and aflatoxin B1 10 µg/kg |

The FSSAI recipe for Narikela Ksheerika cooks chopped or grated coconut kernel in milk to make set pieces. The reviewer records the retained ingredient and the classification of the finished food before applying the composite-food entry. The same limits appear in the applicable Schedule D aflatoxin rows. Ksheerapaka needs a documented variant because the name also covers other preparations. From 1 December 2026 the saffrole entry covers beverages and foods containing mace or nutmeg kernel. The published recipe lists buffalo milk, infused water and sugar. The December plan leaves the numeric field blank for this recipe. A reformulation with either spice requires a fresh recipe and classification review. [Recipe 14.7, PDF p. 98](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf#page=98) · [Earlier saffrole table, PDF p. 4](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/Gazette_Notification_Crop_Contaminants_Natural_Toxic.pdf#page=4) · [Amendment, PDF p. 5](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=5)

The coconut milk powder standard describes drying extracted coconut milk. The water chestnut flour standard specifies milling the dried nuts of Trapa bispinosa or Trapa quadrispinosa. Both finished products remain in the source review queue.
