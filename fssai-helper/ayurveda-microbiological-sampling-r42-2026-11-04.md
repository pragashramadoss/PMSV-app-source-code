# PMSV R42 — Do not confuse the n=5 acceptance plan with collected regulatory units

**4 Nov 2026 | Source-only, regulator-vs-FBO sampling review, no product approvals.**

## Evidence-grounded regulatory sampling direction

The original **2022 Ayurveda Aahara Gazette Schedule D Table 1B** uses **n=5, c=0, Salmonella and Listeria monocytogenes absent in 25 g** for direct consumption and fermented classes. The **2023 FSSAI official microbiological sampling guideline Table 1** confirms *physical collections* used for regulator sampling.

| Actual class (must be verified) | Bulk product units | Packaged product units | Lab distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unprocessed and not direct consumption | 5 units, each 500–600 g | 15 units, each 150–200 g | Three accredited labs (5 per lab) |
| Direct consumption | 5 units, each 500–600 g | 15 units, each 150–200 g | Three accredited labs (5 per lab) |
| Fermented | 5 units, each 500–600 g | 15 units, each 150–200 g | Three accredited labs (5 per lab) |

For bulk collection, FSSAI directs sending **150–200 g of each of the 5 original sampled units to each of three accredited laboratories**. For packaged product, collect **15 separate sample units**, sending a set of 5 to each accredited lab.

**Important:** The 2023 document is sampling **guidance** elaborating the Gazette, not a new amendment and not proof that every food-business internal QC test must be split among 3 labs.

## Regulator and manufacturer are different pathways

- **Regulatory food safety decisions:** original Gazette instructs three independently accredited labs; all three labs must meet the criteria. **No retesting/resampling** provision for microbiological regulatory testing. A single-lab result is not an independent full regulatory compliance determination.
- **FBO routine in-house verification:** FBO chooses necessary test frequencies subject to 2011 licensing minimum requirements. Alternative analytical methods are allowed for in-house tests but **not automatically valid for regulatory compliance**. The regulator's collection protocol must not be presented as a universal mandatory three-lab protocol for routine plant internal testing.
- **Sampling modifications:** for high-value small packs **below 100 g** and large packs **above 1 kg**, changes to prescribed plans are **case-by-case with prior FSSAI approval**. Do not claim automatic absence-in-5-g/10-g, a smaller n, or exemption.
- **Stage and interpretation:** Process hygiene criteria are production process indicators, not market release specifications. Food safety criteria define batch/lot acceptability, tested at manufacture and during market shelf-life, with validated official method and appropriate sample handling.
- **Recipe classification:** All nine Category A recipe identities are source referenced, but whether an actual SKU is direct-consumption, unprocessed or fermented **has not been decided**. Two original recipe title/method discrepancies (14.6 and 14.7) remain on hold.
