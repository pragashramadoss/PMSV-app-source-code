# Melamine laboratory reports for all 533 products

Reviewed on 26 November 2026. Both the 26 November and 1 December testing plans now include guidance on selecting the laboratory method and reporting limit for melamine. Open a product worksheet and expand **Melamine, mercury and methyl mercury** to enter a certificate.

| Food as supplied | Maximum melamine (mg/kg) |
| --- | ---: |
| Powdered infant formula | 1.0 |
| Liquid infant formula | 0.15 |
| Other foods | 2.5 |

The maximum comes from the [original Gazette, page 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Melamine_Milk.pdf#page=3). The [corrigendum](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Corrigenda_Amendment_Melamine_09_05_2016.pdf) gives the provision its current number, CTR 2.5.1. Select the supplied article and record its identifying evidence when the catalogue description leaves a choice.

## Reading a certificate

Enter the lot, certificate reference, analytical method and the reported melamine result with its qualifier and unit. Confirm that the sample, analyte and as-supplied basis agree. A combined melamine and cyanuric acid value cannot stand in for a melamine result. The comparison accepts mg/kg and µg/kg. For a certificate in mg/L or µg/L, obtain a mass-basis result or enter a documented mass-basis conversion with its reference. The Helper does not assume a density.

For liquid infant formula, a result below 0.10 mg/kg establishes an upper bound within the 0.15 mg/kg maximum. A result below 0.25 mg/kg leaves open the possibility that the maximum was exceeded. An unqualified ND has no numerical upper bound. A measured 150 µg/kg equals the maximum. These comparisons describe the reported number. Use the laboratory decision rule and measurement uncertainty to make a conformity decision.

## Method selection

FSSAI method 01.125:2022 covers milk, milk products and infant formula. ISO 23970:2021 reports interlaboratory validation on milk-based and soy-based infant formula, milk powder, whole milk, soy drink and milk chocolate. It gives 0.05 mg/kg as the melamine limit of quantification. Verify the method scope and the actual reporting limit for the sample. Cocoa beans, press cake, cocoa mass and other chocolate types need their own matrix evidence. [FSSAI dairy methods, printed page 328](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual-Dairy-Revision1.pdf#page=333) · [ISO method scope and limits](https://www.iso.org/standard/77481.html)

The individual report CSV and individual testing plan include the entered evidence. The all-product melamine checklist and full testing plans contain the catalogue criteria and blank sample fields.
