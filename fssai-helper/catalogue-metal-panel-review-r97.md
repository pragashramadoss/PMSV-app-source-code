# R97 · Metal limits for the 533-product register

**Regulatory position reviewed on 20 November 2026.** Each catalogue product now has a worksheet covering the eleven headings in regulation 2.1.1(2) of the Food Safety and Standards (Contaminants, Toxins and Residues) Regulations, 2011. The lookup preserves all 382 entries in the original substituted table, their published wording, limits and PDF page references. The existing crop-contaminant and broad-contaminant worksheets remain available. ## Using a product worksheet

Select a product and open its metals worksheet. Each heading gives the source article, limit or exclusion and the reason for the decision. Where a choice depends on the product, record the marketed article or packaging and a reference to the supporting specification. The source lookup searches the published article wording. The CSV export includes the article decisions, supporting references, any documented comparison and the December changes.

For a lab comparison, select a metal, enter the result and units, confirm the analytical basis, and record the report reference. The Helper uses the unrounded decimal entered. It retains minimum and maximum bounds where the source prescribes both. A missing reference, incompatible unit or unresolved matrix keeps the comparison open. A result for a finished confectionery product and a result for one of its ingredients each need their own applicable article.

- **Cocoa powder.** The lead maximum of 5.0 ppm is on a dry fat-free basis. The copper maximum of 70 ppm is on a fat-free basis. A whole-powder result alone cannot settle either comparison. [Lead, PDF p. 20](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=20) · [Copper, PDF p. 23](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=23)
- **Wheat protein products.** Standard 2.4.22 covers vital gluten, devitalized gluten and proteins solubilised by partial hydrolysis. The supplier's grade and process determine whether the hydrolysed-protein lead entry applies. [Wheat protein standard, PDF pp. 65–66](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4_Cereals_and_Cereal_products.pdf#page=65) · [Lead table, PDF pp. 19–20](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=19)
- **Formulated supplements for children.** The product standard covers children older than 24 months and up to 36 months. Its reference to follow-up formula concerns microbiology. The general CTR rules govern the identified finished supplement. For tin, record the form sold and its packaging. [Supplement standard, English text from PDF p. 12](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2018/11/5bfd0a04c4d4bGazette_Notification_Children_Supplement_26_11_2018.pdf#page=12) · [Infant nutrition definitions](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_IFR_VERSION-II_04_01_2024.pdf#page=1)
- **From 1 December 2026.** Plain yellow pea powder, besan and chana sattu have dated lead and cadmium entries for pulse flours. For mixed foods, the marketed article and the pulse species still matter. The amended fish-oil inorganic arsenic limit and total-arsenic screening instructions are also shown separately. The November worksheet uses the edition effective on its review date. [2026 amendment and commencement, PDF pp. 4–5](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=4)

The downloadable register has 533 unique product entries and 89 columns. The first 72 retain the R96 layout. Seventeen columns add the full metal worksheet, sources, dated changes, evidence and lab comparison.
