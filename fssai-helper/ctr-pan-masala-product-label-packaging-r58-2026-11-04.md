## FSSR identity: 2.11.5 is not the generic mouth-freshener route

FSSAI Chapter 2.11, official PDF **page 3 (zero-based 2), visually inspected**, names Pan Masala and lists ingredients that *may* be used, including betelnut, lime, coconut, catechu, spices, natural permitted colours, menthol and non-prohibited flavours. It prohibits added coal-tar colouring matter and other ingredients injurious to health.

| Named Pan Masala characteristic | Printed maximum |
|---|---:|
| Total ash | **8%**, dry basis |
| Ash insoluble in dilute hydrochloric acid | **0.5%**, dry basis |

Actual SKU ingredients, manufacturer product identity, ash methods and representative lot results were **not provided**. These FSSR product-standard references must **not** automatically be transferred to R55's separate generic **5.2.4.1/.2/.3 mouth-freshener categories**.

## CTR version IX aflatoxins: RAW betelnut vs FINISHED mixed food

The official FSSAI CTR Version IX (3 Feb 2026), **PDF page 14 (zero-based 13), visually inspected**, prints distinct articles:

| Analyte | Printed article | Source maximum |
|---|---|---:|
| Total aflatoxins | **Arecanut or Betelnut** | **15 µg/kg** |
| Aflatoxin B1 | **Arecanut or Betelnut** | **10 µg/kg** |
| Total aflatoxins | **Food product containing any of the above mentioned food articles** | **20 µg/kg** |
| Aflatoxin B1 | **Food product containing any of the above mentioned food articles** | **10 µg/kg** |

The food-products-containing clause is *not* proof a specific unidentified finished Pan Masala SKU lawfully belongs to that row. Select applicable raw ingredient and finished-product articles after obtaining actual formula, species, sample matrix and present law. Do not compare against whichever limit is easier or apply the arecanut value automatically to a mixed finished pack. These four cited entries do **not** establish a complete contaminant or pesticide panel.

## Existing retail warning and planned future ad rule

FSSAI Labelling and Display **Version VIII (09.09.2025), Schedule II, paragraph 1(3), PDF page 30 (zero-based 29), visually inspected**, prints:

**CHEWING OF PAN MASALA IS INJURIOUS TO HEALTH**

The printed note specifies the warning statement covers **50% of the front-of-pack label**. Earlier FSSAI extensions in 2023 allowed short compliance deferrals; those are not a perpetual 2026 exemption. The actual SKU and artwork, package display area and latest operative directions must still be checked.

**Effective-date safeguard:** A separate **24 March 2026** notified first amendment to Labelling & Display expressly **commences 1 July 2027**. Its new note requires the warning statement to be *clearly legible or audible* in advertisements. The original Gazette English commencement page **5 (zero-based 4) was visually checked**, and later amendment text was inspected, but the later text page image could not be fetched. **Do not activate this new 2027 amendment as a November 2026 rule.** Existing advertising-warning provisions remain separately subject to current law.

## Pan Masala packaging: August 2026 amendment found, original Gazette image outstanding

FSSAI's **Packaging Amendments** page lists **Amendment 4 dated 7 August 2026**. Its notification listing gives **10 August 2026** as publication. The Gazette reproduction **CG-DL-E-10082026-275349** says the amendment commences upon publication and adds **Pan Masala** as item 11 in **Schedule IV, suggestive packaging materials**.

The reproduced text names paper/paperboard/cellulose or other naturally derived material **free of plastics, synthetic polymer, aluminium foil and metallised layers**, plus **tin or glass containers**. It also cross-references **Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016, Rule 4(1)(f) and 4(1)(i)**. **Separate legally binding plastic restrictions must not be confused with Schedule IV's 'suggestive' heading.** Do not infer that any unlisted material is necessarily lawful or unlawful without reviewing the official Gazette, Plastic Waste Rules and actual multilayer pack.

**Source limitation:** FSSAI official indexed final amendment is verified, and text is available in a Gazette reproduction, but the **actual FSSAI-hosted final 2026 packaging PDF was not fetched and independently page-image checked**. The packaging register therefore **does not grant packaging PASS or auto-approve a material specification**.

## Practical evidence gate before any release or legal PASS

The actual manufacturer's licence and Pan Masala formula (including arecanut, spice, dry nut, tobacco/nicotine and additives), dry-basis ash and acid-insoluble ash lab evidence, effective Gazette/amendment panel, valid aflatoxin raw/finished sampling basis, batch and sample reports, label FOP layout, primary packaging BOM/layer map and suppliers' material declarations are needed, followed by qualified legal/QA review.
