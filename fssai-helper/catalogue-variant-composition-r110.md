# R110 · Cocoa and gum composition limits

Open a product worksheet, select the declared variant, and choose a test parameter. Enter the laboratory result as a percentage and confirm that its reporting basis matches the row. The Helper compares that one result with the selected limit. Use the sample reference and notes fields to identify the laboratory report before saving. Changing the variant or sweetener selection clears the active measurement.

## Reading the comparison

- For gum, the recipe determines whether sucrose and reducing sugars apply. When only artificial sweetener is used, the proviso excludes those two parameters. When the sweeteners are unknown, the Helper keeps those two comparisons pending. The other four limits can be checked in either case. The original 2011 text also prints this proviso on [PDF p. 94](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Food_Additives_Regulations.pdf#page=94).
- For cocoa powder, 10% and 20% dry-basis cocoa butter are shared endpoints in the printed table. Use the declared grade.
- For cocoa mass and cake, the shell-only limit uses an alkali-free basis. The combined shell-and-germ limit uses fat-free dry matter. Only cocoa mass has a cocoa-butter range under this article.
- Four appended register columns carry the variant limits, linked pages, selected variant and measured result. The downloadable reference register has empty measurement fields. The in-app export includes the current worksheet entries.
- The scope is the numerical finished-product composition table in each cited article. Ingredients, contaminants, microbiology, sampling frequency and lot release have separate evidence requirements.
