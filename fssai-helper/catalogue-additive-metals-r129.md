# R129 · Metals in identified additive lots

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Two FoSCoS 99.1 catalogue entries now have a conditional first source under CTR 2.1.1(2). The register covers 508 of 533 products at this level. Twenty-five still await a first source. The qualifications below apply to the supplied additive lot.

| Catalogue entry | Verified additive identity | Published metal entry |
|---|---|---|
| Appendix A additives, subject to the catalogue exclusions | INS 475, polyglycerol esters of fatty acids, or INS 476, polyglycerol esters of interesterified ricinoleic acid | For each named additive: lead 2, copper 25 and arsenic 3 mg/kg by mass |
| Same entry | Either named additive documented as an emulsifying or stabilising agent | Arsenic 3 mg/kg on dry matter |
| Additives VA–VF, subject to the catalogue exclusions | INS 1401 acid treated starch, 1417 acetylated distarch phosphate, 1422 acetylated distarch adipate or 1451 acetylated oxidized starch | Arsenic 3 mg/kg on dry matter when the documented material serves as an emulsifying or stabilising agent |

The original metal table prints ppm for the named entries. On a mass basis, 1 ppm equals 1 mg/kg. The table also prints a separate dry-matter arsenic entry for the functional group. The specific and group entries can give different comparisons for a material with moisture. Use the basis reported by the laboratory. Appendix A identifies INS 475 and 476. The historical Schedule VE supplies the INS numbers, chemical names and functions of the four modified starches. For each actual material, record the applicable current permission, food category, format and classification, including the exclusion for separately standardised additives. Food-use permissions and finished-food use levels require their own assessment. [FoSCoS classification](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product) · [Appendix A, pp. 173–174](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A%284%29.pdf#page=174) · [Schedule VE, Gazette p. 116](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Nutraceuticals_Regulations.pdf#page=116) · [FSSAI direction, p. 7](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Direction_New_compressed.pdf#page=7)

In the metal worksheet, select the supplied INS, enter the specification, current classification or permission reference and sampled lot. For arsenic, also select the documented analytical basis. In the laboratory panel, use the same lot, a mass-based unit, the report reference and a confirmed basis. A blend, downstream food, mismatched lot or unresolved basis keeps the comparison pending. Numerical upper bounds remain inconclusive when they exceed the applicable maximum. The 1 December 2026 metal amendments do not change the entries used here.
