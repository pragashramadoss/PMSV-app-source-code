# R108 · Printed dates on the lot

Open **Product finder → Product worksheet → Packaging, labelling and claims → Printed dates on the lot**. Enter the SKU, artwork, pack and batch code. Transcribe the start and end dates separately, including their wording. Identify the sample and the approved shelf-life instruction. **Save lot date check** keeps the record under that batch. **Start another lot** retains the artwork and specification and clears the previous batch’s dates and evidence.

| Printed marking | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| PKD 01/04/2026 and Use by 31/03/2026 | Use-by precedes the packaging date. Check the coded lot against the packing record. |
| MFD 29/02/2027 | February 2027 has 28 days. The calendar check flags the print. |
| PKD APR 2026 and EXP APR 2026 | Both markings refer to the same month. The individual days and their order remain undetermined. |
| PKD 21/Nov/26 | The FSSAI FAQ illustrates a three-letter month with a two-digit year. Enter the documented full year to compare dates across lots or centuries. |
| EXP JUN 2027 for an approved shelf life of exactly three months | Day-level marking is required under the general rule. A month-year marking is available for a shelf life over three months. |

Select the shelf-life band from the approved product specification. A product with a shelf life over three months can use a full date too. A non-wine alcoholic beverage below 10% ABV has a separate expiry or use-by day, month and year requirement. When another product-specific provision applies, the worksheet leaves the format decision open.

The chronology check compares calendar days or the full range represented by a printed month. It flags an end marking that falls entirely before the start marking. A different separator or presentation prompts review of the original pack. To check whether expiry may be omitted or moved to an outer, use **Small-pack declarations and date marking**. A numerical shelf-life calculation or stock release requires the applicable batch instruction.

Saved readings appear in the product brief and the full register. **Export printed lot dates as CSV** gives one row for each saved batch and artwork. The blank register includes sources and leaves the lot evidence empty.
