# PMSV R57 — Formulated supplements for children: recover the missing FSSR source clause

**4 November 2026 | Research/source evidence only — no finished-product legal PASS.**

## Substantive correction

The historic R29/R51 entry `formulated-supplements-children` has a blank FSSR field and FoSCoS FPC **13.2**. The official **2018 Food Products Standards and Food Additives Eleventh Amendment** explicitly inserts **2.4.11 clause 4: Formulated supplements for children**, for children **older than 24 months through 36 months**. The **FSSAI Chapter 2.4 Version 3 (04.11.2024)** retains this exact standard.

This review adds a **source-only named FSSR clause 2.4.11(4)** to a separate evidence register. It does not fill the original source field, prove actual manufacturer-specific product applicability or close a CTR legal article.

## Important effective-law reconciliation

| Requirement | Original 2018 / later evidence | Source interpretation guard |
|---|---|---|
| Age/product identity | Original Eleventh Amendment, **2.4.11(4)** | This is an older-child nutritional supplement standard for **above 24 months to 36 months**, not a general children's-health product or automatic infant-formula standard |
| Protein quality | **11 Jan 2023 First Amendment**, effective **1 Aug 2023**, original Gazette English p.17; 2024 Chapter 2.4 p.43 | Earlier **70% PDCAAS and PER alternatives are superseded**. The amended source refers to **90% PDCAAS** and a protein:energy ratio phrased **“not more than 5 – 7.5 per cent”**. Preserve this wording and require expert clause interpretation; no automatic pass criterion from an ambiguous interval |
| Nutrient declaration variation | 2018 original ±10%; 2024 Chapter 2.4 p.50, amended **2021** rule | Earlier ±10% is **no longer correct**. The source now permits **minus 10%** against declared nutrients, **subject also to composition-table maximums**. A symmetrical ±10% release check is prohibited |
| Selected source analytical identity fields | 2024 Chapter 2.4 p.43 | Energy **≥4 kcal/g dry basis**; moisture **≤8% by weight**, fat **≥7.50% by weight**, ash **≤7.50% by weight**. References only: no actual test results, method or SKU matching obtained |
| Microbiology | **2.4.11(4)(4)(vi)** | The source refers to **Follow-up formula** microbiology in Appendix B. Confirm current exact requirements, age-appropriate matrix and sampling before applying them |
| Contaminants, pesticides, additives, labels | **2.4.11(4)(5),(7),(9)** | Check full current CTR/Appendix/Packaging/Labelling original notifications and actual formula: a food-category label does not set every analyte or label requirement |
| Proprietary classification | Official FoSCoS FAQ Q82 (July 2022, visually checked p.11), citing **18 Oct 2019** FSSAI order | **Proprietary-food licence not granted** for formulated supplements for **24–36 months** under 13.2. Original 2019 order archive link currently fails; do not claim original PDF independently examined |

**Age-scope caution:** The separate Foods for Infant Nutrition 2020 compendium defines several complementary products for six to 24 months. It must not be silently substituted for this 24–36 month FSSR route.

## Actual product evidence still required

Exact SKU formulation, nutritional ingredient percentages and species, intended marketed age, manufacturer licence/KoB, appropriate protein digestibility and nutrient-method evidence, label/protein source/allergen/preparation instructions, lawful food additives and carry-over, contaminant/metals/mycotoxins/pesticide/veterinary residue article selection, corresponding current microbiology and actual representative laboratory lot results. These are not present in R29/R50/R51.
