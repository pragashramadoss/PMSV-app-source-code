# R102 · Ingredient percentages on artwork

Reviewed on 21 November 2026. Every catalogue worksheet includes **Packaging, labelling and claims → Ingredient percentage on artwork**. Choose the actual SKU, recipe version and pack artwork. A single catalogue food can have several formulations, pack levels and highlighted ingredients.

## Reading regulation 5(2)(g)

For a food sold as a mixture or combination, assess each highlighted ingredient, compound ingredient or ingredient category. One trigger is an indication through words, pictures or graphics that emphasizes its presence. A second covers an ingredient outside the food name when it characterises the food, consumers expect it, and omitting its percentage would mislead. The reference amount is its share going into manufacture on the appropriate weight or volume basis. [Original Gazette, p. 31](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31)

| Proviso | What to check on the actual artwork and recipe |
| --- | --- |
| (i) Flavouring function | Establish that the ingredient under review is used as a flavouring agent. Check the declaration required for the added flavour. If mango and a separate flavour are both present, assess the mango itself. |
| (ii) Reference in the name | Document why differences in the amount do not distinguish the food and why the reference gives consumers an accurate impression. Check other wording and images independently. |
| (iii) Drained net weight | Locate the required drained weight. A highlighted ingredient in a mixed product can still trigger a percentage. |
| (iv) Food-specific provision | Record the specific labelling provision and use its declaration basis. |
| (v) Serving suggestion | Check that the image genuinely illustrates how to serve the food. Record the rationale and review any independent ingredient claim. |
| (vi) Added micronutrients and their preparations | Identify the applicable nutrition declaration under regulation 5(3). |

The first five provisos appear on Gazette page 31. The sixth continues on page 32. The general presentation and added-flavour rules are also relevant to a label that implies fruit content. [Gazette p. 32](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=32) · [Regulation 4(3), p. 29](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=29) · [Regulation 5(5), p. 35](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=35)

## Comparing the recipe with the artwork

A fictional 100 kg candy batch uses 1.2 kg of mango pulp directly and 3 kg of a preparation containing 40% mango pulp by weight. The preparation contributes another 1.2 kg. The resulting ingoing percentage is **2.4% mango pulp**. A reference to “mango” requires the reviewer to check exactly which form of the fruit the recipe and claim support. For a flavour-only recipe, review the flavour description and any suggestion that actual fruit is present.

Use the complete ingoing batch as the denominator. Add a separate row for each contribution and use the supplier’s composition for the named share of a preparation. The helper converts g to kg and mL to L within their respective dimensions. A density conversion, a fresh-fruit-equivalent claim, or a different product-specific basis requires separate evidence. The compound-ingredient listing proviso below 5% in 5(2)(e) concerns listing subingredients. A highlighted constituent still needs its own assessment under 5(2)(g). [Gazette p. 31](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31)

The comparison uses exact decimal inputs before display rounding. A printed value that agrees only after rounding is flagged for a precision decision. The worksheet supplies no statutory percentage tolerance. An unresolved recipe or supplier share leaves the numeric outcome open.

## Inner packs, outer packs and saved checks

Record the total package surface area if relying on regulation 8(1). At 100 cm² or below the provision addresses the ingredient list and directs that information to the multi-unit pack. Record the matching outer artwork and check any ingredient representation on the inner. Product-specific requirements and declaration placement are separate review points. A non-retail container has its own route under regulation 10. [Regulation 8(1), Gazette p. 38](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=38) · [Regulation 10, p. 40](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=40)

Neither amends this ingredient percentage clause.
