# R114 · December metal and toxin testing plans

Reviewed 22 November 2026. Select the assessment date in the catalogue, then choose **Export complete plan for all 533 products**. The full CSV contains 25,369 analyte and sampling-decision rows for 22 November and 25,321 for 1 December. Metals, toxins and veterinary residues follow the selected date. The composition, microbiology and pesticide rows reflect the 22 November review.

| Provision | Change on 1 December 2026 | Product plan |
|---|---|---|
| Lead and cadmium | The pulse entries expressly include pulse flours. The dry-soybean exclusion is retained for cadmium. | Yellow pea powder, besan and plain roasted Bengal gram flour use 0.2 ppm lead and 0.1 ppm cadmium. Soy derivatives and finished foods containing flour retain their classification checks. |
| Inorganic arsenic in fish oil | The maximum is 0.1 mg/kg. Total arsenic below the maximum completes the screen, while a result above it triggers inorganic arsenic testing. | The fish-oil Metals view interprets a quantified total-arsenic result. At exact equality the notification gives no screening disposition. The plan directs the reviewer to obtain an interpretation or speciation result. |
| Total aflatoxins and aflatoxin B1 | The revised food column names oils and oilseeds for further processing, and ready-to-eat oilseeds. | The identified edible oils have maxima of 15 and 10 µg/kg respectively. Mixed foods, derivatives and grade-dependent materials require evidence for the marketed article. |
| Saffrole | The earlier food-category entries are replaced by one entry for foods and beverages containing mace, nutmeg kernel or both as ingredients, with a 10 ppm maximum. | The generic catalogue export asks for the formulation. In a product worksheet, record ingredient presence and a supporting reference under Crop contaminants. The individual product plan then shows the applicable value or the documented absence. |

For a product, open **Testing plan and source decisions**. The date can be set in Metals, Toxins and other contaminants, or Veterinary drug residues. Product-level evidence is private to the worksheet. The generic catalogue export applies the same published planning decisions to each user. The detailed November metal and toxin source tables are retained for reference.

The May 2026 Gazette sets the commencement date and changes the food articles. The existing tables supply the concentration values retained for pulse flours and aflatoxins.
