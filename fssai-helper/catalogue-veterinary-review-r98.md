# R98 · Veterinary drug residues in the 533-product register

**Source review dated 20 November 2026.** The register now covers all four clauses of FSSAI regulation 2.3.2. A direct match means that at least one clause names the marketed food or its production group. A specific drug can still require the animal species, tissue, production origin or report unit.

| Finished-article finding | Catalogue products |
|---|---:|
| A current provision names the food or its production group | 117 |
| Finished classification, production process or scope requires evidence | 29 |
| The recipe indicates an ingredient review | 65 |
| No named finished article was identified from the catalogue description | 322 |
| **Total** | **533** |

One of the original 137 priority products has a direct match in this review, the catalogue entry for ice cream, kulfi and related milk products. The source match concerns the prohibited-use provision for milk products. The register contains zero product-wide regulatory sign-offs.

## What the entries cover

| Provision | Source entries | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| 2.3.2(1), seafood antibiotics | 4 | The November edition includes four seafood tolerances in mg/kg. The amendment omits this clause from 1 December 2026. |
| 2.3.2(2), substances prohibited in production | 14 substances or classes | The listed production groups include meat and meat products, milk and milk products, poultry and eggs, and aquaculture and its products. Establish aquaculture origin for a fishery sample. The EMRL is 0.001 mg/kg, with a chloramphenicol exception of 0.0003 mg/kg. |
| 2.3.2(3), honey | 12 analyte entries under 11 serial numbers | Antibiotic use is prohibited during honey production. The µg/kg MRPLs are used to detect misuse. The sulphonamide and streptomycin entries carry individual or collective conditions. |
| 2.3.2(4), named drugs and foods | 358 food and tissue rows under 49 drug headings | Match the animal, article, portion and unit. The full index retains two rows whose species heading is unresolved. |

The October 2024 Gazette replaced clauses (2) through (4) with effect from 1 April 2025. For the next edition, the 2026 Gazette adds seafood to trimethoprim, serial 45, at 0.05 mg/kg and adds oxolinic acid for seafood, serial 50, at 0.3 mg/kg. The worksheet's comparison uses the assessment date and the reviewed edition. ## Using a worksheet

In **Product finder**, select the food and open **Product worksheet → Veterinary drug residues → Review a sampled article**. Choose the product as sold or a separately sampled ingredient. Select the drug and food row, record the sample or specification reference and enter the laboratory result in the printed unit. The brief and register export identify which physical sample was reviewed. The finder can also display all 117 products that have a named provision.

- **Milk chocolate and milk powder.** The chocolate worksheet can record an assessment of an incoming milk or milk-powder sample. The milk powder worksheet shows the prohibited-use rules for milk products. A separate liquid cow-milk sample has 36 candidate clause (4) rows. Ceftiofur, gentamicin and spectinomycin have cattle-milk limits in mg/L. A result in mg/kg requires its own applicable basis. [Ceftiofur, PDF p. 20](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=20) · [Gentamicin, PDF p. 22](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=22) · [Spectinomycin, PDF p. 25](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=25)
- **Honey and honey wine.** The honey worksheet shows the 12 misuse-testing entries. The mead worksheet can record an incoming honey sample. The finished fermented beverage has a different identity. [Honey table, PDF p. 18](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=18)
- **Fish and shellfish.** The November seafood table lists tetracycline and oxytetracycline at 0.1 mg/kg. Clause (4) also prints 0.2 mg/kg for oxytetracycline in fish and for the relevant group in giant-prawn muscle. Where these can overlap, the worksheet withholds the numerical comparison. It also flags the broad animal-tissue and specific finfish entries for ampicillin. [Seafood table, PDF p. 19](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Contaminants_Regulations.pdf#page=19) · [Veterinary table, PDF pp. 18–20](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=18)
- **Drug identity and tissue.** Serials 39 and 41 use the names sulfadimidine and sulfamethazine. WHO/JECFA identifies them as synonyms. The helper flags the potential overlap between the 0.1 and 0.01 mg/kg tissue entries. The cattle-milk entry under serial 39 has its own basis. [Gazette, PDF pp. 25–26](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=25) · [WHO/JECFA identity record](https://apps.who.int/food-additives-contaminants-jecfa-database/Home/Chemical/4017)

The register has 533 unique catalogue IDs and 99 columns. The first 89 columns follow R97. The ten additional columns contain the finished-article finding, four clause-specific findings, candidate rows, official sources, the December change, sample evidence and any documented comparison.
