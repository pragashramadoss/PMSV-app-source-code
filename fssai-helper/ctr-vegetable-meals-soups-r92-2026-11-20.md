# Thermally processed curried vegetables and vegetable soups

**R92 · Reviewed 20 November 2026 · FSSAI 2.3.4 and 2.3.5**

| Catalogue entry | Article | Food category | What the lot check covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 187 · Curried and ready-to-eat vegetables | 2.3.4 | 04.2.2.8 | Article identity and rigid-container fill |
| 421 · Thermally processed vegetable soups | 2.3.5 | 12.5.1 | Soup identity, total soluble solids by mass and rigid-container fill |

## Product and sample

**Curried vegetables.** Article 2.3.4 covers culinary preparations made from vegetables, legumes, cereals or pulses that are heat processed for preservation. The recipe may include milk fat. The article gives a rigid-container fill minimum. It specifies neither a drained-weight percentage nor a soluble-solids minimum.

**Vegetable soup.** Article 2.3.5 covers heat-processed vegetable soup supplied for direct consumption. Confirm this identity for the product as sold. A dry mix or concentrate requiring dilution goes back for classification. For a blend containing tomato, record the product identity before selecting the tomato-soup row.

| Finished soup identity | Minimum total soluble solids by mass, 2.3.5(2) |
| --- | ---: |
| Vegetable soup, except tomato soup | 5.0% |
| Tomato soup | 7.0% |

At 6.0% total soluble solids by mass, the result meets the minimum for a vegetable soup other than tomato soup and falls one percentage point below the minimum for tomato soup. The sample and reported unit must support total soluble solids by mass in the finished soup. A raw instrument reading, a result for the concentrate, or solids measured on another basis needs the laboratory's basis confirmed before comparison.

## Rigid-container fill

Both articles require the food to occupy at least 90% of the full water capacity of a rigid container. Measure the volume of distilled water at 20 °C that completely fills the sealed container. Compare it with the occupied product volume in the same units. For example, 360 mL meets the minimum for a rigid pack with a measured 400 mL capacity. A nominal pack size or a weight in grams does not establish the volume measurements.

This provision is 2.3.4(3) for the curried preparation and 2.3.5(4) for soup. Retort and aseptic processing descriptions alone do not establish whether the pack is rigid or flexible. The form records the actual container. Soup composition can still be checked for a flexible pouch.

## Remaining product checks

Articles 2.3.4(2) and 2.3.5(3) refer to permitted additives and Appendix B microbiological requirements. The lot brief can retain the relevant report references. The R67 review for catalogue entry 187 and its open contaminants decision remain linked. Entry 421 sits outside the 137-product contaminants queue. Adding its lot worksheet does not change that queue's size.
