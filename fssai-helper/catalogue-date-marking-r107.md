# R107 · Date marking and expiry exemptions

Open **Product finder → a product → Packaging, labelling and claims → Small-pack declarations and date marking**. Select a pack level and the finished product’s identity. Enter the SKU specification or classification record and its approved shelf life. In the two date rows record the actual markings on the pack and, where relevant, on the corresponding retail outer. Save the artwork check to include it in the product brief and register.

| Artwork question | How the worksheet treats it |
| --- | --- |
| Which foods qualify for the product exemption? | Regulation 5(10)(d) lists whole fresh fruit and vegetables that have not been cut, peeled or similarly treated, wine, alcoholic beverages at or above 10% ABV, vinegar, sugar-boiled confectionery, food-grade salt for industrial use, solid sugars, and chewing or bubble gum. Confirm the finished article, including treatment or intended use where relevant. |
| Does my product still need MFD or PKD? | The general provision calls for a manufacture or packaging date. The product exemption in 5(10)(d) concerns the expiry indication. The worksheet assesses each declaration separately. |
| Is “Best before” sufficient? | Where expiry or use-by is required, best before is an additional declaration. Choose **Best before only** to record the actual artwork finding. |
| Is a 30 cm² wrapper eligible to move its dates to the retail outer? | The threshold in 8(1)(a) is strictly below 30 cm². Use all visible surfaces of the formed pack, excluding the seals, and identify the matching multi-unit retail pack. A measurement of 30.00 cm² does not qualify. The separate 100 cm² list does not provide this date exemption. |
| What about shelf life of exactly seven days? | Regulation 8(3) permits omission of manufacture date and calls for expiry or use-by. If a listed product also meets this shelf-life condition, the worksheet displays both clauses for regulatory review. |
| How is beer or another non-wine alcoholic drink assessed? | Enter the documented ABV. For a non-wine alcoholic beverage below 10%, Alcoholic Beverages 5.10 calls for an expiry or use-by day, month and year. Exactly 10% reaches the threshold in 5(10)(d)(iii). Wine has a separate entry. |

For example, on a regular retail pack of documented sugar-boiled candy with a shelf life over seven days, the expiry indication can be omitted under 5(10)(d)(v). For a chocolate bar, select the chocolate worksheet and the ordinary food route. An artwork carrying only best before leaves expiry or use-by outstanding.

The review covers the presence and location of retail dates. Enter any product-specific rule that requires further assessment under **Classification or product-specific provision unresolved**. For non-retail packs, consult regulation 10. This check does not validate a numerical shelf life or date printed on a particular lot.
