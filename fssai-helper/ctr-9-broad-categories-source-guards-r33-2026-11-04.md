| Sodium benzoate | Lead | 2 | 6 |
| Sodium benzoate | Arsenic | 3 | 10 |
| Steviol glycoside | Lead | 1 | 6 |
| Steviol glycoside | Arsenic | 1 | 10 |
| Synthetic food colour-preparation and mixtures | Lead | 10 | 6 |
| Synthetic food colour-preparation and mixtures | Arsenic | 3 | 10 |

**Do not merge analytical-basis qualifiers:** the separate printed arsenic row for *Synthetic food colour-preparation and mixtures* is 3.0 ppm without an express dry-matter note in that row. The different nearby source article *Preservatives, anti-oxidants, emulsifying and stabilising agents and synthetic food colours* prints 3.0 **on dry matter basis**. They are distinct source articles. The Lead table also separately prints *Food colours other than caramel* 10 ppm **on dry colouring matter basis**, distinct from *Synthetic food colour-preparation and mixtures* 10 ppm. Review the correct article, method and analytical basis rather than splicing qualifiers.

**Sorbitol is not all polyols.** Its Lead 1, Arsenic 3 and Nickel 2 ppm source rows should not be assigned to other substances such as mannitol, erythritol, xylitol or a formulated polyol mixture without their own legal article review. Similarly, a general permitted-additives category does not itself have the chemical identity sodium benzoate, steviol glycoside or a synthetic-colour preparation.

## Specific regulatory safeguards

- For category 11.6, require the precise polyol chemical identity and relevant INS/CAS/ingredient specification before selecting a named CTR source row.
- For category 99.1, require exact approved additive and substance form. Do not apply sodium benzoate, steviol glycoside or synthetic-colour limits to unrelated generic additives.
- For Sajji Khar, do not equate product identity with sodium hydroxide, sodium bicarbonate, salt or another alkali without analytical composition/source evidence.
- For enzyme preparations, do not equate enzymes with the source-table article *Sodium carboxymethyl cellulose, enzyme hydrolysed*.
- For a Category 13.2 formulated supplement for children, do not silently inherit the differently named infant formula or infant-food limits.
- For nutraceutical schedules/functional premixes, determine the exact composition and finished matrix. Source rows for individual chemicals or raw crops cannot automatically be transferred to a whole premix.
- Every source row remains a *candidate only*. Full legal assessment still needs current original Gazette notification checks, active amendments, analyte selection, matrix/sample basis, validated method and product-specific evidence.
- The notified May 2026 CTR amendment takes effect **1 December 2026**, not on this audit date. No future amendment is applied in this checkpoint.
