## 2021 original Gazette vs 2024 Chapter 2.4 compendium: material numerical directions

The **2021 original notified amendment** explicitly replaced FSSR **2.4.15 clauses 1 and 2**. It identifies **wafer biscuits** as a type of biscuit; do not treat the separate FoSCoS 7.2.2 listing as a different FSSR finished-product numerical clause.

| Source product / test | Published requirement | Official Chapter 2.4 PDF page (1-based) |
|---|---|---:|
| **Biscuit and wafer:** acid-insoluble ash | **≤0.1%**, dry mass basis | 57 |
| **Biscuit and wafer:** acidity of extracted fat, as oleic acid | **≤2.0%** | 57 |
| **Bread:** alcoholic acidity with 90% alcohol | **≤7.5 mL of 1N NaOH per 100 g dry substance** | 57 |
| **Rusks:** acidity of extracted fat as oleic acid | **≤1.5%** | 58 |
| **Bread and rusks:** acid-insoluble ash | **≤0.2%**, dry mass basis | 58 |
| **Pasta:** moisture | **≤12.5%** | 34 |
| **Pasta:** ash insoluble in dilute HCl | **≤0.1%**, dry mass basis | 34 |
| **Fried instant noodles:** moisture | **≤10.0%** by mass | 34 |
| **Non-fried instant noodles:** moisture | **≤13.0%** by mass | 34 |
| **Both fried and non-fried noodles:** acid-insoluble ash | **≤0.3%**, dry matter basis | 34 |
| **Fried instant noodles only:** acid value | **≤2.0**, source printed unit/method to be confirmed | 34 |
| **Non-fried instant noodles:** acid value | Source table uses **“—”**, not a numeric requirement | 34 |

**Do not revive the pre-2021 biscuit extracted-fat acidity figure (1.5%).** The amended biscuit standard states **2.0%**. Do not confuse the separately retained **1.5% rusk** fat acidity with biscuit. Bread's alcohol acidity has a completely different basis and unit than either fat-acidity test.

**Instant noodle seasoning warning:** The standard explicitly says instant noodle **does not apply to noodle seasoning**, and requires accompanying seasoning to be **labelled distinctly on the package**. Verify the seasoning formula and additives against the applicable independent category; do not borrow noodle moisture or acid-value limits.

## CTR Version IX (3 February 2026) — separate cereal/composite source rows

| Contaminant | Printed legal food article | Published reference (µg/kg) |
|---|---|---:|
| Total aflatoxins | Cereal and cereal products | **15** |
| Aflatoxin B1 | Cereal and cereal products | **10** |
| Total aflatoxins | Food product containing any of the above-mentioned food articles | **20** |
| Aflatoxin B1 | Food product containing any of the above-mentioned food articles | **10** |

All four printed entries were verified on the official CTR PDF **page 14 (zero-based 13)**. These are **candidate regulatory source articles only**. Which one legally applies to an actual biscuit with cocoa/milk/peanut filling, bread, pasta or instant noodle must be established using actual SKU identity and current original Gazette; there is no automatic selection of the favourable number. Likewise, the source **Wheat/barley** DON and OTA articles and **Cereal grains** lead references cannot blindly be assigned to a finished processed mixed product.

These four mycotoxin source rows are not a complete CTR, additives, microbiology or packaging/labelling evidence set.

## Evidence required for real-product regulatory review

Actual FBO manufacturing licence and FoSCoS category, exact ingredient composition and source species/parts, process type (bread/rusk, simple/coated/filled biscuit or wafer, pasta or fried/non-fried instant noodle), noodle seasoning/sachet identity, accredited test method and batch/lot results, ash/acidity/moisture/acid-value basis, all current relevant contaminants and pesticides, separate food additives and microbiology, packaging, allergen/claims/label artwork and effective legal Gazette notifications. Independent QA/regulatory signoff still required.
