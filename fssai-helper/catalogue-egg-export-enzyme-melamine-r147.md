# Egg powder exports, processing-aid preparations and melamine

Reviewed 26 November 2026. All 533 testing plans now include a melamine entry. Of the 533 products, 522 have at least one partial or conditional product-specific source under the Indian Contaminants, Toxins and Residues (CTR) Regulations. Eleven still await one. The export order and the processing-aid specification apply in their own legal settings.

## Melamine

CTR 2.5.1 sets maxima of 1.0 mg/kg for powdered infant formula, 0.15 mg/kg for liquid infant formula and 2.5 mg/kg for other foods. Use the article and form as supplied. For a product with multiple possible classifications, record the labelled form and its supporting reference in the worksheet. The individual testing plan uses that choice. The bulk plan leaves the limit blank for an unresolved article. Results reported by volume require a documented conversion before comparison with a mass-based maximum. [Gazette, p. 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Melamine_Milk.pdf#page=3) · [Corrigenda, p. 1](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Corrigenda_Amendment_Melamine_09_05_2016.pdf#page=1)

## Egg powder for export from India

The Ministry of Commerce and Industry names egg powder in S.O. 4368(E), clause (e). The export reference covers 12 antibiotics, 3 antiparasitic agents, 10 organochlorine or related entries and 4 pyrethroids. The first two tables use µg/kg. The organochlorine table uses mg/kg. The pyrethroid table gives 0.05 for each entry without stating the unit. The download preserves the numbers and leaves those four units open. The destination's more stringent limits govern where applicable. Match the destination, powder lot and the laboratory's residue definition. [Export order, pp. 6–7](https://www.eicindia.gov.in/WebApp1/resources/PDF/Order_Egg_Products1.pdf#page=6)

The colistin row retains the 2021 export reference for traceability. FSSAI's 2024 amendment prohibits its use in poultry and egg production and specifies an extraneous maximum of 0.001 mg/kg for the listed production articles. It does not specify how to convert that figure to finished egg powder. An export lot needs an assessment under both instruments and the destination requirements. The 2025 export amendment also restricts antimicrobials used for growth or yield and specified medicines at supplying farms and processing plants. [FSSAI Gazette, pp. 17–18](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_Notification%20dt%2017_10_2024.pdf#page=17) · [2025 export amendment, pp. 3–4](https://www.eicindia.gov.in/WebApp1/resources/PDF/SO%203225%20%28E%29%2015%20July%202025_Egg%20%20Egg%20Products.pdf#page=3)

## Enzyme preparations

For a preparation matching a marked Appendix C Table 11A processing-aid entry, the footnote gives six criteria. Match the listed preparation, source and intended application. The lead maximum is 5 mg/kg. The microbiological criteria are absence of Salmonella and E. coli in 25 g and a maximum total coliform count of 30 per gram. The footnote requires absence of DNA and antibiotic activity for preparations from microbial sources. The amendment took effect on 1 February 2026. The sampled material is the supplied enzyme preparation. The generic FoSCoS 99.2 entry excludes preparations already covered by the processing-aid route. [Gazette, pp. 16–17](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2025/07/6879d8af12522Palm%20Oil_FPSFA_Notification.pdf#page=16) · [Appendix C, p. 40](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20C%282%29.pdf#page=40) · [FoSCoS, p. 9](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/Compiled%20FPC_FoSCoS.pdf#page=9)

In Product finder, **Export egg powder and enzyme references** downloads all 35 entries. Each product also offers its own references. The ordinary testing plan displays the source and conditions and leaves the applied-limit field empty for these specialised entries. The open-product download contains 152 rows across the 11 products.
