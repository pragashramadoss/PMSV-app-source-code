## Melamine

CTR 2.5.1 specifies maximum levels of 1.0 mg/kg for powdered infant formula, 0.15 mg/kg for liquid infant formula, and 2.5 mg/kg for other foods. The March 2016 corrigendum fixes the section and clause numbering. A liquid formula limit is expressed per kilogram. The Helper asks for the form supplied and the supporting document before it assigns a conditional row.

Infant Nutrition Regulation 7 covers both liquid and powdered formula. Regulation 7(3), as amended in 2022, permits the name Infant Milk Food for a qualifying powdered formula subject to the ingredient exclusions in that provision. Formulated supplements for children under product standard 2.4.11(4) cover children above 24 months through 36 months. This distinct identity takes the other-foods melamine row. Specialised and follow-up nutrition entries retain an explicit article decision.

## Mercury and methyl mercury

CTR 2.1.1 item 6 lists mercury by food article. Twelve catalogue names have a specific named mercury row in this release. They include natural mineral water, packaged drinking water, food-grade salt, carrageenan and named shellfish. For the others the worksheet identifies a species, form or named-article check. The residual row requires a comparison with the named entries and relevant product standard.

Item 7 separately names all foods for methyl mercury calculated as the element, with a maximum of 0.25 ppm. The table heading specifies mg/kg or mg/L. The worksheet preserves the reported analyte and the matrix-dependent unit. A mercury result cannot by itself establish a methyl mercury concentration.

The May 2026 CTR notification takes effect on 1 December 2026. Its changes were screened separately. Neither mercury nor melamine is amended in that instrument.
