# R117 · Toxin laboratory results

In the product worksheet, open **Crop contaminants, natural toxins and fish contaminants**. For a conditional article, first use **Document article and ingredient decisions** to identify the sampled food and its supporting specification or recipe. The **Change effective 1 December 2026** section records the formulation for the revised saffrole entry.

Under **Check toxin laboratory results**, choose the assessment date and analyte. Record the sample role, lot, laboratory report reference, result and unit. Confirm the article, analyte definition and mass basis when they match the cited provision. The product worksheet keeps one measurement per analyte. Switching analytes retains the other measurements. **Save worksheet on this device** retains them when you reopen the product.

| Report example | Individual product plan |
|---|---|
| Whole milk powder sampled separately for dairy whitener, aflatoxin M1 reported at 0.0041 mg/kg | The ingredient result exceeds the whole milk powder maximum of 4 µg/kg. The report stays identified as an ingredient sample. |
| Same ingredient with a result of <0.005 mg/kg | Inconclusive. The laboratory’s upper bound is 5 µg/kg and does not establish whether the sample meets the 4 µg/kg maximum. |
| Total aflatoxins and aflatoxin B1 reported for one finished product | Each analyte has its own result, unit, reference and comparison. |
| A volume-based result, an unquantified ND, or a report with no matching food entry | The product plan carries the reported value and the reason a numerical assessment is pending. |

The product CSV and review brief include entered measurements. Generic testing plans for all 533 catalogue products contain the regulatory planning rows. A comparison applies to the particular analyte, sampled article and assessment edition.
