# Aflatoxins in spiced cocoa mixes and egg pickles

Reviewed 24 November 2026. The sample described in the laboratory report determines which entry in CTR 2.2.1(1) to use. The Gazette gives separate maxima for the incoming spice and a finished food that contains it. The same values apply in both the 24 November and 1 December testing plans. [Contaminant Gazette, p. 29](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=29) · [May 2026 amendment, p. 5](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf#page=5)

| Laboratory sample | Total aflatoxins, maximum | Aflatoxin B1, maximum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Finished food with a documented spice or spice mix | 20 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |
| Separately sampled spice or spice mix | 30 µg/kg | 15 µg/kg |

## Dry mixtures of cocoa and sugars

FPSFA 2.7.6(3) permits spices. Use the finished-food entries when the formulation lists a qualifying spice and the sample is the dry mix as marketed. Cocoa and sugar alone give no grounds for the spice entry. A separate incoming spice lot has its own limits. [Cocoa product Gazette, p. 22](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification/Gazette_Notification_Pulses_Grains_Maize_14_08_2018.pdf#page=22)

## Pickled eggs

FPSFA 2.5.3(5) describes cooked eggs combined with a spiced oil-based gravy. The vinegar process specifies a spice mix and then filters the pickling solution. Document the actual finished formulation and the portion tested. The helper leaves a drained egg or filtered liquor sample open pending a basis for that sample. [Pickled eggs Gazette, p. 30](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%20DATED%2021_10_2024_ammendments.pdf#page=30)

In the crop-contaminant worksheet, select the sampled article and enter its recipe, specification or sample reference. The laboratory controls accept a separate result for each analyte. Each comparison requires the matching sample role, lot or sample identifier, report reference and mass basis.
