## Minimum package to reach *independent regulatory assessment*

1. **Product and manufacturer:** Exact SKU, manufacturer, FSSAI licence/registered category, complete recipe with percentages, processing stages, intended use, primary pack, current label/artwork and shelf life.
2. **Applicable law:** Current original Gazette and effective amendments; exact standardized or proprietary classification; correct legal named contaminant food article, other qualifying articles, exceptions, raw-to-finished processing and units; applicable microbiology, additive, toxins, pesticide, veterinary residues and labelling/claims rules.
3. **Actual laboratory evidence:** Correct accredited method/scope and sample matrix, lot sample results, LOQ/reporting basis where applicable, other current required results, approved risk assessment and documented independent regulatory/QA decision.

**The R50 gate stops at 'EVIDENCE_REVIEW_READY'.** Even a complete intake bundle must never automatically mark the finished product compliant or authorize a commercial batch release. No need to add fictitious laboratory results or claim that every regulator decision can be completed from public pages.
