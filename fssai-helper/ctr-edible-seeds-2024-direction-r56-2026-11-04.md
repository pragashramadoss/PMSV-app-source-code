## Critical wrong-article distinctions

- **Whole fruit vs seed:** watermelon fruit flesh is not automatically watermelon seed for pesticide residues, sampling basis, lead, or aflatoxins; the legal commodity wording matters.
- **Whole seed vs seed oil/powder:** oil or powder can require another food category, analyte basis or processing-factor/method assessment.
- **Fruit seeds vs vegetable seeds:** product category 4.1.2.2 or 4.2.2.2 is a licensing route; actual species and food part must be determined before adopting any ingredient identity or limit.
- **No blanket nut/oilseed MRL transfer:** peanut, sesame, hemp, pumpkin, sunflower or other seed provisions cannot be applied merely because an item contains dried seeds.
- **The order does not waive** purity, microbial, contaminant, pesticide, packaging, label or import/export requirements otherwise legally applicable.
