## Methods, form and concentration boundaries

Printed pages 234–235 were visually checked: foreign matter uses the weighed sample; total/acid-insoluble ash and extractives use the air-dried ingredient basis. Appendix 2.2.6 calls for alcohol of a specified strength without resolving that strength for these entries; no 90% or 95% solvent is inferred. Appendix 2.2.7 refers to chloroform water. Appendix 2.2.10 explicitly uses a volume/weight percentage for volatile oil. The remaining apparatus/procedure pages and complete current method acceptance have not been fully reconciled; this is not a laboratory SOP.

Only dried botanical material of the named species and part is a candidate source match, subject to the unresolved naming issues. Other parts, species, oils, extracts, mixed and unknown materials remain outside an established match. A minimum volatile-oil content in heartwood/root does not permit replacing that part with essential oil.

The recipe's preparation reduces water to one-eighth of its original quantity. That instruction does not establish an eightfold contaminant multiplier: total ingredient mass, solids, analyte transfer and retention are not established by the water ratio. The helper supplies no finished-tea limit or food-dose calculator. The medicinal doses and therapeutic claims are not food-use guidance. Adopted ingredient purity criteria and their disclosure under Ayurveda Aahara regulation 3(5) remain distinct from a pharmacopoeial reference lookup.
