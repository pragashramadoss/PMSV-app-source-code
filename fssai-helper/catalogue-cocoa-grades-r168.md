# R168 · Cocoa bean procurement grades and finished-food metals

Reviewed 28 November 2026. Both testing plans cover 533 catalogue products.

## Receiving a cocoa bean lot

Open **Cocoa Beans → AGMARK cocoa bean purchase grades**. For a processor buying beans to make cocoa mass, liquor or cake, the same panel appears on that product’s worksheet and applies to the incoming bean lot. Enter the lot, purchase specification and laboratory report, then enter the results in the units shown. Each result is compared independently with Grades I and II. Verify the grade named in the order and the Schedule II general quality conditions before making the receiving decision. Authorised grading and marking are separate from a receiving check.

| Measurement | Unit | Grade I | Grade II |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mouldy beans | % by count, maximum | 3 | 4 |
| Slaty beans | % by count, maximum | 3 | 8 |
| Beans with insect damage, germination or flatness, combined | % by count, maximum | 3 | 6 |
| Moisture | % by mass, maximum | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| Weight of 100 beans | grams, minimum | 100 | 90 |
| Uniformly brown fermented beans on cutting | % by count, minimum | 80 | 70 |

For example, 95 g per 100 beans meets the Grade II weight criterion and falls below Grade I. The remaining five measurements still require their own results. Enter the combined defect percentage from distinct affected beans. Adding separate defect tallies can count the same bean twice. FSSAI 2.3.54 has separate limits for insect damage and for germinated and flat beans. The existing FSSAI worksheet retains those measurements.

## Metal limits in the open-products export

The available-checks CSV now includes nine comparisons already present in the individual metal panels. For eight non-beverage foods, the candidate tin articles each give 250 ppm. They cover the dry coffee–chicory blend, coconut milk powder, cocoa beans, cocoa mass/liquor/cake, arrowroot, egg powder, colostrum supplied as food and royal jelly. Verify the actual food, processing and pack. A tin used for storage alone does not establish canning. An instant drink prepared from powder, a beverage sold in a can, a named infant food or a receiving product made using an enzyme requires its own classification. For the dry coffee–chicory blend the candidate copper articles each give 30 ppm. Each new row identifies the candidate articles, their common maximum and the outstanding scope question.

## Outstanding domestic sources

On 1 December, the count becomes 523 of 533 with ten outstanding, including purified vending water.
