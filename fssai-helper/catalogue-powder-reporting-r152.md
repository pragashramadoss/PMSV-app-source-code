# Interpreting laboratory results for coconut milk powder and colostrum powder

Reviewed 26 November 2026. Open either product in the Helper, expand **Open product worksheet** and find **dry-basis check**. For colostrum, first select **Colostrum Powder** under **Product variant**. Enter the sample and report references, the basis printed on the report, and the result. As-supplied results require measured moisture from the same powder sample. The individual product plan includes the readings and comparison. The full-catalogue exports contain the calculation instructions.

| Finished sample | Composition question | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coconut milk powder | Is fat at least 60% on dry basis? Is moisture at or below 2.5% as supplied? | A report of 58.8% fat as supplied with 2.0% moisture gives exactly 60% fat on dry basis. Free fatty acids have a separate criterion on the extracted fat. |
| Cow or buffalo colostrum powder | Is total ash at or below 9% on dry basis? Is moisture at or below 4% as supplied? | A report of 8.64% ash as supplied with 4.0% moisture gives exactly 9% ash on dry basis. |

For an as-supplied percentage, multiply by 100 and divide by (100 minus the moisture percentage). A result already reported on dry basis goes directly to comparison. The Helper compares the unrounded value and reports moisture separately. A single composition comparison addresses the named parameter only.

For IgG in **cow colostrum powder**, AOAC 2010.01 uses protein G affinity liquid chromatography. AOAC 2012.26 uses an optical immunoassay based on surface plasmon resonance. Ask the laboratory to identify the species, method and reporting basis. For a buffalo or mixed-species powder, obtain documented method applicability for that sample. The FSSAI powder criterion is at least 8.5% IgG by mass as supplied. Lactoferrin and total protein require separate measurements.

Both editions contain 34 analytical references. The laboratory guide includes 203 entries for 26 November and 280 for 1 December. Of 533 products, nine and ten respectively still await a first product-specific domestic contaminant, toxin or residue source.
