# Polyol metals and outstanding product sources

26 November 2026 · R143

The R142 queue contained 13 catalogue entries. The three polyols below belong to one of those entries. This brings coverage to 521 of 533 products with at least one partial or conditional finished-product contaminant, toxin or residue source. Twelve entries still need a source specific to the marketed product or an applicable group.

## Which polyol limit applies?

| Supplied material | INS | Indian CTR arsenic maximum for a qualifying emulsifier or stabilizer | JECFA lead purity maximum | JECFA nickel purity maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maltitol | 965(i) | 3 mg/kg on dry matter | 1 mg/kg | 2 mg/kg |
| Maltitol syrup | 965(ii) | 3 mg/kg on dry matter | 1 mg/kg | 2 mg/kg |
| Xylitol | 967 | 3 mg/kg on dry matter | 1 mg/kg | 2 mg/kg |

**For the Indian comparison**, confirm the single supplied material, the category 11.6 classification, its permitted intended use and its actual function on the supplier or application record. Select emulsifier or stabilizer only when that function is documented. The laboratory report must identify the same lot and express arsenic on a dry-matter basis. This distinction matters especially for a syrup. The Helper leaves the limit unapplied for sweetener-only use, an unidentified preparation, a retail blend or an as-sold arsenic result. Maltitol syrup is a defined additive grade that naturally includes several hydrogenated saccharides. Its supplier specification should identify INS 965(ii). Standardized sorbitol has its own worksheet. [FSSAI Appendix A, INS identities](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20A%284%29.pdf#page=310) · [FSSAI functional classes, rows 506 and 508](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20C%282%29.pdf#page=74) · [Indian arsenic table, PDF p. 25](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/5f3d09f97b78aGazette_Notification_Limit_Metal_19_08_2020.pdf#page=25)

**For the JECFA purity checks**, use the monograph for the actual additive grade and its stated analytical basis. These references are listed independently of the Indian arsenic provision. The focused CSV identifies the source for every row.
