# Microbiological criteria by food form and process

27 November 2026 · R160

Seven product entries now ask for the form or process that determines the Appendix B sampling plan. The catalogue download lists the possible routes. In the individual worksheet, select the form and enter the specification, batch record or regulatory assessment reference. The single-product export then shows the criteria for that route, with the sampling plan, units and source pages.

| Catalogue product | Evidence that determines the route |
| --- | --- |
| Plain buttermilk | Confirm fermented-milk identity under 2.1.13. For a drink based on fermented milk, check the recipe for at least 40% fermented milk by mass. The liquid left after churning butter needs its own assessment. |
| Dried mint | Establish whether the marketed leaves are dried whole/broken or have been ground or crushed. Record the manufacturing specification when the description is ambiguous. |
| Dried laurel or bay leaf | Whole dried leaves and ground bay leaf powder have separate rows in Tables 3A and 3B. |
| Smoked meat sausages | Check whether further cooking is required. For the fully cooked route, document achievement of the minimum 75 °C core temperature. |
| Cured or pickled and cooked or smoked meat, whole pieces or cuts | Identify the preservation step, final heat treatment and intended consumption. Table 5 categories 1–5 require cooking before consumption. A ready-to-eat pickle needs separate review. |
| Cured or pickled and cooked or smoked meat, comminuted | Apply the same process and intended-consumption checks to the comminuted product. |
| Fermented meat, whole pieces or cuts | The Table 5 fermented-meat definition specifies chopped or ground meat. The worksheet records the assessment and leaves the numeric plan open for intact cuts. |

The updated plans cover both 27 November and 1 December. Process-hygiene and food-safety rows retain their individual organism, test portion, unit and sampling values. Product-specific chemical contaminant source decisions are tracked separately.
