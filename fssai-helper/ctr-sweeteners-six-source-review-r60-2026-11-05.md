# PMSV R60 — Six sweeteners: named FSSR standards, ash methods and CTR source guards

**5 November 2026 | Source evidence only — no whole-product compliance PASS.**

## What was newly reviewed

Six **distinct original catalogue IDs**, none of which was in the R45–R59 focused set: **Cane jaggery**, palm **Gur/Jaggery**, **Misri**, **Golden Syrup**, **Dried Glucose Syrup**, and **Edible Lactose**. Their FSSR source clauses and selected parameters were checked against the official FSSAI Chapter 2.8 and Chapter 2.1 compendia. Both FSSAI-hosted PDFs were visually examined on the cited pages, while the original amending Gazettes and full current legal panels have **not** been exhaustively reconciled.

## FSSR selected source values (not actual SKU test results)

**Cane jaggery, 2.8.4(2):** moisture ≤7%, sucrose ≥70%, total sugars ≥90%, reducing sugars **≥5.5% AND ≤20%**, sulphate ash ≤4%, acid-insoluble ash ≤0.5%, water-insoluble/extraneous matter ≤2%. The **5.5% minimum** is expressly shown as a later inserted provision **effective 1 September 2023** in the official compendium **PDF page 11**. No added colour is permitted by the printed cane jaggery clause. Preserve source methods and bases; do not make an automatic release decision.

**Palm gur/jaggery, 2.8.4(1):** total sugars as invert sugar ≥90%, sucrose ≥60%, water-insoluble extraneous material ≤2%, **total ash** ≤6%, acid-insoluble ash ≤0.5%, and moisture ≤10% **except liquid/semi-liquid variants**. Those values and the botanical source are **different** from cane jaggery.

**Misri, 2.8.2:** total ash ≤0.4% and sucrose ≥98%; extraneous matter ≤0.1% and no added colouring matter. Do not infer the named 'hard-boiled sugar confectionery' metal article just from the word *candy*.

**Golden syrup, 2.8.6:** moisture ≤25%, **total ash** ≤2.5% and total sugars as invert sugar ≥72%. **PDF pages 12–13** show that the adjacent **sulphated ash ≤0.1% / glucose ≥99%** table is the preceding **Dextrose 2.8.5** standard, **not** Golden Syrup. This is a material PDF text-order trap: screenshot takes precedence over garbled extraction.

**Dried glucose syrup, 2.8.7:** total solids ≥93%, reducing sugars ≥20%, **sulphated ash** ≤1.0%. The source prohibits added natural/coaltar colouring and added sweetening/flavouring agents. This product is **not** pure dextrose under 2.8.5.

**Edible lactose, 2.1.20:** whey-based milk ingredient, moisture ≤6%, lactose ≥99% **dry basis**, sulphated ash ≤0.3%, **pH of 10% solution 4.5–7.0**, and scorched-particle maximum **Disc B**. The official dairy chapter pages **72–73** were visually inspected. Neither the FPC 11 sweetener list nor the word 'sugar' makes lactose legally refined white sugar or erases milk-derivative allergen checks.

## Why these values cannot auto-select CTR lead limits

FSSAI CTR **Version IX, 3 February 2026**, visually checked **PDF pages 2 and 4**, prints **separately qualified** lead source entries:

| Exact source food-article description (shortened) | Source lead value |
|---|---:|
| All sugars, sugar syrup, invert sugar / specified coloured sugars with **sulphated ash exceeding 1.0%** | **5 mg/kg** |
| Anhydrous/monohydrate dextrose; refined white sugar with qualified sulphated ash **≤0.03%** | **0.5 mg/kg** |
| Edible molasses, caramel liquid, solid glucose and starch-conversion products with **sulphated ash exceeding 1.0%** | **5 mg/kg** |

**Do not** apply these amounts automatically to any of the six actual products. 'Total ash' is not 'sulphated ash', 'acid insoluble ash' is a third test, and a sulphated ash result **exactly 1.0%** does **not** satisfy 'exceeding 1.0%'. The standardized dried glucose syrup source maximum of 1% sulphated ash also does not establish the printed >1% lead source article. The actual legal commodity, full applicable regulation and analytical result need separate adjudication.
