# PMSV FSSAI Product Helper R93

## Coverage on 20 November 2026

The product finder has an evidence worksheet for each of the 533 catalogue IDs. Of these, 475 follow a product standard and 58 follow a special or category route. Those 58 include 18 additive ingredients with a separate identity or purity standard. All 533 entries have a page reference in an official source.

The worksheets represent 356 distinct product or purity standards. A shared standard can cover multiple catalogue forms. The app preserves differences between a numbered and named heading, a prior source-edition review matched by document fingerprint, a reference to the parent article, and a category or licensing directive.

## Official sources and checks

- Captured 227 distinct official PDFs across 251 document links. Identical copies and alternative official URLs count once in the PDF total. PDF page numbers are one-based. Each captured document includes its digest. Amendment mentions are navigation references and still require examination of scope and commencement.
- Reviewed all 504 distinct product and subtype listings in the Food-O-Copoeia directory. 244 catalogue IDs matched a named entry and 413 had a supported name or regulatory-article cross-reference. Shared articles retain the monograph’s exact subtype. The portal opens search results using a session, so the worksheet gives its exact search title.
- Compared matched parameters with the same product name, direction, unit family and dry-basis qualifier. 209 numerical agreements and 1 differing numerical entries were recorded across 86 products. These are selected composition cross-checks. A numerical agreement does not establish every condition, method or provision.
- The Tenth Amendment of 2020 sets the trans-fat maximum for partially hydrogenated and winterised soyabean oil at 2% by weight from 1 January 2022. The directory displays 5%. The worksheet links page 28 of the original Gazette, Chapter 2.2 and the date governing application. The applicable clauses are displayed for 10 catalogue entries, including conditional fat spread types.
- Linked existing named metal profiles for 147 products to the original printed article, analyte, numerical amount and Gazette page. The commodity lookup provides 1856 sourced rows for metals, crop contaminants, residues and other published entries. Source qualifiers, ingredient versus finished-food form, units, amendment dates and method scope remain part of a product decision.
- Direct visual checks of pages 84, 88, 91, 93, 94, 95, 96, 98 and 102 of the 25 July 2025 Ayurveda Aahara order confirmed nine printed recipe headings. The documented title and method interpretation questions for 14.6 and 14.7 remain open.

## Using the files

Open Product finder, select an entry and open its product worksheet. The app provides the standard or classification, additive tables and notes, microbiology routes, contaminant references, pack material, label and claims checks. Enter a SKU or sample reference and capture the published article and evidence. Optional device saving preserves a worksheet in that browser. Export product review brief produces a portable text copy. The catalogue CSV is also available directly from Product finder.

## Outstanding regulatory decisions

Of the 533 catalogue entries, 396 have some direct product-specific contaminant evidence and 137 still need an exact finished-product article decision. Every individual row contains its next action. Some require a species, formulation, process or pack. Others require legal-source interpretation or clarification even when product records are available. Full verification against every applicable current provision remains open for all 533. A report for a commercial lot is a separate assessment.

The downloadable register and the app export have the same 533 catalogue IDs. The last CSV columns can record the SKU or formulation, process or variant and evidence document reference. The standalone HTML includes the searchable tables and works offline. Opening external official documents requires an internet connection.
