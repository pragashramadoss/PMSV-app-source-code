## Important legal finding — tin is not always can-restricted

Version IX contains distinct **Tin 250 mg/kg** rows for **'Pulp and pulp products of any fruit'** and **'Jam, Jellies and Marmalade'** (PDF page 11). Unlike the separate **'Canned foods other than beverages'** entry, the two named fruit-product rows do **not** print a requirement for a metal can. Do not add a can-only gate to them. However, a Murabba or Fruit Cheese catalogue entry cannot automatically inherit those articles without proper legal classification and processing/matrix evidence.

## Assessment conditions

For any candidate limit: verify the exact final product/SKU and recipe, source article, actual botanical species/processing, relevant amendments, finish-pack type where the source actually requires it, analytical matrix and units, method, laboratory result and panel completeness. If any applicable source clause conflicts, the original Gazette and later operative amendments must be reconciled. A single within-limit measurement cannot establish full product compliance.

The FSSAI CTR compendium is Version IX dated 3 February 2026; the 2026 amendment that starts on **1 December 2026** is not operative on 4 November 2026. The source table was inspected by PDF text and page images. This audit adds only supplementary source-candidate metadata to the draft audit branch; existing catalogues, operative numerical rules, PASSES, commercial releases and `gh-pages` remain unchanged.
