# Imported Sajji Khar, colostrum and royal jelly

Reviewed 24 November 2026. Across the 533 product entries, 516 have at least one partial or conditional contaminant, toxin or residue source. Seventeen still need a first source. Colostrum and royal jelly are among those 17. Their product standards support the composition checks below.

## Imported Sajji Khar (Papad Khar)

FSSAI’s 23 June 2021 clarification directed testing imported Sajji Khar under the existing provisions for foods not specified until specific standards are notified. Customs circulated the clarification in Instruction 26/2021. The worksheet asks for the bill of entry or consignment reference, the ingredient lot, and confirmation that the instruction applies on the assessment date. Finished papad and other alkalis need separate assessments. ## Cow or buffalo colostrum and colostrum powder

Regulation 2.1.23 gives separate tables for colostrum and powder. The 2024 amendment, effective 1 May 2025, identifies the immunoglobulin analyte as IgG. Its minimum is 1.8% by mass in colostrum and 8.5% in the powder. Protein is calculated from total nitrogen multiplied by 6.38. In the powder table, total ash has a maximum of 9% on a dry basis, and scorched particles are checked against Disc B (15 mg). [Product identity and composition, pp. 8–9](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/5bfd095d0ad63Gazette_Notification_DairyProducts_27_11_2018.pdf#page=8) · [IgG amendment, pp. 25–26](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%20DATED%2021_10_2024_ammendments.pdf#page=25)

## Royal jelly

Select Type 1 for bees fed only natural bee food or Type 2 when additional nutrients are supplied. The standard gives different carbon-isotope delta ranges for the two types. The Type 1 range is −29 to −20‰ and the Type 2 range is −29 to −14‰. Four sugar maxima apply only to Type 1. For both types the moisture row pairs a maximum heading with a printed range of 62–68.5% by mass. The worksheet sends that result for manual interpretation. Furosine is an optional freshness indicator. [Product definitions and chemical requirements, pp. 24–25](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Pulses_Grains_Maize_14_08_2018.pdf#page=24)
