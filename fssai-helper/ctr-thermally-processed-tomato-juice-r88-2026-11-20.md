# Thermally processed tomato juice

Reviewed 20 November 2026. Catalogue entry 7 corresponds to FSSAI 2.3.8 and food category 14.1.2.2. It covers unfermented, heat-processed tomato juice, including properly reconstituted juice. The 2017 Sixteenth Amendment replaced this clause. The Gazette and the May 2025 FSSAI chapter give the same characteristics, limits and pack rules.

## Laboratory results

| Provision | Characteristic and reporting basis | Requirement |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2.3.8(5), row 3 | Soluble solids excluding salt, % by weight | At least 5.0 |
| Row 4 | Sodium chloride, % by weight | At most 3.0 |
| Row 5 | Total titratable acidity as citric acid, % by weight | At most 10.0 |
| Row 6 | Volatile acidity as acetic acid, % by weight | At most 0.4 |
| Row 7 | pH | At most 4.5 |
| Row 8 | Sugar expressed as invert sugar, % by weight | At most 42.0 |
| 2.3.8(2) | Mineral impurities as % of dry weight after deducting common salt | At most 0.1 |

Each result has its own reporting basis. Enter the final value stated by the laboratory. A pH result answers the pH row only. The two acidity rows require different analytes and acid equivalents. FSSAI’s fruit and vegetable methods manual, section 2.12, describes the salt correction for tomato-juice soluble solids. [Final notification, PDF p. 10](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Fruits_Vegetables_28_11_2017.pdf#page=10) · [Soluble-solids method, PDF p. 35](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual_Fruits_Veg_25_05_2016.pdf#page=35)

## Container measurements

Under 2.3.8(6), measure the volume of distilled water at 27 °C that fills the sealed container completely. For glass, deduct 20 mL from that measured capacity once. The required juice volume is 90% of the applicable capacity. All container materials are covered. Use the full measured capacity as the input so the Helper can apply the glass deduction. [Final notification, PDF p. 11](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Fruits_Vegetables_28_11_2017.pdf#page=11)

| Container | Full measured capacity | Capacity used | Minimum juice volume |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Glass bottle | 300 mL | 280 mL | 252 mL |
| Metal can or other identified non-glass pack | 300 mL | 300 mL | 270 mL |

For cans, 2.3.8(5) also specifies negative vacuum in row 1 and a maximum headspace of 7 mm in row 2. Record the reported gauge-pressure sign or a confirmed interpretation of the gauge scale. A magnitude without its sign convention leaves the vacuum assessment open. Changing the container type clears its measurements and retains the juice’s laboratory results.

The product brief includes the lot, reporting bases, individual comparisons, applicable pack checks and sources. Clause 2.3.8 also specifies colour, flavour, texture and freedom from extraneous material. It excludes added colours and artificial flavours. Catalogue entry 7 is outside the 137-case contaminant work queue.
