# R128 · Finished Ayurveda Aahara contaminant testing

24 November 2026. The offline plan now includes ten Schedule D Table 2 entries for each of the ten Category A catalogue recipes. Regulation 5 points covered finished foods to Schedule D. The Gazette page images confirm the seven metals on PDF page 24 and the two starred aflatoxin entries and melamine on PDF page 25. The recipe order identifies 14.2 Ksheerasara on PDF page 92, bringing this plan to ten recipes across the catalogue. [Regulation 5](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2022/05/62789a20b54bdGazette_Notification_Ayurveda_Aahara_09_05_2022.pdf#page=16) · [Recipe 14.2](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf#page=92)

| Printed maximum | Finished-food condition |
| --- | --- |
| Lead 2.5, copper 30, arsenic 1.1, tin 250, cadmium 1.5, mercury 1 and methyl mercury calculated as the element 0.25 | mg/kg or mg/L on the documented reporting basis |
| Melamine 2.5 | mg/kg |
| Total aflatoxins 20 and aflatoxin B1 10 | µg/kg for foods containing a listed ingredient |

The starred footnote covers arecanut/betelnut, cereals and cereal products, dried figs, nuts, oilseeds or oil, pulses, and spices or spice mixes, with the processing categories printed in the table. Record the exact ingredient and its classification. Coconut alone does not establish a listed group in this helper. [Metal entries](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2022/05/62789a20b54bdGazette_Notification_Ayurveda_Aahara_09_05_2022.pdf#page=24) · [Aflatoxins, footnote and melamine](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2022/05/62789a20b54bdGazette_Notification_Ayurveda_Aahara_09_05_2022.pdf#page=25)

In the product worksheet, open **Testing plan and source decisions → Ayurveda Aahara · Schedule D**. Record the recipe and finished-product specification. Recipes 14.6 and 14.7 also require a reference resolving the variant described in the printed order. A measured comparison uses the finished sample, report, analyte and compatible unit. The bulk plans show the published value as a reference and reserve the applicable limit column for a documented recipe. The focused export covers chemical contaminants. Microbiological sampling is a separate part of Schedule D.

The catalogue still has 506 of 533 products with a partial or conditional first source in the Contaminants, Toxins and Residues Regulations. Two of the 27 pending products, Narikela Ksheerika and Ksheerapaka, now have usable finished-food testing plans under the Ayurveda Aahara Regulations. A separate CTR article and wider product compliance review remain open.
