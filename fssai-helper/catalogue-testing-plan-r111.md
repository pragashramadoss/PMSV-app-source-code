# R111 · Full catalogue testing plan

Open the product finder and choose **Export testing plan for all 533 products**. To review a single product, open its worksheet, expand **Testing plan and source decisions**, and select a review area. The product download includes all four areas. The reference plan uses catalogue identity. The worksheet register export also carries entries saved on the device.

| Area | Rows | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Composition and identity | 3,822 | Product and ingredient standards, form-specific limits, alternative provisions and decisions that need a recipe, class, process or source check. |
| Microbiology | 2,550 | Appendix B and product-specific requirements, process hygiene, food safety, exceptions and open mappings. The n, c, m and M fields retain the published values where available. |
| Metals | 5,863 | Eleven CTR metal headings for each product. A limit appears when the reviewed identity or a documented common maximum supports it. |
| Toxins and other contaminants | 6,396 | Twelve CTR headings per product with named finished articles, recipe or ingredient conditions, and outcomes where the stated identity has no named match. |
| Total | 18,631 | All 533 catalogue identities appear in all four areas. These are criteria and applicability decisions. The count is not a number of compulsory laboratory tests. |

## Choosing an applicable row

- Filter by **Catalogue ID** and **Review area**. Some distinct products share a display name.
- Match **Product form or sample** and the conditions before using a limit. A process-hygiene row, a food-safety row and an ingredient limit answer different questions. A blank limit directs the reviewer to the accompanying status and action. A blank sampling-plan field does not mean zero.
- Select the named milk class or cheese variety. For flavoured milk, identify the milk used to make it. For mozzarella, match the moisture grouping and fat-in-dry-matter band together. Shared dairy ingredients in an Ayurveda Aahara recipe require a separate recipe decision.
- For milk products, the product-specific Appendix B exclusions apply to the displayed and exported rows. Examples include the casein-only yeast and mould count, the exclusion for mould-ripened cheese, the ripened butter aerobic-count exception and the infant-formula Enterobacteriaceae footnote.
- Specific SKU evidence, pesticide and veterinary-residue decisions, methods and test frequency are separate worksheet or source checks. The plan does not issue product or batch approval.

## Source coverage

The plan combines the current Helper's source register with page-linked rows from the earlier product review. For 64 products, 974 links connect catalogue identities to 758 distinct source-edition records. Archived rows are matched to the source document and page. Conflicting, qualitative, conditional and non-applicable cells retain their scope. The frozen-fruit and frozen-curried-vegetable rows were checked directly against the current Chapter 2.3 PDF; the unusual process wording in 2.3.39 is flagged. All 72 fruit-juice table entries were compared with the current edition for °Brix, acidity and the direction of the limit.

Sources include the FSSAI [food product standards index](https://fssai.gov.in/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards), [Chapter 2.1 dairy tables](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Chapter%202_1_Dairy_products_and_analogues.pdf#page=9), [Chapter 2.3 fruit and vegetable tables](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=13), [Appendix B footnotes](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards/Appendix%20B%281%29.pdf#page=19) and [the alcoholic beverage regulations, Version V](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/Comp_Alcoholic_Beverages_V_04_12_2025.pdf#page=15). The testing CSV includes the official source for each row.
