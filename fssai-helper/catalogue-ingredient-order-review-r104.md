# R104 · Ingredient order on artwork

Open **Product worksheet → Packaging, labelling and claims → Ingredient order on artwork**. Enter the approved recipe quantities and transcribe the artwork independently, one printed ingredient or INS identifier per line. If the artwork uses a different name, record its mapping on the recipe row. Select a complete list or a comparison of selected entries. **Save ingredient-order check** makes the findings available in the product brief, order CSV and full register. Missing formulation evidence joins the existing **Product finder → R&D recipe requests** export in column C.

## Choosing the comparison

| Review | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| Finished-food list | Compare the eligible ingredients on a common weight or volume basis at manufacture. Single-ingredient foods have a separate treatment under the opening of regulation 5(2). A one-entry list has no relative order to compare. [Gazette p. 30](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=30) |
| Additives within one functional class | Enter each additive’s actual quantity and map the specific name or recognised INS identifier printed on the pack. Check the functional class and identification separately under 5(5). The comparison shows the quantitative rank of the selected additives. Regulation 5(5) supplies the identification rule. A sequence of INS numbers alone supplies no evidence of relative amounts. Regulation 11 concerns additives sold as such. [Gazette p. 30](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=30) · [p. 35](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=35) · [p. 40](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=40) |
| Bracketed compound ingredients | Check the supplier’s composition at manufacture of the compound. Enter quantities by mass or percentages by mass and transcribe the bracket contents. The compound’s position in the finished-food list is a separate comparison. [Gazette p. 31, 5(2)(e)(i)](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31) |
| Compound constituents listed individually | Supply the breakdown and calculation reference. Enter the amount each source contributes to the final batch after applying the supplier proportions. Identical ingredients from distinct named sources are added together. [Gazette p. 31, 5(2)(e)(ii)](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31) |
| Compound share below 5% | Enter the compound share and evidence of which additives perform a technological function in the final food. Those additives remain relevant to disclosure. A 5.00% share falls outside the below-5% proviso. Assess allergen declarations separately. [Gazette pp. 30–31, 5(2)(c) and (e)](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31) |
| Reconstituted food | For dehydrated or condensed foods intended for reconstitution with water, document the preparation directions, enter the quantities on the prepared basis and check the accompanying statement. For other foods, review added water, water in declared brine or syrup, and ingredients evaporated during manufacture under 5(2)(f). [Gazette p. 31](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/12/5fd87c6a0f6adGazette_Notification_Labelling_Display_14_12_2020.pdf#page=31) |

## Reading the finding

For a fictional 100 kg batch, additive A contributes 300 g and additive B contributes 0.200 kg. If the printed list reads B then A, the helper flags that A contributes more. If both contribute 200 g, the amounts have equal rank. The helper compares the supplied quantities at their entered precision. The reviewer checks that they come from the approved formula applicable to this artwork.

Mass units can be combined with other mass units and volume units with other volume units. A percentage comparison uses a single percentage basis throughout. When converting mixed units or a preparation into its final-recipe contribution, retain the underlying calculation and supplier evidence. The worksheet flags ambiguous aliases, repeated entries, and percentage totals above 100%. A complete-list check identifies unpaired entries. A selected-entry check identifies any other printed entries outside its scope.

A blank or absent list leaves the transcription open. For a small pack, check the total area and the corresponding multi-unit artwork under regulation 8(1). Non-retail containers follow regulation 10. Product-specific labelling, allergen disclosure, additive permission and limits require their own review.
