# R127 · Formulated supplements for children

Reviewed 24 November 2026. One catalogue product gains a conditional first contaminant source. Of 533 products, 506 now have at least one partial or conditional source match and 27 still await their first match.

## Identify the product and sample

FPSFA 2.4.11(4) covers formulated supplements for children over 24 months up to 36 months and permits milled cereals and pulses. Record the marketed age range and product specification. For the finished food, record the ingredient list, classification and sampled lot. A cereal in the recipe alone does not establish which finished-food entry applies. Where both entries appear possible, keep the result pending until the classification is resolved. Paragraph (7) applies the contaminant regulations to the product. It also requires manufacturing practices that minimise pesticide residues and freedom from hormone and antibiotic residues as determined by agreed analytical methods. Record the method and reporting limit for the latter. The plan includes both provisions as qualitative checks.
