# R121 · Pesticide residue checks for tamarind, frozen fruit and chia

Reviewed 23 November 2026. Under **Product finder → Pesticide residues**, choose the tested product form, identify its process and enter the specification or process record. Each eligible form now has three pesticide comparisons. Each saved laboratory result retains the sampled article, Gazette page, reporting basis and sample reference.

| Tested form | 2,4-D (mg/kg) | Paraquat (mg/kg as cation) | Thiometon (mg/kg, residue sum) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Plain seedless tamarind or plain single-fruit frozen product, once the fruit entry is confirmed | 2 | 0.05 | 0.5 |
| Plain whole chia seed, once the food-grain entry is confirmed | 0.01 | 0.1 | 0.03 |

For thiometon, the laboratory result must include the parent compound, its sulfoxide and its sulfone, expressed as thiometon. A parent-only result leaves the reporting-basis confirmation open. A paraquat result must be expressed as the cation. The worksheet checks the reported concentration or upper bound after the reviewer confirms the basis. ## Which samples qualify

- **Seedless tamarind.** Confirm ripe fruit with the shell and seeds physically removed. Verify the full process and any added ingredients. Dried pulp, paste and concentrate need separate assessment. [FSSAI 2.3.49, p. 73](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=73)
- **Frozen fruit.** Identify a single fruit and document physical preparation and freezing only. Blanching, added syrup or other treatment requires separate assessment. [FSSAI 2.3.37, p. 40](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=40)
- **Chia.** Identify plain whole seed and document its classification as a food grain for the selected entry. The Gazette uses the other-food-grains branch for 2,4-D and paraquat, and the food-grains row for thiometon. Milled, extracted and heat-treated products fall outside this check. The FSSAI identity standard places chia under 2.4.6(25). [FSSAI Third Amendment 2020, p. 18](https://stg-old.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2020/08/5f362b888233bGazette_Notification_FSS_Additives_Third_Amendment_14_08_2020.pdf#page=18)

FSSAI paragraph 3 extends raw-commodity residue limits to physical processing and lists drying among the processes excluded from that extension. The May 2026 amendment taking effect on 1 December addresses other provisions. The same six additional product–pesticide combinations appear in both testing plans. [Processing clarification, paragraph 3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/01/5ffd3100b024cLetter_Clarifications_Tolerance_Limit_11_01_2021.pdf) · [May 2026 amendment](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/contaminants-toxins/272840.pdf)

The register includes the expanded sources under **Pesticide article and processing checks** and **Pesticide source references**. The **Conditional 2,4-D group source and evidence** column retains the original first-source entry. Coverage stays at 501 of 533 products with a partial or conditional source. The other 32 await their first documented route.
