# Product standards for powders and starches

Reviewed 24 November 2026. The six worksheets cover 26 numerical comparisons. Each result belongs to the named product, lot, laboratory report, unit and analytical basis. The 39-row export also includes identity, qualitative criteria and two readings that need clarification.

| Marketed product | What the worksheet checks |
| --- | --- |
| Arrowroot | Identity of the purified rhizome starch under 2.4.14(1). |
| Tapioca sago | Eight numerical parameters plus paste colour and absence of colouring matter. |
| Palm sago starch | The same ten parameters. The Gazette expressly extends the sago provision to the two named palm species. |
| Coconut milk powder | Moisture, fat on dry matter, free fatty acids in the extracted fat expressed as lauric acid, and bulk density. |
| Carob powder | Total ash, acid-insoluble matter and tannin in husk-free powder from roasted carob pods. |
| Water chestnut flour (Singhare ka Atta) | Moisture, alcoholic acidity on dry matter and acid-insoluble ash. Protein and uric acid have separate source-review entries. |

## Arrowroot and sago

The original regulation defines arrowroot under clause 1. The 2017 amendment replaces clause 2 and gives the sago table, including its extension to palm sago starch. The helper previously displayed both sets of sago entries under arrowroot and duplicated them in the sago plans. It now selects the actual product before displaying or exporting the criteria. ## Singhare ka Atta

For protein on dry matter, the English Gazette gives a minimum of 9.0% and the Hindi table gives a maximum of 9.0%. Both tables print 100 mg/kg for uric acid without specifying the direction of comparison. Each worksheet shows the two references and leaves the automatic comparison blank for those parameters. The finished flour also remains open for a specific contaminant provision. The existing aflatoxin criteria for separately sampled incoming dried Trapa nuts remain available in the ingredient worksheet. ## Coconut milk powder and carob powder

For coconut milk powder, use a maximum of 2.5% moisture, a minimum of 60% fat on dry matter, a maximum of 0.2% free fatty acids in the extracted fat expressed as lauric acid, and bulk density of 0.30–0.45 g/mL. For carob powder, the maxima are 1.2% total ash and 5% acid-insoluble matter by weight. The tannin range is 0.10–0.15%.
