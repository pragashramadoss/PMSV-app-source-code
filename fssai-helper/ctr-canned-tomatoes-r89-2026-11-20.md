# Canned tomatoes

Reviewed 20 November 2026. Catalogue entry 186 is canned tomatoes, FSSAI 2.3.3A, food category 04.2.2.4. The Sixteenth Amendment inserted this article. It was notified on 17 November 2017, published on 21 November 2017 and required compliance by 1 July 2018. [Notification and scope, PDF p. 8](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Fruits_Vegetables_28_11_2017.pdf#page=8)

## Product and covering liquid

Record whether the tomatoes are peeled or unpeeled and whether the style is whole, diced, sliced, wedges, pulp, crushed or chopped. Regular packs have added liquid. Solid packs have no added liquid. For a confirmed packing medium of water, tomato juice or tomato purée, the Helper checks the following figures. Other or unidentified media require a scope review.

| FSSAI 2.3.3A | Test | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (5)(1) | Added common salt as a percentage of net weight | 3% |
| (5)(2) | Total calcium ions when calcium chloride is added as a firming agent | 0.045% for whole tomatoes or 0.080% for non-whole tomatoes |
| (5)(3) | pH of the covering liquid | 4.5 |

The salt determination counts natural chlorides as 2% of the dry weight. Enter a laboratory result already corrected on that basis. For calcium, use the measured total calcium ions. The recipe dose of calcium chloride is a different quantity. On opening, paragraph (6) also specifies colour, odour, firmness, uniformity and freedom from defects. [Gazette requirements, PDF p. 9](https://fssai.gov.in/docs/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards/Gazette_Notification_Fruits_Vegetables_28_11_2017.pdf#page=9)

## Inspection per 1 kg net weight

| Characteristic | Whole style | Non-whole styles |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Aggregate blemish area, maximum | 3.5 cm² | 3.5 cm² |
| Peel present on peeled tomatoes, maximum | 30 cm² | 125 cm² |
| Peel missing from unpeeled tomatoes, maximum | 30 cm² | 125 cm² |

Use an inspection result reported on the 1 kg net-weight basis in (7). Changing the peel declaration changes the defect being measured. The Helper clears the affected result when its interpretation changes. [FSSAI consolidated chapter, PDF pp. 8–9](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=8)

## Can weight and container volume

Paragraph (8) specifies negative vacuum, headspace of at most 7 mm and drained weight of at least 56% of the net contents of the can. The headspace is measured from the top of the double seam to the product surface. For a can containing 400 g net, 224 g drained meets the minimum exactly. Use actual net contents and drained solids from the same can. The 2016 FSSAI fruit and vegetable methods manual, section 1.4, gives a tomato-specific 11.2 mm square-opening sieve and a two-minute drain. Net contents are gross weight less can tare. [FSSAI method, PDF pp. 5–6](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual_Fruits_Veg_25_05_2016.pdf#page=5)

Paragraph (9) separately sets a 90% minimum volume fill. Measure the full capacity with distilled water at 27 °C. For glass, deduct 20 mL once from that capacity before calculating the minimum. A 300 mL glass container therefore needs 252 mL of product and a 300 mL metal can needs 270 mL. The Helper applies (9) to an identified container and shows the (8) can-specific measurements for a metal can. It leaves the scope of those can-worded rows open for other materials.
