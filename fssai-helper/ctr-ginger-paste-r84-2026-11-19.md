# Ginger paste in the catalogue and Krishara

Reviewed 19 November 2026. FSSAI clause 2.3.28(2), row 4, identifies ginger paste separately from general culinary paste. The two composition minima agree in the original notification and the consolidated chapter.

| Test on the ginger paste | Minimum | Laboratory reporting basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total soluble solids | 3.0% | Salt-free, mass by mass |
| Acidity | 1.0% | Expressed as acetic acid |

The Helper compares each result independently, including a result exactly at the minimum. Use the reporting basis on the laboratory record. Uncorrected °Brix and pH cannot be compared directly with these criteria. A missing result leaves only that test pending.

The 5 June 2024 draft, STD/42-FA/Notification/2023, proposed an acidity exception for retort or aseptically processed products. The 2025 consolidated page reproduces the original ginger-paste row. The review identified the proposal in the consultation record and did not verify a subsequent operative notification or direction adopting it. The Helper compares with the published table and links the proposal beside acidity. Verify the effective instrument before applying an exception to a lot.

For rigid containers, clause 2.3.28(3) also requires the paste to occupy at least 90% of water capacity. Water capacity is determined using distilled water at 20 °C. The clause refers to Appendix A for permitted additives and Appendix B for microbiological requirements. Its colour provision allows caramel only among added colours. These provisions require their own review.

## Where the comparison applies

- **Ginger paste, catalogue item 193.** Select the named product, enter the laboratory reporting basis and result for each test, then export the product brief with the optional lot reference.
- **Krishara (Khichdi), recipe 13.1.** The ingredient table lists ginger rhizome as Ardraka, item 4. The preparation adds a paste to ghee. Selecting purchased ginger paste opens the comparison for that supplied ingredient. Check the supplier formula and its suitability for the recipe separately. When grinding ginger on site, record the form received and the processing step. The finished khichdi has no numerical limit assigned by this review.
- **Sugandhit Ksheera, recipe 14.6.** The first milk preparation uses pieces of ginger. Its ginger selector records the actual supply and retains the printed method. Switching to paste gives access to the separate article.
- **Juice, ginger–garlic blends and extracts.** Each needs its own article assessment. The ginger-paste row supplies no automatic minimum for these materials. Dried-spice contaminant rows have their own product and reporting bases.

The 533-product catalogue includes one named ginger-paste article. This review adds composition evidence. The 137 open contaminant-article decisions and 112 candidate ingredient-to-article links are counted separately.
