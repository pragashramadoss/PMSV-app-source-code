# Sulphites in sugars and syrups

Reviewed 25 November 2026. The 13 catalogue entries link to 16 form-specific Appendix A references and one dried-syrup pack declaration. Each reference concerns the sampled sugar or syrup. The testing-plan export identifies the food category, reporting basis and official pages.

| Sample | Table 11 reference for sulphites |
| --- | --- |
| Refined sugar | 20 mg/kg on its separately named line. Review the laboratory basis manually. |
| Anhydrous or monohydrate dextrose | 15 mg/kg as residual SO₂. |
| Icing sugar | 20 mg/kg as residual SO₂. |
| Khandsari sugar | Sulphur grade 150 mg/kg as residual SO₂. Direct addition is prohibited for the desi grade. |
| Bura sugar | 150 mg/kg as residual SO₂. |
| Dried glucose syrup | Parent entry 11.1.3 lists 150 mg/kg. For sugar confectionery, 11.1.3.1 prints 20 mg/kg with notes 111 and 44. Note 111 specifies 150 mg/kg for this use. The pack rule below also applies. |
| Edible lactose | Direct addition is prohibited. Check the manufacturing record. |
| Plantation white sugar, cube sugar and Misri | 70 mg/kg as residual SO₂. |
| Gur or jaggery, including the separate cane jaggery entry | 50 mg/kg sulphite residue in the finished jaggery. Review the report basis manually. |
| Golden syrup | Category 11.3 gives 70 mg/kg for qualifying inverted sugar syrups outside 11.1.3. For glucose syrup documented for sugar confectionery, 11.1.3.2 prints 20 mg/kg with notes 111 and 44. Note 111 specifies 400 mg/kg for that specific use. Confirm identity, process and destination. |

For packaged dried glucose syrup, above 40 mg/kg of sulphur dioxide by mass, Schedule II, 1(2), item 2 requires a rectangular box stating **DRIED GLUCOSE SYRUP FOR USE IN SUGAR CONFECTIONERY ONLY**. Exactly 40 mg/kg does not trigger this item. A lot above 40 mg/kg marked for another use requires review even when within the 150 mg/kg ceiling.

Choose the grade and intended use in the product worksheet. For a numerical comparison, enter the sample reference and laboratory report and confirm the residual SO₂ basis. An ND or qualified laboratory result requires individual interpretation. To retain the assessment, select **Save worksheet on this device**. The product-specific download includes the selected form and entered result. The catalogue-wide download lists the reference conditions for every reviewed form.

These checks address food additives and the dried glucose syrup pack declaration. Finished-product contaminant, toxin and residue coverage stands at 517 partial or conditional sources and 16 open products.
