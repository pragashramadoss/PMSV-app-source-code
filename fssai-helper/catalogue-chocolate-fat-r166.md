# Vegetable fat in chocolate

Reviewed 27 November 2026 for chocolate under FPSFA 2.7.4. The worksheet records the recipe or batch, the mass basis for the calculation and the supplier specification. Use the individual chocolate export to retain those details.

| Recipe check | How to apply it |
| --- | --- |
| Quantity | Divide the combined mass of added vegetable fats other than cocoa butter by the finished mass after deducting other added edible foodstuffs under 2.7.4(1). Document the ingredient and amount behind each deduction. The maximum is 5%. |
| Example | A 100 kg finished recipe with 20 kg of documented deductible inclusions has an 80 kg calculation basis. Exactly 4 kg is 5%. At 4.01 kg the result is 5.0125%, above the limit. |
| Material | Check the applicable FSSAI fat standard and the evidence for all three conditions in 2.7.4(5). Verify a non-lauric fat rich in symmetrical POP, POSt and StOSt triglycerides, miscibility in all proportions with cocoa butter and compatible physical properties, and refining or fractionation without enzymatic modification of the triglycerides. A trade designation such as CBS or CBR leaves the material review open. |
| Cocoa materials | Verify the minima for the declared chocolate type. Added vegetable fat cannot replace cocoa material required to meet a minimum. |
| Artwork | When the chocolate contains added vegetable fat other than cocoa butter, the prescribed bold declaration is **CONTAINS COCOA BUTTER EQUIVALENT/VEGETABLE FAT IN ADDITION TO COCOA BUTTER**. |

The chocolate provision applies to the finished food under 2.7.4. Cocoa mass, cocoa liquor and cocoa cake have their own fat restriction in 2.7.8(c).

## Correction to the preserved spice paste worksheet

R165 incorrectly flagged Listeria monocytogenes in Table 3B. For wet-ground (paste), preserved or pickled spices, the official table lists Salmonella, sulphite-reducing clostridia and Bacillus cereus. The worksheet now includes those three food-safety criteria and the four Table 3A process-hygiene criteria. This correction applies to this named category.
