## Source-level result

The original 137-case register has **seven** Category 99 catalogue entries whose historical FSSR field is blank. All **7/7** can be assigned a source-confirmed **FoSCoS umbrella category** (99.1, 99.2, 99.3 or 99.7), but **0/7** have an actual identifiable manufacturer/SKU-specific permission, current FSSR clause and CTR article legally decided. ### Legal-scope distinctions

1. **Tartaric-acid label:** "L(+/-)" must not become a guessed INS/CAS or isomer. Identify the exact actual substance, grade and permitted end-use before selecting a purity/contaminant provision.
2. **Nutraceutical additives VA–VF and botanicals Schedules IV/VI/VIII:** these are historical category labels, not proof that the cited schedule numbers, permissions or limits remain unchanged in later legally effective directions. Verify the actual notified schedule edition and ingredient.
3. **Sajji Khar:** confirm real chemical composition; no blind transfer from sodium carbonate or a generic alkali designation.
4. **Enzymes:** FoSCoS 99.2 is **not interchangeable** with **99.4 Processing Aids**. Collect the enzyme commission number, production organism/part, manufacturing source, technological purpose, carrier and residual-safety evidence.
5. **Flavouring premixes:** FPSFA **3.3.1** prohibits specified flavouring agents in foods and restricts supporting additives and solvent use. The official **Chapter 3 Version 3 (7 May 2025), page 87**, was visually checked. A generic category 99.3 label cannot bypass substance-level prohibition or permitted use.
6. **Functional premixes:** verify **each component**, the exact functional route, permitted level and grade, not just the word premix.

The official FPSFA compendium itself states it is a reference and original Gazette notifications/amendments prevail. No full current-law reconciliation, real manufacturer evidence, test methods, sample results or label checks is claimed in R54.
