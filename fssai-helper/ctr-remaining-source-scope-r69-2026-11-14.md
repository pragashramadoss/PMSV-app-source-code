# PMSV R69 — remaining source-scope checks

14 November 2026. Seventy catalogue identities received a fresh source-wording and scope review. Together with the selected earlier rounds, all 137 queue identities now have a linked focused review. **All 137 exact CTR article decisions remain OPEN.** This is source coverage, not full legal verification of 533 products.

The source bytes were retrieved from FSSAI; URLs and SHA-256 values are in the accompanying JSON. Scanned Ayurveda pages and FPC18 clauses were inspected as images. No numerical rule was activated and no product or lot was approved.

## New findings

- The previously unavailable Ayurveda images are now verified: recipes 14.6 and 14.7 really do have the title/method discrepancy recorded in R53. It cannot be resolved by swapping titles. Method-only Karpura/Kasturi and flower identity remain open; the 14 ingredient CTR gaps remain open.
- The original milk-logo graphic notification commences on 1 July 2028. Its four size rows are recorded as future source information, with the literal boundary wording retained. This does not settle earlier logo obligations.
- FPC18 requires paragraph 3 eligibility, Annexure II additive mapping, base-food microbiology and base-food toxin checks. Starch sweets have a category-tree/Annexure II entry but no separately titled Annexure I descriptor.
- Arrowroot does not inherit the adjacent tapioca-sago table; vending water does not inherit packaged-water limits; the two papad placements use the same standard.
- Soy protein classes have strict boundaries; soy curd form/culture and malt-extract grade change the source criteria. Legacy dextrin claims and the printed groundnut-flour acid-value unit remain explicit reconciliation questions.
