# PMSV R67 — Thermally processed curried vegetables / RTE vegetables

**11 November 2026. Source-only evidence; zero finished-product legal PASS or commercial releases.**

## Source verified

The official [FSSAI Chapter 2.3 Version 3 (07 May 2025)](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf) prints **2.3.4** across **PDF pages 10–11 (zero-based 9–10)**. Both page images were visually checked. The existing original R29/R50 catalogue record `04-04-2-thermally-processed-curried-vegetables-ready-to-eat-vegetables` is FPC **04.2.2.8**, FSSR **2.3.4**, original P2; it is one distinct previously unresolved CTR article applicability case.

The source describes products from **fresh, dehydrated, frozen or previously processed vegetables, legumes, cereals or pulses**, prepared singly or in combination. Salt, nutritive sweeteners, spices/condiments, vegetable oils/fats and **milk fat** are potential ingredients. Heat processing before or after packing must prevent spoilage. It separately requires:
- Permitted additives only under applicable regulations and appendices;
- **Appendix B microbiological compliance**;
- **Rigid container fill ≥90% of water capacity**, water capacity measured using distilled water at **20°C**.

**Critically, 2.3.4 itself does not print an independent minimum TSS, pH, or drained-weight percentage.** The adjacent **2.3.5 vegetable soup** text on PDF p.11 has TSS ≥5%, tomato soup ≥7%; **do not transfer these to 2.3.4**. Likewise the preceding **2.3.3** liquid/sauce/solid vegetable drained-weight table does **not** automatically govern 2.3.4.

## Actual-product evidence holds

The 90% fill rule does not establish commercial sterility or validated process lethality. A real manufacturer needs product formulation and acid status, pack/container and closure integrity, thermal-process authority/validation and microbiological safety, particularly for shelf-stable low-acid RTE foods. Presence of milk fat is possible; **do not label all curried vegetables milk-free or vegan without recipe evidence**. The actual presence of legumes/cereals/pulses affects classification and contaminant article selection.

**No exact finished-food CTR metal, toxin, pesticide, microbiological or additive matrix, original Gazette/amendment reconciliation, real lot results or competent independent regulatory decision is available in the source register.** The FSSAI compendium is reference-only; original effective Gazettes/amendments prevail.
