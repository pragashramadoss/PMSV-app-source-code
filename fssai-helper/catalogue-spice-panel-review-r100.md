# R100 spice and culinary herb decisions and pesticide report lines

Reviewed on 21 November 2026. Thirty-six marketed catalogue products have a single-spice or culinary-herb sample choice. Each of the 533 worksheets can also record a physically separate spice ingredient. The browser retains multiple pesticide results by product, sample, report and pesticide.

## Selecting a source

| Evidence available | Decision in the worksheet |
|---|---|
| The Indian table names the pesticide and spice | Paragraph 1(a). The worksheet displays the printed limit, analytical conditions and Gazette page. A possible broader article or unresolved residue definition requires interpretation. |
| No applicable Indian provision, registered pesticide, FSSAI MRL established for another food, applicable adopted Codex MRL located | Paragraph 1(b). The reviewer records the Codex article, residue definition, reference, qualifier and value. That value is explicitly labelled as reviewer-entered in the worksheet and exports. |
| Same registration and other-food evidence, with a documented search finding no applicable Codex MRL | Paragraph 1(b). The comparison uses 0.1 mg/kg. |
| No applicable Indian provision and the pesticide is confirmed unregistered with CIB&RC | Paragraph 1(c). The comparison uses 0.1 mg/kg after the Indian-article and registration evidence is recorded. |
| Registration unknown, other-food condition unresolved, unsettled source, or incomplete Codex search in the registered branch | The record shows the outstanding check. |
| Formulated food, unidentified process, extract, heated or chemically treated product | Establish the applicable finished-food provision independently. A separately tested spice ingredient can have its own record. |

The 8 April 2024 order replaces the 2022 orders annexed to its PDF. An unsuccessful search of registrations does not establish unregistered status. An extraneous limit, proposed Codex value or foreign national limit needs an independent legal basis. The comparison concerns the measured residue. Pesticide-use permissions and finished-product release require separate decisions. ## Matching the actual spice

The 36 sample choices include 32 with Codex lookup codes. Examples include coriander seed HS 0779, cinnamon bark HS 0777, dried basil leaf DH 0722 and dried oregano DH 5271. The Codex database asks users to check individual commodities as well as corresponding subgroups and groups. The search must cover the tested plant part and form. The lookup codes alone do not establish that a Codex limit exists for a pesticide. [Codex database instructions](https://codex.fao.org/codex-texts/codex-online-databases/pesticides-residues) · [Codex commodity hierarchy](https://codex.fao.org/codex-texts/codex-online-databases/pesticides-residues/commodity-categories) · [FSSAI chapter 2.9](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_9_Salt_Spices_Condiments%20and%20related%20products.pdf)

For Metalaxyl-M the Indian Gazette gives 0.5 mg/kg for black pepper, 0.02 mg/kg for chilli and 0.2 mg/kg for dried chilli. The black-pepper row is available only for that pepper variant. Mustard seed used as a condiment and dried mint can also raise alternative Indian article wording. The worksheet displays the relevant source pages when that wording needs review. [FSSAI Gazette, serial 120, p. 43](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_MRL_Pesticides_03_01_2019.pdf#page=43)

The fallback routes require a dated Indian source check and registration evidence. Known copper cross-references, dithiocarbamate definitions, chemical-name discrepancies and documented restrictions can require review. Ethylene oxide and its marker are referred to the specific FSSAI decision. The March 2025 government list supplies restriction alerts. Review later decisions for the assessment date. [43rd Food Authority meeting, item 3.3](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/43rd%20FA%20Minutes.pdf#page=8) · [Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Annexure I](https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/267/AU2425_WnccM8.pdf?source=pqars)

## Recording a laboratory report

1. Open a product worksheet and its pesticide section. Choose the named source assessment or the spice order.
2. Select the sampled commodity, process and pesticide. Enter the lot and report references and the result in mg/kg. For a less-than report, enter the reported threshold. A threshold above the limit leaves the comparison inconclusive.
3. Choose **Save this result to the panel** or **Save spice result to the panel**. Add the next pesticide from the report. Saving the same sample article, role, lot reference, report reference and pesticide again updates that line. Separate lots and reports retain separate entries.
4. Use **Export these results as CSV** for one row per result, or export the product brief or full register. The 114-column register keeps the first 107 R99 columns and adds seven fields. Its blank version contains no laboratory data.

The panel uses local browser storage. When local saving is blocked, the worksheet keeps the result in the current session and offers the CSV export. R99 worksheet entries saved under the same browser origin remain available as the active assessment. To add one to the panel, open the saved result and use the panel save button.
