# Hingu in Krishara khichdi

**Review 80 · 18 November 2026**

Recipe 13.1, ingredient 5, gives Hingu as *Asfoetida/ Ferula asfoetida Regel.*, with oleoresin gum as the part and Q.S. as the quantity. The recipe adds Hingu during preparation. Its quantity and cooking instructions do not establish a contaminant limit in the finished khichdi. [FSSAI recipe order, PDF p. 88 / printed p. 53](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf#page=88)

## Identifying the supplied spice

The Spices Board Act includes asafoetida at Schedule entry 34. The Board’s commodity profile identifies it as the dried exudate from the underground parts of *Ferula*. FSSAI 2.9.29 defines Hing and Hingra as natural root or rhizome gum resin and gives compounded asafoetida a separate composition that includes a carrier. The version 4 compendium dated 1 August 2025 reproduces all 11 grade-specific purity limits from the original notification checked in R76. A supplier’s declared grade, botanical identity, part, drying and composition are needed to distinguish unblended resin from root powder, a solvent extract, essential oil or compounded Hing. ## Interpreting laboratory reports

The FSSAI order of 22 June 2021 directed laboratories to use the approved revised spices manual immediately and superseded the 2016 manual. It provides for later method updates. The five methods below are printed in the 2021 edition. The separate Indian Standard supplies a starch method specific to asafoetida. [FSSAI order and manual](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual_Revised_Spices_Herbs_22_06_2021.pdf#page=1) · [FSSAI methods directory](https://fssai.gov.in/food-testing/manuals-methods-analysis)

| Parameter | Reference | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total ash | FSSAI 10.006:2021, PDF pp. 24–26 | Reports mass percentage. Moisture-free conversion is a separate step. |
| Acid-insoluble ash | FSSAI 10.007:2021, PDF pp. 27–28 | Also distinguishes a mass percentage from an optional moisture-free result. |
| Alcoholic extract | FSSAI 10.010:2021, PDF pp. 32–33 | The asafoetida note specifies about 2 g for natural resin and 10 g for compounded material with 90% v/v alcohol. Its calculation is **100 − (residue % + moisture %)** by mass. The same page also prints a general dry-basis spice calculation. |
| Galbanum, ammoniacum and other foreign resins | FSSAI 10.034:2021, PDF pp. 85–86 | Qualitative tests with different observations for natural and compounded material. The method sheet prints 10.034, while the contents page prints 10.34. |
| Colophony | FSSAI 10.035:2021, PDF pp. 87–88 | Qualitative thin-layer chromatography with abietic acid as the marker. |
| Added starch | IS 7807:1975, clause 8, PDF p. 12 | Microscopy indicates added starch. The standard then uses enzymatic estimation with diastase. Hing and Hingra have a 1% by-weight maximum under 2.9.29. The compounded grade has no numeric starch maximum in that clause. |

For alcoholic extract, IS 7807 clauses 6.1–6.2 use the same residue-and-moisture expression. Amendment 1 of August 1976 adds completion of extraction as an endpoint to clause 6.1. The product standard expressly cites U.S.P. 1936. Matching the numerical calculation does not establish full equivalence to that edition. Keep the laboratory’s method designation and reporting basis with its result. The manual’s turmeric starch method has a different matrix.
