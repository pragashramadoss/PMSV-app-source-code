# Aflatoxins in Singhara flour

Reviewed 25 November 2026. FPSFA 2.3.64 defines Singhara flour by milling dried nuts of *Trapa bispinosa* or *Trapa quadrispinosa*. The contaminants table names nuts and foods containing nuts. Its wording does not expressly assign the finished flour to either category. The Helper provides a conditional assessment for flour that retains the ground nuts. A different species, a blended flour or an extracted starch needs its own classification.

| Documented sample or decision | Total aflatoxins | Aflatoxin B1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Separately sampled incoming nuts for further processing | 15 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |
| Finished flour with a documented basis for using the nuts-for-further-processing entry | 15 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |
| Finished flour with a documented basis for using the food-containing-nuts entry | 20 µg/kg | 10 µg/kg |
| Finished flour for further cooking, with the two entries established as the only candidates and their selection unresolved | Hold comparison pending classification | 10 µg/kg, shared by both entries |

Record the sample lot and the specification showing the species and milling process. For a finished-flour classification, enter the decision reference. A laboratory result also requires the matching sample role, lot, report, unit and analytical basis. An intake report cannot be used as the finished-lot measurement. For example, a total result of 18 µg/kg requires a classification decision before it can be compared with 15 or 20 µg/kg.

The amendment taking effect on 1 December 2026 changes the aflatoxin entries for oils and oilseeds. The cited nut and food-containing entries give the same limits in the two reviewed editions. This review establishes the first conditional finished-product source for Singhara flour. The register now has 518 partial or conditional sources, 15 products pending a first source and no full product sign-offs.
