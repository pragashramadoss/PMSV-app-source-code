# R75 — IS 10500 source concordance

Reviewed 17 November 2026. The base IS 10500:2012 document and all four amendments were downloaded from the public BIS standard-detail service. This resolves R74's missing full-text access. Tables 1–6 and the relevant requirements/sampling clauses have been reconciled. Referenced laboratory-method documents and complete FSSAI applicability have not been independently reconciled.

All 137 product-specific CTR decisions remain open. All 14 Ayurveda ingredient applicability gaps remain open; four now have a much fuller water-standard reference. No finished-food limit or water-quality approval is inferred.

The BIS metadata labels amendment 3 as 2020, whereas the actual document heading is February 2021. R74's metadata inventory is historical; this follow-up uses document headings. These headings are not presented as independently verified Gazette commencement dates.

## Interpretation retained

- **Acceptable and conditional upper values:** The higher permissible column is conditional on absence of an alternative source. Colour and sulphate add further conditions. It is not a routine substitute for the acceptable reference.
- **Iron–manganese interpretation remains open:** Amendment 1 changes the iron cell from 0.3 to 1.0 mg/L. The original Fe + Mn combined 0.3 mg/L remarks remain in the retrieved text; none of amendments 1–4 removes them. Both are retained for clarification.
- **Residual chlorine is a minimum:** The chlorinated-water row specifies a consumer-end minimum and a separate viral-protection minimum. Its second-column value is preserved as printed; the helper does not turn it into a general maximum.
- **Uranium is separate from alpha/beta activity:** Amendment 3 adds uranium to Table 3 at 0.03 mg/L without relaxation. Amendment 4 changes its method options. It is not automatically removed by the ingredient-water FAQ radioactive-parameter wording; that applicability question requires confirmation.
- **Methods and sample volumes matter:** Amendments 2–3 update bacteriology to IS 15185 and distinguish operative MS2 requirements from guidance clauses. The eight microbial/biological entries retain 100 mL, 1 L and 10 L sample bases and their water-system scopes.

The iron change does not authorize silently deleting the combined iron–manganese remark. The chlorine row is not converted from a minimum into a maximum. Qualitative odour/taste checks are not replaced with zero. The pesticide unit is µg/L, chemical concentration is generally mg/L, and radioactive activity is Bq/L. No conversion to food-commodity mg/kg limits is made.

Sampling remains tied to the prescribed method. Amendment 3's Annex A entry prints Part 65 alongside a Part 2 title fragment; retain that inconsistency for method-document verification rather than silently correcting the source. Guidance clauses 4.2.1–4.2.6, 4.3.1–4.3.7 and 4.3.10 are distinguished from the specified MS2/protozoan requirements.

The June 2025 FSSAI vending-water replacement retrieved in R74 remains a draft source in this implementation. This BIS comparison does not establish a final FSSAI replacement, general applicability to packaged/mineral water, a blanket FAQ exemption or product release.

## Interface and verification

The five existing water-related product pages now offer a searchable 74-entry reference. Filters change the displayed entries only; the complete review export retains all 74. The source reference has no PASS button and makes no analytical comparison or release decision. The R74 supplier-document checklist remains separate.

Automated checks cover amendment precedence, the iron/manganese hold, chlorine direction, uranium method options, pesticide units, all eight microbial sample bases, complete exports, source-link integrity and refusal of altered approval/completeness flags. Browser verification is recorded at the tested revision in draft PR #2.
