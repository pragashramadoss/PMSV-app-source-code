# PMSV FSSAI Helper — live source-proof interface for 121 products (11 October 2026)

Scope: GitHub gh-pages preview only. WordPress unchanged.

All 121 products in the first-source regulatory gap queue now have a collapsible product-specific research panel in the Helper contaminant screen. The 29 previously unpaired identities also display their additional subtype, species, process or composition proof requirements. Other products see reference-only names of official candidate articles, never auto-applied numerical maximums.

The panel fails closed if the selected product identity, FoSCoS category, FSSR clause, manifest counts, no-PASS status, official FSSAI reference table or stored product dossier is altered. A missing or mismatched dataset displays a withheld warning rather than any numeric or legal conclusion.

Stats are unchanged: 533 catalogue products, 412 with partial exact evidence, 121 still awaiting first exact named product-source evidence, 0 full compliance approvals and 0 new first-source promotions.
