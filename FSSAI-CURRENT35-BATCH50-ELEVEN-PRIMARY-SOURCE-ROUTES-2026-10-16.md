### Specific primary evidence

- **Children's formulated supplements**: 2018 FSSAI original Gazette and the current FoSCoS category 13.2 refer to a *specific standard* for children above 24 months up to 36 months. Older infants, broader FSDU and general supplements require differentiated legal treatment; no infant lead limit transferred. The original Gazette was retrieved as official indexed content, not fully page-image inspected in this run.
- **Trehalose**, FSSR 3.3.4: official FSSAI compiled chapter 3 PDF page 90 (zero-based page 89) **visually inspected**; maximum *total ash* **0.05%** and LOD **1.5%**. Total ash ≠ sulphated ash and neither gives a CTR lead maximum. The recipe standard authorizes conditional uses, not a tested commercial batch.
- **Sajji Khar**, 3 June 2026 FSSAI NSF&FI official 75-page register, pdf page 52 (zero based page 51) **visually inspected**: rows 44, 45 and 51 within *closed/withdrawn applications* section. The names of applications and withdrawn outcomes do **not** establish every Sajji Khar is prohibited, approved, or has a legal lead maximum.
- **Ayurveda Aahara 14.3 Narikela Ksheerika**, FSSAI 25 July 2025 order *official indexed original text*: cow milk (1 part) + coconut endosperm Q.S., cooked/reduced. The indexed source supplies named recipe facts, but the original PDF page screenshot was not retrievable. No claim of an actual marketed formulation or ordinary milk CTR lead maximum.
- **Ayurveda Aahara 14.7 Ksheerapaka**, same official order indexed text: buffalo milk (1 part), aromatic flowers, water and sugar Q.S., infusion and reduced cooking. No ordinary fluid milk legal limit assigned from recipe name.
- **Polyols, food additives, nutritional schedule additives, enzymes and functional-ingredient premixes**: licensed category names and regulation framework are not an individual chemical standard, purity monograph, ingredient composition, toxin crop source or finished-commodity CTR article. Source records include exact evidence requested.
