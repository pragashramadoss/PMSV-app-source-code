Important Proprietary Food distinction

Regulation 2.12
Route follows the finished formulation. If the formulation remains within the confirmed standardized product and its applicable permitted ingredients/additives, the standardized route remains applicable. An additional ingredient outside that standardized formulation routes the finished product to Proprietary Food, provided the ingredient itself has the required FSSAI/IFCT basis. A mere deviation only in a quality parameter of a standardized food is not treated as a Proprietary Food route. PMSV does not borrow another product’s permission.

First: what does “500 ml per day” mean?

Important before reading the limits
500 ml means the total quantity of the finished Caffeinated Beverage consumed in one day. It does not mean 500 ml of taurine, caffeine or any other ingredient. FSSAI requires the label declaration “consume not more than 500 ml per day” for the caffeinated beverage.
Example: 250 ml packTwo 250 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Example: 125 ml packFour 125 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Why the calculator converts mg/day to mg/L: FSSAI gives taurine, D-glucurono-γ-lactone, inositol and pantothenic acid as maximum amounts per day. To help formulation, the calculator divides the FSSAI daily maximum by 0.5 L of finished caffeinated beverage to show the equivalent concentration. The FSSAI regulatory limit remains the original mg/day value.

Official regulatory sources used

Strict source policy: regulatory evidence in this helper is restricted to official FSSAI/FoSCoS sources. Social media (including Instagram), Scribd, news sites, blogs, commercial sites, Wikipedia and other third-party pages are not accepted as evidence. If an official current provision cannot be verified, PMSV leaves it locked rather than substituting another website.
