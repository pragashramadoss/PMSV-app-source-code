What defines this product?

Core standard
WaterWater used must conform to the packaged drinking water standard or mineral water standard referenced by FSSAI.
Total caffeineMinimum 145 mg/L · Maximum 300 mg/L, calculated from all caffeine sources in the formulation.
Carbonated versionFoSCoS maps Carbonated Caffeinated Beverage to food category 14.1.4.1.
Other ingredients / additivesFor ingredients, flavours, sweeteners, food additives, contaminants and microbiology, the caffeinated beverage standard directs the product to comply with the carbonated-water requirements.
Important: the carbonated-water base standard itself says caffeine up to 145 ppm, but the specific caffeinated-beverage standard separately requires 145–300 mg/L total caffeine. The specific caffeinated-beverage provision is therefore the relevant caffeine rule for this product.

Special label points

Product-specific
High caffeine declarationDeclare the caffeine amount as “High Caffeine: X mg/serving size”, where X is caffeine in the pack/serve.
Caution statementProminently state that the product is not recommended for children, pregnant or lactating women, and persons sensitive to caffeine.
Daily-consumption declarationThe standard links the daily-consumption declaration to the maximum amounts of optional functional substances and provides a 500 ml/day example framework.
Non-caloric sweetenersProducts containing aspartame, acesulfame-K or other non-caloric sweeteners must also satisfy the applicable special labelling provisions under current Labelling and Display rules.
