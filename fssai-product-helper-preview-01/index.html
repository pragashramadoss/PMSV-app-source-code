Important Proprietary Food distinction

Regulation 2.12
Adding an extra ingredient does not automatically make the product Proprietary Food. Regulation 2.12.1(1) covers food that has not been standardized, while a mere deviation in the quality parameters of a standardized food does not qualify it as proprietary. Regulation 2.12.1(2) restricts non-additive ingredients in proprietary foods to the permitted categories stated there. If the product continues to be assessed as a standardized Caffeinated Beverage, clause 2.10.6(2)(III)(A) separately requires Food Authority approval for an additional item/ingredient outside the specified list. PMSV verifies regulatory conclusions only from FSSAI regulations; it does not independently verify IFCT listings.

First: what does “500 ml per day” mean?

Important before reading the limits
500 ml means the total quantity of the finished Caffeinated Beverage consumed in one day. It does not mean 500 ml of taurine, caffeine or any other ingredient. FSSAI requires the label declaration “consume not more than 500 ml per day” for the caffeinated beverage.
Example: 250 ml packTwo 250 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Example: 125 ml packFour 125 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Why the calculator converts mg/day to mg/L: FSSAI gives taurine, D-glucurono-γ-lactone, inositol and pantothenic acid as maximum amounts per day. To help formulation, the calculator divides the FSSAI daily maximum by 0.5 L of finished caffeinated beverage to show the equivalent concentration. The FSSAI regulatory limit remains the original mg/day value.

Mandatory label requirements — pre-packaged food

FSSAI Labelling & Display Regulations
1. Name of the food — Front of PackUse the name that indicates the true nature of the food. If this formulation is classified under the standardized product, use Caffeinated Beverage. A brand/fancy name may be additional, not a substitute. Reg. 5(1)
2. List of ingredientsDeclare “Ingredients” / “List of Ingredients” and list ingredients in descending order by weight or volume at manufacture. Added water and compound ingredients must be handled as required. Quantitative ingredient declaration applies where an ingredient is emphasized or characterises the food. Reg. 5(2)
3. Nutrition informationFor this beverage, declare nutrition per 100 ml or single-consumption pack and the applicable per-serve % contribution to RDA. Include energy, protein, carbohydrate, total sugars, added sugars, total fat, saturated fat, trans fat, cholesterol where applicable, and sodium. Also declare serving size and number of servings per pack. Reg. 5(3)
4. Veg / Non-Veg declarationDisplay the applicable prescribed symbol on the principal display panel close to the product/brand name, unless a specific exemption legally applies to the finished product. The decision must consider ingredients, additives and processing aids, not only the main raw materials. Reg. 5(4)
5. Food additivesFor additives present in the finished food, declare the functional class together with the specific name or recognised INS number in the ingredient list. Flavouring agents require the applicable flavour declaration. Reg. 5(5)
6. Brand owner / manufacturer detailsDeclare the name and complete address with the applicable qualifier such as Manufactured by, Marketed by, Manufactured & Marketed by, or Packed & Marketed by. Imported foods require importer information as applicable. Reg. 5(6)
7. FSSAI logo & licence numberDisplay the FSSAI logo and brand-owner licence number in contrast colour. Where the manufacturer / marketer / packer / bottler differs from the brand owner, its licence number must also be displayed as required. Reg. 5(7)
8. Net quantity, Retail Sale Price & Consumer CareDeclare these in the manner prescribed under the Legal Metrology framework referenced by the FSSAI Labelling & Display Regulations. Reg. 5(8)
9. Lot / Code / BatchA batch number, code number or lot number must be declared. Reg. 5(9)
10. Date markingDeclare Date of manufacture or packaging and Expiry / Use by; Best Before may additionally be given. Manufacture/packing date and Expiry/Use by must be grouped together at one place. Storage conditions must be stated where shelf life depends on them. Reg. 5(10)
11. Country of origin — if importedImported food must carry country of origin and comply with the additional requirements of the FSSAI Import Regulations. Reg. 5(11)–5(12)
12. Instructions for use / storageGive directions needed for proper use or health and safety, such as storage after opening, where applicable. Reg. 5(13)
13. Allergen declarationApplicable allergens must be declared separately in the prescribed “Contains …” format. Cross-contact allergens may be declared as “May Contains …” where applicable. Reg. 5(14)
