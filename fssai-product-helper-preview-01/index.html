Other permitted ingredients / limitsFSSAI clause 2.10.6(2)(IV) makes the applicable ingredient, flavour, sweetener, additive, contaminant and microbiological requirements of 2.10.6(1) part of this product standard.
How PMSV applies the rule: for a Caffeinated Beverage, use the product-specific caffeine requirement in 2.10.6(2)(II): 145–300 mg/L. The cross-reference in 2.10.6(2)(IV) is used to bring in the other applicable ingredient, sweetener, additive, contaminant and microbiological requirements.

First: what does “500 ml per day” mean?

Important before reading the limits
500 ml means the total quantity of the finished Caffeinated Beverage consumed in one day. It does not mean 500 ml of taurine, caffeine or any other ingredient. FSSAI requires the label declaration “consume not more than 500 ml per day” for the caffeinated beverage.
Example: 250 ml packTwo 250 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Example: 125 ml packFour 125 ml packs = 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage in one day.
Why The calculator converts mg/day to mg/L: FSSAI gives taurine, D-glucurono-γ-lactone, inositol and pantothenic acid as maximum amounts per day. To help formulation, PMSV divides the FSSAI daily maximum by 0.5 L of finished caffeinated beverage to show the equivalent concentration. The FSSAI regulatory limit remains the original mg/day value.

Example: FSSAI maximum taurine = 2,000 mg/day. If 500 ml of the finished caffeinated beverage is the daily quantity, the equivalent concentration is 2,000 mg ÷ 0.5 L = 4,000 mg/L. * ppm is calculated as mg/kg = mg/L ÷ density (kg/L). These converted values help formulation; the regulatory limit itself remains the FSSAI mg/day value.

Other ingredients FSSAI permits for this product

Applied through clause 2.10.6(2)(IV)
Sugar / carbohydrate sweetenersSugar, liquid glucose, dextrose monohydrate, invert sugar, fructose and honey are listed as permitted ingredients. No separate numerical maximum is stated for these ingredients in this clause.
Fruit/vegetable extractives, flavours and coloursPermitted through the applicable cross-referenced requirement. Where a separate additive rule sets a limit or condition, that rule must also be met.
AcidsCitric, fumaric, tartaric, phosphoric, lactic, ascorbic and malic acid are listed. PMSV does not assign a numerical maximum unless FSSAI gives one in an applicable provision.
Preservatives / emulsifiers / stabilisers / gumsPermitted items must comply with the applicable FSSAI additive permissions and limits. The product-standard clause itself does not give one common numerical maximum for this whole group.
Mineral salts / other listed ingredientsSalts of sodium, calcium and magnesium, vitamins, licorice and its derivatives are listed as permitted ingredients subject to applicable FSSAI requirements.
