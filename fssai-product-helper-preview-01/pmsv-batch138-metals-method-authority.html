What the two original manuals actually establish

The FSSAI-hosted Trace Elements manual (61-page PDF) and Metals and Minerals manual (52-page PDF) assign different analytical procedures to the same identifiers FSSAI 09.001:2024 through FSSAI 09.007:2024. Their actual tables of contents have been separately checked.

Method identity HOLD: Never use a bare "FSSAI 09.xxx:2024" as a complete instruction. An acceptable laboratory proposal needs the source PDF and full method title, operative adoption or applicability decision, specific analyte/matrix, lab accreditation scope, demonstrated recovery/precision and the actual laboratory LOQ. Product legal limits and commercial release also require separate signed decisions.
Illustrative performance mismatch — not product limits: for identifier 09.001:2024, the Trace Elements procedure describes approximately 0.4 mg/kg Pb capability with exclusion of ≥40% fat foods and milk powder. The separate Metals and Minerals ICP-OES procedure lists Pb LOQ of 6 mg/kg. These are different procedure-level figures, neither is the specific lab's verified LOQ nor proof of fitness for a case.
