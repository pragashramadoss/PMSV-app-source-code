## Two newly matched official product-subtypes (not lot approvals)

FSSR Chapter 2.5 §2.5.3(2) **Frozen egg products** and §2.5.3(4) **Liquid egg products** distinguish whole hen's eggs from isolated yolk and albumen. The distinct whole-egg subroute consists of all edible contents removed from shell, subsequently pasteurized/frozen or preserved. Official FSSR: https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/6_%20Chapter%202_5%20%28Meat%20and%20Meat%20products%29.pdf

FSSAI Contaminants Version IX 03.02.2026, §2.3.1 serial **13**, “sum of benomyl and carbendazim expressed as carbendazim”: **Eggs, 0.1 mg/kg (shell free basis)**. Official CTR: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf

These two source links are conditional named whole-egg pathways only. A manufacturer cannot assume that a cooked/pasteurized/frozen, separated, preserved or concentrated egg food meets the raw shell-free test basis. Processing effects, legal scope and assay data must be established separately before any legal numeric application.
