## Scope and anti-promotion principle

Ten FSSAI-hosted published chapter PDFs were **not** claimed. Instead, **six distinct FSSAI-hosted official compiled chapter PDFs** were opened and their exact relevant pages visually inspected, yielding **ten** *additional* exact published product-identity facts for previously unverified case identities. FSSR compiled chapters are editorial consolidation, not the controlling Gazetted law in case of conflict.

Published product-definition evidence is distinct from (1) actual product formulation/declared composition evidence, (2) the operative **CTR contaminant** article and numeric limit for the exact legal commodity, (3) an accredited test method with adequate LOQ and (4) signed CoA for the actual sampled lot. All remain **unapproved** in this batch.

### Important findings for an industrial reviewer

- **Cocoa mass/cake, 2.7.8**: actual source differentiates mechanically ground nib liquor from partially/fully de-fatted cake, and lists mass cocoa butter at **47–60%**. Do not presume a high-fat-excluding digest is in scope, or that cocoa powder is the same legal article.
- **Sweetened dry cocoa mixes, 2.7.6**: separate cocoa-powder proportions/categories and a 7% product moisture criterion; the product is not chemically identical to neat cocoa powder.
- **Chia seeds, 2.4.6(25)**: FSSAI botanical identity is *Salvia hispanica* (mint family); chapter placement alone does not establish cereal metal-comparator coverage.
- **Arrowroot, 2.4.14**: purified rhizome starch, not an undifferentiated cereal flour or a specific pesticide analyte.
- **Frozen fruits, 2.3.37**: specific fruit species and named pesticide must be identified before an individual MRL or processed commodity interpretation.
- **Animal casings, 2.5.2(14)**: gut, bladder and oesophagus, not ordinary muscle meat.
- **Egg powder, 2.5.3(3)**: whole/yolk/white fractions, dehydrated; no fresh egg pesticide MRL is auto-assigned.
- **Pickled eggs, 2.5.3(5)**: hard-boiled egg and pickling solution (hens or quail), not interchangeable with fresh shell-free eggs.
- **Salt substitutes, 2.9.30(6)**: sodium content **≤120 mg/100 g** is an actual *product-composition* standard; NOT a lead maximum.
- **Royal jelly, 2.8.3(3)**: additive-free worker-bee gland secretion, distinct from honey, beeswax and meat. Royal jelly antibiotic substances and legal residue comparators need specific confirmation.
