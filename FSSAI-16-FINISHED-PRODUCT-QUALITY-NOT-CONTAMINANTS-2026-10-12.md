Critical findings:
- Fungi extract §2.3.62: 7% saltless extract versus fungi concentrate 24%; dried fungi concentrate has 9% water and 5% salt maxima, unlike liquid concentrate salt max 20%. These are distinct product forms and do not inherit *canned mushrooms* contaminants.
- Fruit cheese §2.3.33 minimum 65% TSS and generally 45% original prepared fruit (strawberry/raspberry exception 25%). Fruit bar/toffee §2.3.19 max 20% moisture and min 75% TSS, 25% fruit.
- Date paste §2.3.56 max 20% moisture, 1.2% total ash and 0.1% acid-insoluble ash; no additives.
- Candied/crystallized/glazed produce §2.3.26: at least 70% total sugar and reducing sugars at least 25% **of total sugar**, not of total product.
- Frozen fruit/veg §2.3.37/38 requires -18°C thermal-centre stabilized; frozen vegetables additionally peroxidase NEGATIVE. Frozen curried vegetables §2.3.39 has a distinct finished matrix.
- Frozen fried potato §2.3.60: at most 78% moisture, 1.5% FFA in oil as oleic acid, 0.5% burnt defects; -18°C centre after freezing; cross-section minimum 4 mm for strips cut on all four sides.
- Cocoa beans §2.3.54: max 8% moisture, 4% mouldy, 8% slaty, 2% insect-damaged and 4% germinated/flat by count; NOT cocoa-powder dry-fat-free lead limit.
- Ginger paste §2.3.28 TSS at least 3% salt-free basis; generic culinary paste/sauce at least 8%, demonstrating why finished subtypes cannot be transferred.
