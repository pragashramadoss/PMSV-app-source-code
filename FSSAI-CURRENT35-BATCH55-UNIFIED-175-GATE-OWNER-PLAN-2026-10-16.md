## Immediate evidence flow and dependencies

**Workstream A — prepare first (70 independently draftable requests):**
- **35 official method-source / adoption verification requests**, functional routing `FSSAI_QA_DIVISION_AND_LAB_DIRECTOR`. A role label names a required evidence authority or review function; it does not imply an actual FSSAI official has agreed to own the task. Request full method title, PDF date/version, notified or adopted authority, instrument method analyte, method-number collision resolution and current status.
- **35 actual marketed-product identity requests**, functional routing `MANUFACTURER_OR_SUPPLIER_QA`. Require signed label/formulation or controlled BOM reference, physical sample identity, correct finished state, crop/animal/ingredient source, fat/moisture, and as-sold or reconstructed basis as appropriate.

**Workstream B — conditional, after commercial identity proof (35 requests):**
- **35 legal CTR article/analyte rulings** by the `REGULATORY_AFFAIRS_LEGAL` function. Independently identify current enacted commodity article, named analyte, limit, units and conversion/reporting basis; document why any related comparator article is or is not legally applicable. No default limit may be applied merely because a similar article appears in the FSSAI compendium.

**Workstream C — conditional laboratory scope (35 requests):**
- **35 laboratory matrix method and LOQ validations**, technical routing `LABORATORY_TECHNICAL_MANAGER`. This requires the official method version, marketed sample identity and signed commodity/analyte/limit conclusion. Obtain accreditation and full matrix scope, LOQ below the lawfully applicable limit, interference/recovery evidence and signed report conditions.

**Workstream D — final lot adjudication (35 requests):**
- **35 traceable lots and independent QA/RA sign-offs**, routing `SITE_QA_AND_INDEPENDENT_REGULATORY_REVIEWER`. Actual representative CoA, chain-of-custody, results, uncertainty, sample basis and independent reviewer decision are needed before individual lot disposition. No automated PASS follows from completing a checklist or obtaining a product-standard chapter reference.

**Dependency nuance:** The 105 downstream *approval* gates still have unsent documentary request templates; evidence-seeking may be planned, but those approvals cannot be granted until upstream conditions are met. The first 70 are 'requests can be prepared' only, not authenticated documents.

## Ownership: role types versus actual commitments

The static source deliberately records **no named person, no agreed dates and no fictitious completed evidence**. A site/business coordinator must still nominate actual stakeholders, establish priorities and obtain firm dates. The planner offers only *optional browser-memory drafts* for proposed case-level contacts and proposed follow-up dates; filling these never changes approval or outreach status.

Case worklist filters cover all 35, P0, P1, seven unnamed-material cases, 28 published identity references, temporary assignments and unassigned cases. Owner-role selector exports either all **175 original requests** or one role's **35 requests**. A separate 35-case CSV export contains draft coordinator/date information if the user typed it. All exports are **user-initiated** and should be reviewed for confidentiality before distribution. The role request CSV has **blank assignee and date fields** and does **not** leak temporary coordinator names.

There is **no browser local/session storage, server-side state, background save, API submission, email, authority contact or WordPress modification** in the Batch55 planner. Unsaved drafts disappear on reload. Saving and restoring in a persistent controlled work management system would require separate authorization and design.

## Governance and legal-source boundaries

- [Current FSSAI-hosted CTR Compendium IX (3 February 2026)](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) is a review reference; original effective notified Gazette controls conflicts. A 'foods not specified' row is not automatically suitable for each food category, and related product comparators remain unsigned until legal classification.
- [FSSAI official methods index](https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-testing/manuals-methods-analysis) and prior draft/2024 metals manuals require actual adoption/version resolution and matrix-specific validation.
- [May 2026 original Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/272840.pdf) is effective **1 December 2026**, and the [2026 laboratory amendment Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/276298.pdf) indicates **1 April 2027** commencement. Neither future-effective change is silently applied as current 16 October law.
- [FSSAI non-specified foods applications portal](https://www.fssai.gov.in/standards/non-specified-food) indexes September 2026 application status; specific Sajji Khar applicant row and supplier identity still require re-verification. A June 2026 closed/withdrawn application is neither general permission nor general prohibition.
