## Result

Three **published finished-product identity / quality** standards were located, cross-checked against actual pages in the FSSAI PDFs, and pinned to the matching unresolved catalogue IDs. This extends the B29–B30 FSSR product-standard research from **10 to 13 distinct pending foods** and provides **20 new published quality criteria**. These are NOT new approvals of FSSAI contaminants, toxins and residues (CTR) applicability or actual laboratory tests.

The main official sources are [FSSR Chapter 2.3, Version 3, 07 May 2025, pages 105–106](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit_Vegetable_products.pdf#page=105) and [FSSR Chapter 2.4, Version 4, 07 May 2025, pages 25–26](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4_Cereals_and_Cereal_products.pdf#page=25).

### Interpretation safeguards

- **Chia impurity limits are nested**: the mineral and animal-origin ceilings restrict the total 1% extraneous matter; they are not additive allowances above that total.
- **Coconut powder** may have optional *sodium caseinate*, which has ingredient and allergen implications; the name “coconut milk” does not make it cow/buffalo dairy milk for contaminant applicability.
- **Water chestnut flour** is made from *Trapa* nuts. Classification as wheat flour or cereal grain solely because of another FCS position would be legally unsound.
- These FSSR entries establish directly published product standards. Whether a real as-sold article matches the specified standard is an **independent** ingredient/process verification question.
- The FSSAI CTR Version IX metal/toxin article applicability to these three foods remains **FIRST_EXACT_SOURCE_PENDING**. No published FSSR quality criterion has been misused as a CTR contaminant maximum.
- The prospective December 2026 CTR changes are **not** activated in October.
