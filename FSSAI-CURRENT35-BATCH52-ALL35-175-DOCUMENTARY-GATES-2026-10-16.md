## Reviewer safeguards

1. **Method hierarchy:** A draft consultation manual, FSSR quality specification, FoSCoS licensing category or original 2022 Appendix C enzyme listing cannot independently establish a 2026 current CTR statutory contaminant limit for another product. Distinguish instrument type, effective date and exact legal product article.
2. **Commercial proof:** Never infer real product formulation, moisture/fat basis, processed state, animal/botanical source or license from generic published commodity definitions. The seven materials require named INS/CAS/EC/supplier/schedule or BOM evidence as applicable.
3. **Laboratory proof:** A cited procedure or method number is not an accredited, matrix-validated, LOQ-suitable laboratory result. Maintain manual-number ambiguities and fat/matrix exclusions where previously flagged.
4. **Release:** No generic product standard, category assignment or CoA for another food releases a lot. Actual sample identity, chain-of-custody, results, uncertainty and independent reviewer sign-off are mandatory.
5. **Confidentiality:** This Batch52 display has read-only publicly sourced metadata and filter/search only; it does not collect/save customer BOMs, transmit supplier details or auto-clear legal gates. CSV is a **blank request template** and must not be confused with completed evidence.
6. **Traceability:** The underlying batch44 175 request IDs and B51/B50 catalogue IDs are conserved. The UI refuses to display new conclusions on digest/crosswalk mismatch. If a source version changes, make a new versioned review rather than silently repurpose the original legal gate.
