## Official positive applicability — conditional, never blanket

- FSSR Chapter 2.3 Version 2 (04.11.2024), **§2.3.62**, explicitly lists dried, powder, pickled, salted, fermented, quick-frozen and in-oil edible fungi. It does not establish that every edible fungus is a mushroom.
- FSSR **§2.3.43** explicitly permits pickles with mushrooms, but not every vegetable or mixed pickle contains them.
- Contaminants Regulations Version IX (03.02.2026), **§2.2 naturally occurring toxic substances, first table row**, names **Agaric acid, “Food containing mushrooms”, maximum 100 ppm**.
- Nine broadly named FoSCoS products have an identifiable **mushroom-containing finished-food subroute** with this named source; true species, composition, processing, analytical matrix and actual sold lot remain unknown and numeric limits must NOT be automatically assigned.

### Mandatory constraints

1. Positive species common and scientific identification plus content of actual manufactured lot is essential before any product-level legal limit may be assigned.
2. `100 ppm` is the legal article limit, **not an assay or compliance result**. No other metal, pesticide, Appendix A, microbiology, lab or other safety review is cleared.
3. Canned mushroom lead/tin articles are legally separate from Agaric acid and cannot transfer to jar, pouch or pickled/fermented products.
4. Full identity and sample form testing basis is required for powders, oil-packed foods, pickles and processed fungi.
