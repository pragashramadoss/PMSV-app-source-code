## Acceptance sequence required for real legal/testing approval
1. Actual manufacturer/SKU identity, controlled BOM and label, food form and sample basis obtained and authenticated.
2. Regulatory/legal owner identifies original notified operative Gazette article, exact contaminant clause, defined edible basis, current effective date, limit, units and signed relevance to that SKU. Never transfer different article numbers.
3. A NABL ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory's current scope demonstrably includes exact analyte, product/matrix, method and range/LOQ. Generic lab accreditation without relevant scope is insufficient.
4. The representative lot sample, preparation, sample chain of custody, recovery, QC blank, uncertainty and reporting basis are pre-agreed and documented.
5. Actual dated, traceable, signed lot CoA and test report are compared against the signed operative article using the authorised decision rule.
6. Independent QA/Regulatory authority signs or rejects the actual marketed-product and lot decision. This repo cannot issue those signatures.
