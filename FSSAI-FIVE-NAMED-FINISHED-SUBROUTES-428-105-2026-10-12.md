Chapter 2.3 §2.3.2 permits canned, bottled, flexible and aseptic fruit salad/cocktail. The named **canned fruit cocktail** limit requires confirmation of the actual can and exact fruit cocktail. §§2.3.3 and 2.3.62 likewise include variants other than canned vegetables or mushroom species; their canned commodity articles are applicable only to the matching named subroute. §2.3.41 covers chutneys from many fruits and vegetables, while CTR separately names **Mango chutney**. The FoSCoS entry `L(+/-)tartaric Acid` is broader or ambiguous relative to CTR's expressly **L(+)-Tartaric acid**. Do not treat it as proof of L(+) stereochemistry.

**Always blocked:** transferring the numbers to all catalogue variants; claims of tested-lot or ingredient compliance; applying canned limits to mere airtight packaging; using dried fruit/vegetable or raw-commodity articles for processed goods; treating any of these as a completed contaminant, pesticide, additive, microbiology or analytical panel.
