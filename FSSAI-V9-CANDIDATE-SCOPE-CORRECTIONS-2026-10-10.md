## Scope distinctions

- **Dairy:** 'Milks' with its concentration-factor condition is not an automatic lead-article candidate for 13 different milk fats, cream products, colostrum and isolated milk fractions. A separate 'Secondary milk products (as consumed)' review-only candidate was left where already present, without asserting its applicability.
- **Sugar:** the official dextrose/refined-white-sugar lead row cannot automatically stand in for Bura, jaggery, cube sugar, dried glucose syrup, golden syrup, icing sugar, khandsari, misri or plantation white sugar. The conditional high-sulphated-ash sugar article remains review-only.
- **Royal Jelly:** is a bee product; two sugar lead-row candidates were removed.
- **Fruit juice:** ready-to-drink fruit juice is not equivalent to an industrial concentrate or concentrated vegetable puree. Five inappropriate ready-to-drink candidates and one additional puree/juice candidate were removed.
- **Infant foods:** ready-to-use infant formula is not interchangeable with six follow-up, complementary or special infant-food identities; broader infant-food source candidates remain for proper classification.
- **Arrowroot / Chia:** neither is a cereal grain, so four generic cereal-product article candidate references were removed.
- **Tartaric acid:** L(+/-) racemic tartaric acid is distinct from the specifically cited L(+) acid; one misleading candidate removed.

The corrected review file records reasons for the exclusions and preserves all **236 pending statuses**. Removed candidates do **not** prove exemptions from contaminants or pesticide MRLs. Only an exact source article and confirmed finished-food identity can support a subsequent evidence status upgrade. Consumer-facing numerical findings must be separately verified against the source tables, with all units, qualifiers and current amendments.
