# PMSV — Two new qualified first named contaminant article matches

Official source: [FSSAI Contaminants Version IX, 03 Feb 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf); applicable standardized identity: [Chapter 2.1 dairy](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/2_%20Chapter%202_1%20(Dairy%20products%20and%20analogues).pdf), [Chapter 2.3 fruits and vegetables](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3%20%28Fruit%20%26%20Vegetable%20products%29.pdf).

- 2.1.14 **Ice Cream, Kulfi, Chocolate Ice Cream, Softy Ice-Cream, Milk Ice, Milk Lolly and Dried Ice Cream Mix**: FSSAI Version IX expressly names **Ice-cream, iced lollies and similar frozen confections** under Lead **1 mg/kg** (PDF page 4) and Arsenic **0.5 mg/kg** (PDF page 9). Exact *only for ready-to-eat frozen forms*. The FoSCoS combined identity includes dried ice cream mix and the cold-food row **must not** transfer to the dry form.
- 2.3.36 **Dehydrated Vegetables**: FSSAI explicitly names dried/dehydrated vegetables **other than onions** under Lead **5 mg/kg** (PDF p. 3), while **dehydrated onions** are separately named under Lead **10 mg/kg on dry matter basis** (PDF p. 3). Select botanical identity and exact product form before interpreting or measuring.
