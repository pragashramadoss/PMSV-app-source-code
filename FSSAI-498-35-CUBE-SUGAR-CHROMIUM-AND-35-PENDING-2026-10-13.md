## One additional conditional finished article

FSSR [Chapter 2.8, 2.8.1(5), PDF page 3](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_8%20(Sweetening%20agents%20including%20Honey).pdf) defines **Cube Sugar** as cube or cuboid blocks manufactured from refined crystallised sugar. The FSSR product has sucrose ≥99.7%, moisture ≤0.25% and **total ash ≤0.03%**. Additives permitted in the Regulations/Appendices may be included: any actual additives must therefore be checked.

The official [FSSAI CTR Version IX §2.1.1, Chromium p.13](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) lists **Refined sugar — 0.02 mg/kg**. The positive **conditional source-only** route is restricted to actual as-sold cubes composed only of shaped, unblended refined crystalline sugar, with supplier and formula proof and competent legal confirmation that the named CTR article covers the marketed cube finished product. It is not a confirmed food-limit assignment or an actual sample PASS.

**Do not infer a lead limit:** the FSSR uses **total ash**, whereas the CTR lead refined-white-sugar provision (p.2) requires **sulphated ash ≤0.03%**. An analytical sulphated ash result and specific legal identity would be required independently before making that link. Do not transfer the chromium source to Misri, whose standard allows any sugar or palmyrah juice.
