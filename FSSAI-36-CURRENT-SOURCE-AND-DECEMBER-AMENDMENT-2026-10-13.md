# FSSAI 36 pending exact source cases — updated current-status panel and December 2026 amendment safeguard

**Assessed:** 13 October 2026. **GitHub gh-pages preview only**, no WordPress changes.

The effective canonical ledger remains **533 product identities, 497 partial conditional first-named contaminant sources, 36 pending**. No additional case was promoted because no actual finished-product identity, qualifying legal article, analytical-basis evidence or measured lab results were supplied. The old 493/40, 495/38 and 497/36 source history remains unchanged and is presented as a collapsed historical disclosure instead of a potentially misleading current heading.

## Verified scope and changes

- All **36 unique** pending product identities joined to the current official-FSSAI decisive-source review, 4-document intake packet and effective 533-product ledger.
- **144 source-evidence requests** carried into the effective current view (four per product).
- **144 explicit positive source verification gates** (marketed food form, legal article scope, analytical basis, actual lab result), all pending.
- **180 amendment-to-product crosschecks** (36 products × five changes) under the official Gazette published May 2026.
- The five amendments cover pulse flour lead/cadmium, fish-oil inorganic arsenic, oilseed aflatoxin terminology, mace/nutmeg saffrole, and seafood veterinary drug additions.
- Chia as an oilseed and ingredient-dependent prepared products are future **watch candidates only**, never assumed to qualify.
- The official May 2026 amendment Gazette **272840** specifies **1 December 2026** commencement. As at 13 October it is **not effective**. The app must not activate any of its entries early; even on/after the nominal commencement it requires a fresh legal verification.
- None of the 36 identities is automatically within a uniquely identified named new article. An absent article is NOT an exemption.
- No extra numeric limits were applied, no lab PASS was asserted and no product was approved.

## UI

A new current source-status section is shown at the top of the research subsection for all **533** product identities, with exact finished-product decision and evidence checklist for the **36** unresolved cases. All earlier historical research and the 40-case local receipt notes remain accessible but **collapsed and clearly described as historical**. This prevents previously promoted categories from looking like they remain pending merely because the older 40-case module is still included.
