## Closure responsibility and handoff

**Manufacturer/supplier QA:** authenticate each actual as-sold marketed substance/product, ingredient identity, composition, basis and controlled specification. The supplied registry description alone is insufficient.

**Regulatory affairs/legal:** independently locate the currently operative original Gazette article for the *actual finished commodity and named contaminant*; record source and effectiveness; review special cases (children/infant class, plant versus dairy, processed matrices, additives, nutraceutical ingredients, honey versus royal jelly); sign an applicability decision. Do not copy related-product numbers as limits.

**Laboratory technical manager:** provide scope of accreditation, named-analyte procedure, version, matrix, recovery, sampling basis, LOQ and any preparation exclusions. **Actual traceable lot results** and independent QA review are required separately.

**Site QA/reviewer:** review representative lot identity, CoA authenticity, sample traceability and result uncertainty against a **signed legally applicable threshold**; then make a separate release decision.
