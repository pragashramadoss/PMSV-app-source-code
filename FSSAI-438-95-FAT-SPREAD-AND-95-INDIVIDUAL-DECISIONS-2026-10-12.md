## One additional source-supported conditional subtype

FoSCoS category 02.2.2 Fat spread — FSSR **2.2.5(3)** includes milk-fat, mixed-fat and vegetable-fat spreads (FSSR Version 3, PDF p46).
CTR Version IX §2.1.1 Nickel names **hydrogenated, partially hydrogenated or interesterified vegetable fats including fat spread**, **1.5 mg/kg** (PDF p13).

A separate *qualifying finished subtype* exists when the actual fat spread contains appropriately processed vegetable fat. Milk-fat-only and solely unhydrogenated-fat spreads remain excluded from that Nickel article. The source does **not** establish the actual marketed product's processing or a measured Nickel result. Minarine low-fat spread Lead/Arsenic articles are separate and must not be assigned to generic 40–80% Fat Spread.

**Important food-matrix protections:**
- **Vegetable puree**: §2.3.14 separately standardizes tomato puree/paste. Broad §2.3.13/2.3.16/2.3.17 does NOT prove an identical finished article. Copper tomato solids dry-matter basis cannot be copied onto generic as-sold puree.
- **Cereal/egg products**: raw MRLs and unprocessed shell-free egg figures are not finished food evidence without exact legal processing and sampling scope.
- **Sugars**: refined sugar, high-ash sugars and starch-conversion syrup articles require exact chemical/sulphated ash category and test basis; they do not cover all sweeteners.
- **Dairy analogues**: FAQ Q7 requires corresponding-category safety rules but does not make analogues legally ordinary milk.
- **Contaminant amendment effectivity**: Version IX is **3 February 2026**. Current notified amendments and applicability must be checked separately before a legal clearance decision; compendium snapshots are not proof of current full amendments.
