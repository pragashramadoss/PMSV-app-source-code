# PMSV — Eleven FSSAI Chapter 2.3 process and quality source verifications (11 October 2026)
## Scope and evidence
**Eleven current FoSCoS product identities; 48 source-pinned quality and process rows.** Official [FSSAI Food Products Standards Chapter 2.3, Version 1](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3%20%28Fruit%20%26%20Vegetable%20products%29.pdf) verified against the printed pages 25–56. The current [FSSAI Contaminants Version IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) remains the separate chemical source.

Verified differences:
- Fruit, vegetable and spicy chutney 2.3.41: TSS 50 / 25 / 25% respectively, fruit+vegetable 40%, pH 4.6, total ash 5%, acid-insoluble ash 0.5%; rigid pack fill 90%. **Mango Chutney 2.3.42 is another standard and its Lead/Tin article must not leak to 2.3.41.**
- Culinary vs ginger paste 2.3.28: salt-free TSS minimum 8% versus 3%, both with acetic acid acidity minimum 1%; no colours other than caramel. 
- Murabba 2.3.25: TSS minimum 65%, fruit-content stated minimum 55%, but rhizome/vegetable variants require qualification.
- Candied/crystallised/glazed 2.3.26: total sugar minimum 70%, reducing sugar minimum 25% **of total sugar**, not 25% of finished food.
- Three named pickle routes 2.3.43: brine NaCl 12%, citrus citric acidity 1.2%, oil/vinegar drained mass 60%, vinegar acetic acidity 2%; medium must be confirmed. The source text "free from copper" is not transformed into a 0 mg/kg chemical MRL.
- Vegetable pulp/puree industrial and concentrated 2.3.13/16/17: 90% rigid-pack fill and relative concentration criteria where the underlying juice/pulp/puree is known.

**No new exact Version IX contaminant article was established in this batch**. None of the 11 product identities is auto-cleared or receives a sample compliance PASS; summary remains 533 total, 410 partial exact and 123 unresolved. WordPress unchanged; GitHub `gh-pages` only.
