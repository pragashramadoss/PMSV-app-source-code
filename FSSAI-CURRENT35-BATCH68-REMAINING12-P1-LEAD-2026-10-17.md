## Purpose and evidence results

This is a research workbench, **not** approval of any contaminant value, laboratory result or batch release.
The 12 remaining P1 cases are ranked:
- **P1 #5 — Catechu (Edible):** Catechu extract identity ≠ betelnut or pan masala. Required: Source plant/species, aqueous or other extraction, ingredient vs finished food.
- **P1 #11 — Chia Seeds:** Chia seed identity ≠ cereal, pulse, nut or oilseed lead class by assumption. Required: Salvia hispanica, raw/hulled/milled, botanical authentication.
- **P1 #12 — Carob Powder:** Carob powder ≠ cocoa powder on dry fat-free basis. Required: Carob pod species, roast status, powder specification and moisture.
- **P1 #14 — WATER CHESTNUT FLOUR (SINGHARE KA ATTA):** Water chestnut flour ≠ cereal flour or canned chestnut. Required: Botanical water chestnut species, peeled/dried/milled as-sold flour.
- **P1 #15 — Animal Casings:** Casing ≠ meat, edible offal or gelatin. Required: Animal species; natural intestine, collagen-reconstituted or artificial; food contact/use.
- **P1 #18 — Dried Glucose Syrup:** Ash-threshold solid glucose comparator ≠ every dried glucose syrup. Required: Dried glucose syrup manufacturing route, DE and measured sulphated ash percent.
- **P1 #19 — Edible Lactose:** Purified edible lactose ≠ milk, secondary milk product or refined cane sugar. Required: Milk origin, lactose purity, moisture, hydrate/anhydrous form and as-sold grade.
- **P1 #22 — Salt Substitutes:** Salt substitutes ≠ food-grade salt. Required: Each declared substitute salt, mineral carrier, percentage and product formulation.
- **P1 #23 — Trehalose:** Trehalose ≠ refined sugar/glucose just because it is sweet. Required: Exact disaccharide identity, CAS, manufacturer specification, crystallization/hydrate and purity.
- **P1 #24 — Sajji Khar:** Sajji Khar trade name ≠ sodium hydroxide or another specific alkali. Required: Mineral and chemical species composition, CAS/INS when applicable, food grade.
- **P1 #32 — SILVER LEAF (Chandi-ka-warq):** Edible silver leaf identity/purity ≠ lead criterion. Required: Edible silver leaf fineness, animal-origin-free processing aids and food-use grade.
- **P1 #35 — Dietary Fibre (Dextrin-soluble fibre):** Dextrin-soluble fibre ≠ maltodextrin, generic starch or glucose. Required: Fibre identity, dextrinification process, degree of polymerization, dietary fibre specification.

Two regulatory **published comparison-only** entries require particularly strong blocking:
- **P1 #18 Dried Glucose Syrup:** CTR published **5 mg/kg** Lead for a narrowly identified solid-glucose/starch-conversion category where sulphated ash **exceeds 1.0%**; **not** an approved lead limit for any unspecified dried glucose syrup lot. Must prove both material classification and ash threshold and authenticate operative Gazette.
- **P1 #22 Salt Substitutes:** CTR food-grade salt comparison **2.0 mg/kg**; not a lead limit for an unspecified salt substitute formulation. Must authenticate each constituent and the original applicable legal article.
