# PMSV FSSAI Helper — Finished-food exact-article conditional review (11 October 2026)

Source: [FSSAI Contaminants Version IX, Section 2.1](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) and the FoSCoS catalogue.

9 unresolved FoSCoS identities have 21 **specific finished-form options**, referencing 43 source-checked legal metal article occurrences. The group includes: mango chutney; cucumber pickles; canned fruit cocktail and tropical fruit salad; specified canned mango, pineapple, grapefruit, mandarin orange, raspberry and strawberry products; canned vegetables including beans, peas, carrot, sweetcorn, tomato, asparagus and edible mushrooms; processed tomato concentrate; and tomato puree/paste requiring dried tomato solids for copper.

Each analyte, exact article text, numerical value, mg/kg unit and analytical-basis condition is individually matched to FSSAI Version IX core. **A broad FoSCoS identity does not prove the individual subtype.** Product standard, ingredients, recipe, botanical species, packing state, analytical method, current legal amendments and sample evidence remain to be checked before applying any actual limit.

A collapsible, product-specific source-candidate panel is integrated into the Helper's contaminants screen. Matching review worksheets receive the same variants. None of the source numbers is auto-applied to a selected finished product. The evidence ledger remains **533 products, 412 partial, 121 awaiting first exact product-specific source**, with **no compliance approvals**.
