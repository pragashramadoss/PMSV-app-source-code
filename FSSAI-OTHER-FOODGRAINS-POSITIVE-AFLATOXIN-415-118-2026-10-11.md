# FSSAI FSSR 2.4.6(15) — positive aflatoxin first source (11 October 2026)

Official FSSR Chapter 2.4 defines Food Grains as whole/broken cereal, millet and pulse kernels, with (15) for other food grains. Current Version IX 2.2.1 crop contaminants have identical named cereal and pulse article ceilings: Aflatoxin B1 **10 µg/kg**, Total Aflatoxins **15 µg/kg**. Thus the exact residual whole-foodgrains product has partial positive legal source coverage, without choosing an assumed botanical grain species or assigning irrelevant pesticide or metal articles.
