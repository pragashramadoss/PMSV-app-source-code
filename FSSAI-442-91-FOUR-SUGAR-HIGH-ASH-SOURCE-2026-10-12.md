## Four conditional named finished-product sources

- **Khandsari Sugar** (2.8.1(3)): Khandsari Desi or Sulphur sugar with confirmed sulphated ash >1%. Gate: FSSR 2.8.1(3) HCl-insoluble ash 0.5%/0.7% is not sulphated ash.
- **Bura Sugar** (2.8.1(4)): Bura sugar with confirmed sulphated ash >1%. Gate: FSSR 2.8.1(4) acid-insoluble ash <=0.7% cannot substitute.
- **Plantation White Sugar** (2.8.1(1)): Plantation white sugar with confirmed sulphated ash >1%. Gate: Refined white sugar (≤0.03% sulphated ash) is a separate source article.
- **Golden Syrup** (2.8.6): Golden invert sugar syrup with confirmed sulphated ash >1%. Gate: Golden syrup FSSR total ash ≤2.5%, not a measured sulphated ash result.

## Four excluded sugars not promoted

- **11-11-1-dried-glucose-syrup:** FSSR 2.8.7 sulphated ash ≤1% precludes high-ash >1% source assignment
- **11-11-1-cube-sugar:** FSSR 2.8.1(5) total ash ≤0.03% is NOT a tested sulphated ash result
- **11-11-1-misri:** FSSR 2.8.2 total ash ≤0.4% is NOT a tested sulphated ash result
- **11-11-1-icing-sugar:** FSSR 2.8.1(6) sugar/starch composition does not demonstrate high sulphated ash

**Regulatory interpretation safeguards:** Total ash, ash insoluble in dilute hydrochloric acid, and sulphated ash are distinct analytical methods. A legal high-ash article requires *sulphated ash strictly exceeding 1.0%*, not 1.0%. Golden Syrup total ash <=2.5%, Khandsari HCl-insoluble ash limits and Bura acid-insoluble ash cannot prove the CTR requirement. Dried Glucose Syrup FSSR sulphated ash <=1.0% directly prevents high-ash application to a conforming item.
