## Representative source-critical findings

1. **Dried Glucose Syrup (FSSR 2.8.7):** Chapter 2.8 requires **sulfated ash ≤1.0% by mass**. The Version IX Lead named article for solid glucose and starch conversion products with sulphated ash **>1.0%** cannot be applied as an unconditional 5 mg/kg limit to a product meeting that standard. This excludes only that exact conditional article, **not all lead limits**. Other source articles including “Foods not specified” require separate applicability review.
2. **Cube Sugar (FSSR 2.8.1(5)):** the chapter-quality **total ash ≤0.03%** must not be substituted for Version IX **sulphated ash ≤0.03%**.
3. **FSSR Fruit/Vegetable, Cereal Flakes:** Official Chapter 2.3 standard **2.3.20** conflicts with the current PMSV catalogue route **2.4.35** for this exact identity, so cereal-only numerical claims are blocked pending master-data correction.
4. **Raw cocoa beans, cocoa mass/cake and cocoa powder are different regulated matrices.** Dry fat-free substance metal bases used for cocoa powder cannot automatically transfer to cocoa beans, cocoa mass, chocolate or cocoa/sugar blends.
5. **Dairy analogues** need actual animal-milk, plant fats and proteins and FoSCoS subtype confirmation; no blanket milk-derived Aflatoxin M1 or pesticide application is declared.
6. **Rabbit, casings and processed eggs** need correct species, animal tissue, veterinary-drug residue definitions and specific processing state. Chapter-specific egg quality parameters are not pesticide residue limits.
7. **Processed or packaged fruit/fungi products** cannot assume the canned mushrooms/fruit cocktail/pickled cucumber articles from the generic food name. Actual material, metal packaging, sterilization and as-sold test basis must be proven.
8. **Special categories 102 and 18** are FoSCoS identities, not automatically product-specific CTR contaminant rows. Nutraceutical and additive premixes require each permitted substance identity and actual recipe.

### Four coffee-chicory legal product forms (FSSR 2.10.4)

The standard differentiates roasted-ground coffee-chicory (moisture ≤5%, caffeine ≥0.6%), instant (moisture ≤4%, caffeine ≥1.4%), decaffeinated roasted-ground (moisture ≤5%, caffeine ≤0.1%) and decaffeinated instant (moisture ≤4%, caffeine ≤0.3%). **All four require at least 51% coffee** on the specified mass/dry basis, but ash and caffeine limits depend on precise subtype. Four variants with 22 numerical composition values are recorded source-exactly; no OTA, lead or pesticide PASS is inferred. See [official Chapter 2.10, PDF pp.7–10](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_10_BEVERAGES_Other%20than%20Dairy%20and%20Fruits%20Vegetables%20based.pdf) and [FSSAI Version IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf).
