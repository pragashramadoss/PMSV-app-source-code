## Audited source anchors

1. **[FSSAI CTR Compendium IX, 3 February 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf)**. Specifically reviewed: lead 2.1.1, PDF p.2–6; total and B1 aflatoxin 2.2.1, PDF p.14; pesticide-specific table 2.3.1, PDF p.16–39, including the note on non-fixed MRLs at p.39; antibiotic/bee-honey, veterinary and seafood distinctions 2.3.2 at p.39–50. This is an official compiled reference, not a substitute for original operative notified text.
2. **[FSSAI FPSFA chapter-wise compendium](https://fssai.gov.in/food-law/regulations/compendium/food-products-standards)**. Establishes identity-standard routes including 2.11 (Other Products), 2.4 (Cereals), 2.5 (Meat and Meat products), not an instant CTR contaminant limit.
3. **[Official historical FPSFA consolidated source dated 20 December 2022](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Food_Additives_Regulations_20_12_2022.pdf)**, including 2.11.4 silver leaf, 2.11.7 carob and 2.11.8 dietary fibre. *Historical* text is a product-identity reference only; compare latest chapter/current originals before legal signoff.
4. **[FSSAI original FPSFA amendment list](https://fssai.gov.in/food-law/regulations/amendments/food-products-standards)**, including 29 December 2020 Tenth Amendment covering animal casings, egg powder, pickled eggs, water chestnut flour. Confirm the applicable original gazette and later amendments.
5. **[FoSCoS FSSAI standard-product registry](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product)** supports the category terminology (e.g., Chia, Arrowroot, Trehalose, Sajji Khar, broader ingredient classes), **not actual manufacturer compliance**.
6. The [original May 2026 CTR amendment Gazette](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/272840.pdf) expressly commences **1 December 2026**; it is future-effective on the 17 October 2026 as-of date.

## Exact hold reasoning

For **lead**: CTR's "Foods not specified" fallback is indeed visible in the metal table. A reviewer must **not** choose it prematurely when a specific article, purified ingredient monograph, formulation basis, or other operative rule may govern. The product identity standard and the contaminant maximum are different legal inquiries.

For **pesticides**: CTR's 0.01 mg/kg note for unfixed MRLs should **not** be used to generate an unqualified product-wide test panel or mark a dried/processed food compliant. Obtain relevant pesticide names, crop/food class and any permitted processing/concentration interpretation.

For **aflatoxins**: Total and B1 are different analytes; the CTR expressly refers to food products containing some listed foods. The user has not supplied a signed original premix formulation, so a rule involving cereal/oilseed/nut/spice constituents cannot yet be assigned.

For **royal jelly**: a honey-specific MRPL is not proof of royal jelly scope. Likewise antibiotic provisions for meat/milk/eggs/seafood are not freely transferable. Confirm original effective text and named analytes from an authorized RA review.
