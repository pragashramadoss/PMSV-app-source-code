## New official notification discovered

FSSAI's public Gazette listing records the September 2026 final Food Safety and Standards (Laboratory and Sample Analysis) Amendment Regulations, 2026, bearing F. No. QA/11023/31/2022-QA-FSSAI(2); Gazette ID CG-DL-E-17092026-276298.

**Effective only from 1 April 2027**, according to two separate accessible full-text transcriptions, including the direct line "They shall come into force on 1st April, 2027." As of **16 October 2026** neither newly inserted 2.5 nor 2.6 is operative by virtue of this amendment. The rules may already contain separate similar obligations (e.g., importing test reports); no assertion that a current duty does not exist is supported.

Specific safeguards:
- New 2.5 will require use of manuals amended/adopted by FSSAI, with allowance for validated methods prescribed by listed recognised bodies only when a relevant FSSAI manual method is unavailable. **This future rule does not certify any 2024 09.xxx manual**, or settle the 52-page vs 61-page method identity collision.
- New 2.6 describes **14-day regulatory food analyst and appeal/referral reporting** and **five-day import reporting**, subject to reasons/delay route. It **does not create a generic five-day or fourteen-day deadline for industry CoAs**.
- Current regime and legal analyte/commodity limits require separate legal examination.
