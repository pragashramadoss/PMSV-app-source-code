## Exact amendment 1: Pickled eggs (FSSR 2.5.3 clause 5)

The October 2024 Gazette entirely **substitutes** the previous Pickled Eggs clause. The two product forms have *different* analytical requirements. See [Gazette pp.30–31](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%20DATED%2021_10_2024_ammendments.pdf#page=30).

| Tested characteristic | Vinegar-based egg pickle | Oil-based egg pickle | Regulatory test direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Acidity as acetic acid | **0.8% minimum** | **0.6% minimum** | Minimum, not permitted range |
| Sodium chloride | **3.0% maximum** | **3.0% maximum** | Percent by mass |
| pH of pickling solution | **3.2 maximum** | **3.6 maximum** | Separate **maximum**, not the previous shared pH 3.2–3.6 interval |

The official prescribed processes also distinguish vinegar-water (50:50), a heated solution of 70 ± 2°C, an egg-to-solution average ratio of 1:1.25, and maturation; versus the oil-based preparation with an egg-to-gravy ratio of 70:30 (±1%) and 48-hour maturation. The applicable detailed process and correct finished variant require evidence, not an assumption from the food category.

**Important correction:** Older consolidated FSSR references contain a single 3.2–3.6 pH range and 0.6–0.8 acidity text. Do **not** apply those superseded values to samples after the amendment took effect.

## Exact amendment 2: Cow/buffalo colostrum (FSSR 2.1.23)

The same [Gazette, pp.25–26](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/GAZETTE%20NOTIFICATION%20DATED%2021_10_2024_ammendments.pdf#page=25), substituted two specific *immunoglobulins-G* (IgG) rows:

| As-sold FSSR product | Minimum IgG | Legal reference |
|---|---:|---|
| Liquid cow/buffalo colostrum | **1.8% (m/m)** | 2.1.23, item 2(I)(a), row 7 |
| Cow/buffalo colostrum powder | **8.5% (m/m)** | 2.1.23, item 2(II)(b), row 8 |

The amended analytical target is **IgG specifically**, not an undifferentiated total-immunoglobulins COA. Older powder material may show 12.5% total immunoglobulins. For this amended row the operative Gazette states 8.5% IgG. Original FSSR other clauses (protein, fat, moisture, labeling, hygiene) must still be assessed separately.
