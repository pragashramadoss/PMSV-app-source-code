# PMSV FSSAI — three positive canned *subtype* source routes, 8 variants (11 October 2026)

Official product standards: FSSR 2.3.1 Thermally Processed Fruits, 2.3.2 Thermally Processed Fruit Salad/Cocktail/Mix and 2.3.3 Thermally Processed Vegetables (Chapter 2.3). These permit canned, bottled, flexible and aseptic product forms. Thus 'thermally processed' alone does not prove the item is canned.

Verified named FSSAI Contaminants Version IX Chapter 2.1 Lead 1 mg/kg articles: canned fruit cocktail, canned tropical fruit salad, canned mangoes, canned pineapple, canned strawberries, canned carrots, canned green peas, canned tomatoes. Tin 250 mg/kg article: canned citrus/stone fruits/vegetables/fruit cocktail/mangoes/pineapple/raspberries/strawberries/tropical fruit salad. This yields 8 named canned branches x 2 analytes = 16 source values, referenced ONLY when the actual finished canned form is proven.

Unqualified catalogue items are not promoted and no numeric source is applied automatically. Current first-source inventory remains 533 products, 414 with partial exact source, 119 still lacking first-source proof. Verified form/type, packaging, amendment, method and lab result remain outstanding. No complete laboratory or legal PASS.
