## Case acceptance must remain divided into independent gates

### 1. SOURCE_VERSION_AND_METHOD_AUTHORITY — 7 per case

1. **SRC_PRIMARY: Primary authoritative FSSAI/manual/Gazette PDF URL is identified.** Retrieve original official source; not a third-party screenshot or unverified extract.
2. **SRC_ISSUER: Issuer and original issuing authority independently identifiable.** Check official FSSAI regulatory or Gazette issuer and original file provenance.
3. **SRC_FULL_VERSION: Document full title, publication date, version and page shown.** Record full method/manual title and version, not an isolated 09.xxx identifier.
4. **SRC_ADOPTION: Final/notified/adopted status and its legal or technical basis checked.** Consultation/working copy cannot be represented as final without relevant official adoption evidence.
5. **SRC_TIME: Effective date, supersession, withdrawal and applicability date checked.** Validate legal and method source timing as of 16 October 2026, including later amendments.
6. **SRC_METHOD_ID: Analyte, method title and unique numbering collision resolved.** 2024 09.xxx method numbers can refer to different procedures across FSSAI-hosted manuals.
7. **SRC_AUTHORITY: Independent source-authority review signoff evidence requested.** Obtain documented method-precedence determination from an authorized technical reviewer.

### 2. COMMERCIAL_ARTICLE_IDENTITY — 7 per case

1. **ID_MFG: Actual manufacturer, supplier, product ID and license basis documented.** Names from catalogue are not identities of an actual marketed lot.
2. **ID_LABEL: Controlled market label/specification and correct sold form available.** Confirm liquid, whole, dried, powdered, blended, processed and intended use.
3. **ID_BOM: Authenticated controlled material identity/formulation reference obtained.** Obtain a signed BOM/formula document reference without copying confidential formulation into this public helper.
4. **ID_BOTANICAL: Ingredient origin, botanical or species, CAS/INS/EC as relevant established.** Identify source crop, animal species, individual additive or enzyme as applicable.
5. **ID_MATRIX: Fat, moisture, soluble solids, dry/fat-free and reconstitution basis checked.** Only when relevant; signed nonapplicability determination needed if excluded.
6. **ID_SAMPLE: Manufactured batch, sample, chain and physical identity matched.** Do not claim sample compliance without representative product traceability.
7. **ID_QA: Manufacturer QA attestation and current revision independently reviewed.** Catalogued product standard alone does not authenticate actual product.

### 3. LEGAL_CTR_ARTICLE_AND_ANALYTE — 7 per case

1. **CTR_GAZETTE: Exact operative original notified Gazette and amendments identified.** Official compiled CTR compendium is a reference, not a substitute for effective original text.
2. **CTR_COMMODITY: Applicable legal commodity/article text matches actual as-sold product.** Do not transplant comparators from coffee to blends, cocoa powder to liquor, milk to coconut products, etc.
3. **CTR_ANALYTE: Exact regulated analyte/chemical/residue definition named.** Aflatoxin B1 vs total, named pesticide MRL, specific antibiotic or metal must match.
4. **CTR_LIMIT: Numerical maximum, units and expressed basis documented by signed regulatory reviewer.** No applicable value or numeric result is populated by this preparatory tool.
5. **CTR_SCOPE: Processing, conversion, ingredient and exception considerations independently resolved.** Assess food matrix, dry/fat-free, concentration factor and composite mixture provisions.
6. **CTR_DATE: Time-window and future-effective amendment guard rechecked.** May 2026 CTR amendment starts 1 December 2026; 2027 laboratory rule not yet in force as of review.
7. **CTR_SIGNED: RA/Legal independent article-and-maximum determination received as actual evidence.** Do not infer approval from a product identity chapter or category heading.

### 4. LAB_MATRIX_METHOD_AND_LOQ — 8 per case

1. **LAB_IDENTITY: Exact selected testing laboratory and location identified.** Neither assumed accreditation nor a search-result listing establishes the individual lab identity.
2. **LAB_FSSAI: FSSAI notification status for chosen laboratory at sample date checked.** Use current FSSAI list and relevant notification, where required for regulatory sample analysis.
3. **LAB_NABL: NABL/ISO IEC 17025 certificate number, validity and status verified at source.** Accredited institution alone does not guarantee test scope or continued validity.
4. **LAB_ANALYTE: Accredited scope explicitly includes exact analyte and measurement range.** Lead, chromium, nickel, aflatoxin B1, total aflatoxin or named pesticide cannot be interchanged.
5. **LAB_MATRIX: Accredited or otherwise appropriately validated scope covers actual submitted food matrix.** Confirm as-sold cocoa, carob, water, high-fat product, egg brine, salt substitute or other relevant matrix.
6. **LAB_METHOD: Selected full method title/version and FSSAI adoption status resolved.** Do not rely on colliding 2024 09.xxx numbers or wrong analyte procedure.
7. **LAB_LOQ: LOQ, recovery, blanks, precision, uncertainty and legal maximum compatibility established.** Reported detection capability is not laboratory-validated method LOQ; obtain matrix evidence.
8. **LAB_SAMPLE: Sampling instructions, sample preservation, subcontracting and custody scope agreed.** Prove that collected product lot and actual analyzed matrix/basis are the same legally relevant material.

### 5. LOT_COA_AND_INDEPENDENT_SIGNOFF — 8 per case

1. **LOT_ID: Traceable marketed manufacturing lot and product identity recorded.** Tie supplier COA and laboratory report to same formulation and physical batch.
2. **LOT_SAMPLE: Approved representative sampling plan, collector and date documented.** Sampling method, sample size, storage and chain-of-custody independently checked.
3. **LOT_CERT: Complete original signed testing laboratory CoA/report authenticated.** Verify source, date, sample ID, laboratory reference and amendments/reissued reports.
4. **LOT_RESULT: Named analyte, actual result, units and reporting basis compared correctly.** Never compare arbitrary contaminant units or as-reconstituted result to as-sold maximum.
5. **LOT_METHOD: CoA references the approved method, matrix, lab scope and LOQ.** The analyte test must be applicable to the same legal commodity and lot.
6. **LOT_UNCERTAINTY: Method uncertainty, censoring/non-detect, recoveries and decision rule reviewed.** No apparent numeric PASS without appropriate legal comparison and uncertainty decision.
7. **LOT_SITE_QA: Independent site QA inspection/disposition documented by an authorized reviewer.** A sample reference, issue log or supplier self-claim is not a site QA lot release.
8. **LOT_RA: Independent regulatory reviewer explicitly signs article basis and lot conclusion.** Prevent a one-person or self-certified approval triggered by checklist completion.


### Why a named notified laboratory is not enough

The [FSSAI Food Laboratories official portal](https://fssai.gov.in/food-testing/food-laboratories) states that FSSAI recognizes and notifies NABL-accredited laboratories under Section 43 and makes a primary-food-laboratory list available dated **7 September 2026**. The [2026 notifications archive](https://www.fssai.gov.in/notifications.php?pages=6) separately lists **3 September 2026** NABL-laboratory Gazette notifications. A name appearing on one list does **not** prove the exact laboratory site is currently notified for the regulatory purpose, nor does it establish accreditation covering the particular **analyte, actual food matrix, detection/quantification range, analytical method and report date**. Check the original certificate, relevant current NABL scope, FSSAI notification where applicable, and sample-specific laboratory procedure before interpreting a result.

A product such as **cocoa mass or cake** needs fat/matrix-specific lead testing checks; **misri** needs chromium in the actual sugar-crystal matrix rather than adopting an infant nutrition method; a **polyol** needs the actual named CAS/INS and nickel as an analyte, not a laboratory internal standard. No particular laboratory was selected and none of these cases was signed off by this batch.

### Legal effective dates

The [currently FSSAI-hosted CTR Compendium Version IX dated 3 February 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) is an authoritative hosted *review reference*; the original operative Gazette takes precedence wherever wording or amendment effect differs. The [May 2026 CTR Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/272840.pdf) states **1 December 2026** commencement; its future provisions cannot be applied as enacted law on 16 October 2026. Prior batch evidence also identified the [laboratory/sample-analysis amendment Gazette](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/276298.pdf) as a **1 April 2027** future-effect provision, subject to confirmation against the original full Gazette before taking action. A method's presence in the [official FSSAI methods index](https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-testing/manuals-methods-analysis) does not by itself authenticate its amendment/adoption, matrix suitability or the lab's accredited scope.

## Browser-only workflow and privacy

- Select one of the 35 cases or expand to all cases. Filter the P0/P1 priorities and the five original gates, search individual check code, text, analyte, or case.
- Each checkpoint displays its permanent `PMSV57-xx-gg-cc` ID, inherited `PMSV44-xx-gg` gate, functional owner role, exact required evidence and original dependency.
- Allowed *temporary, unverified triage labels only*: `UNASSESSED`, `REFERENCE_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED`, `SOURCE_OR_SCOPE_GAP_IDENTIFIED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE_REQUIRES_SIGNED_REASON`. The last option does not mark a requirement waived; signed review is still needed.
- Optional browser-memory draft accepts an **evidence document reference only** (up to 120 characters) and a short follow-up note (up to 240 characters). No confidential BOM, laboratory report contents, supplier contact information or specific human legal advice should be pasted into it.
- Download all **1,295 original blank questions** as CSV, export only entered notes as a distinct CSV, or export/restore your own temporary metadata as JSON. The blank worklist **does not include typed session content**.
- Import rejects wrong digest, unknown or repeated checklist IDs, unsupported status, surplus schema fields including approval claims, any authenticated document count, any legal approval, any lab method approval or any lot PASS. Validation is atomic before any draft replacement.
- There are **no** remote data writes, local/session browser persistence, automatic email submissions, file-content uploads, real evidence receipts or approvals.
