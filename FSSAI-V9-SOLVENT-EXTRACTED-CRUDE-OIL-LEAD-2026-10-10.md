# FSSAI 2.2.9 source review — solvent-extracted crude vegetable oils (10 October 2026)

**Finding:** The exact FSSR 2.2.9 product category has source-backed **partial** contaminant evidence for Lead. This is not full compliance.

- **Product identity**: FSSAI Chapter 2.2, Version 5 (1 August 2025), §2.2.9 describes solvent-extracted crude vegetable oils using food-grade hexane from edible-oil sources recognized in §2.2.1. These crude oils cannot be consumed directly and must be refined before consumption. [Official FSSAI Chapter 2.2](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_2_Fats_oils%20and%20fat%20emulsions.pdf).
- **Exact grouped contaminant article**: FSSAI Contaminants Regulations, Version IX (3 February 2026), §2.1.1 **Lead — Vegetable Oils, crude: 0.1 mg/kg**, expressly excluding cocoa butter. [Official Version IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf).
- **Different arsenic scope**: Version IX **Arsenic — Vegetable oils, crude: 0.1 mg/kg** lists particular source oils without the broader "other oils" group. This must *not* be auto-applied across every 2.2.9 oil; require exact input source.
- **Verification gate**: the actual 2.2.9 product ID, name, source row, numerical value and mg/kg unit must agree with the loaded official table. Other metal limits, pesticides, source-oil-specific requirements, processing and operative amendments remain unverified.

**Audit consequence:** exact partial evidence products 297 → 298; unresolved exact-evidence identities 236 → 235; products with independently verified complete compliance = 0.
