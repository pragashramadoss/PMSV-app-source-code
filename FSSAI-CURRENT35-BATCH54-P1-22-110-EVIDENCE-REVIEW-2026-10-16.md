## Official contaminant source cross-check

Original FSSAI-hosted [CTR Version IX (3 February 2026)](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) was reopened during this batch, with **specific table pages visually inspected**:

1. **Lead fallback** — CTR PDF page 4 (zero-based index 3) contains “Foods not specified” and a separate “chemicals not otherwise specified” description (with pH, use/form and legal definition considerations). These are not synonyms and must never be used automatically for the 15 unresolved lead/materials without signed classification. Other named commodity articles may take precedence.
2. **Mycotoxins** — PDF pages 14–15: ochratoxin A is listed for **coffee** and specified cereal products, not expressly every final coffee-chicory mixture; total aflatoxin, aflatoxin B1 and aflatoxin M1 have **different analyte definitions and distinct food/article scopes**. Crop, processing and composite article must be established before choosing a limit.
3. **Pesticide MRLs** — PDF page 17 and subsequent pesticide tables require an **identified pesticide/residue definition and legally corresponding food**; some eggs entries use a *shell-free basis*. Frozen fruit of unknown botanical species, purified arrowroot starch, egg powder fractions and pickled eggs cannot be assigned one collective 'pesticide residues' limit.
4. **Ash-dependent sugar lead entries** — PDF pages 2 and 4 distinguish high-sulphated-ash sugar/syrup/starch conversion products from other sugar articles. FSSR **dried glucose syrup** specifies a sulphated ash ceiling of 1.0%; a comparator requiring **ash exceeding 1.0%** is inappropriate for conforming dried glucose syrup. **Trehalose** total ash does not become sulphated ash without product-specific evidence.
5. The [original notified May 2026 CTR Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/272840.pdf) **takes effect 1 December 2026**; none of its provisions is silently applied to 16 October cases.

A published FSSR ingredient-quality standard, FoSCoS classification category, botanical/family identity or application-register entry **does not** establish a legally applicable finished-product CTR numeric limit or real lot PASS. If a compiled document conflicts with original notified/operative Gazette wording, original effective law governs.

## Three checks that would cause material classification errors

**Coffee/chicory ochratoxin A:** even if FSSR 2.10.4 confirms the named coffee-chicory mixture, it is not equivalent to an as-sold bag of coffee for automatic OTA limit transfer. Record blend fractions/instant versus roasted form, identify legal finished article and validate toxin recovery.

**Egg powder/pickled eggs pesticide MRLs:** an MRL for a named pesticide in shell-free eggs cannot be applied to a dried whole/yolk/white powder or egg-with-acid-brine package without legal processing-basis justification and matrix validation.

**Premix aflatoxin B1 vs total:** an ingredient listing nuts, oilseeds, spices or cereals potentially raises composite food aflatoxin questions; first identify which toxin and crop constituents, their amounts, finished product presentation and the operative legal article. The existence of a composite article does **not** mean every premix automatically matches it.
