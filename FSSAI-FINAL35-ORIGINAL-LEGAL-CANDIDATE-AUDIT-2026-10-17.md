# Final 35 FSSAI CTR cases — substantive legal-candidate audit (NOT APPROVAL)

**17 October 2026. Completed desk-research triage for all original 35 cases. This is not a product or lot clearance.**

## Why the prior closeout was not enough
The older case register documented an unresolved CTR legal article for every product, but did not systematically distinguish *actual named article*, *unlisted-article fallback*, *chemical monograph*, and *individual pesticide/antibiotic/natural-toxin* law. This addendum makes those decisions explicit for every case, **without inventing approvals**.

The official FSSAI CTR Version VIII (April 2025) contains **Lead: “Foods not specified” 2.5 mg/kg** (PDF p.4) and **Copper: “Foods not specified” 30 mg/kg** (PDF p.7). The indexed Version IX (3 February 2026) still displays copper fallback language. This review did not authenticate the complete Version IX lead row or original operative Gazette for an actual marketed product; **these figures are legal research candidates only**, not selected legal limits. “Chemical not otherwise specified” and Indian Pharmacopoeia clauses require their own classification, rather than indiscriminate application of the food fallback.

The FSSAI published OTA crop-contaminant entry names coffee (not automatically a coffee–chicory final mixture). The aflatoxin table includes named constituent foods and products containing them, but only where the actual formulation supports that identity. Pesticide limits are determined **pesticide × commodity** and sometimes sample-preparation/processed-matrix rules. FSSR product-quality properties such as 999/1000 purity for silver leaf are **not** the same as a lead concentration threshold.

## Original 2011 metal-contaminant notification independently inspected

The [original FSSAI 1 August 2011 Contaminants, Toxins and Residues notification](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Contaminants_Regulations.pdf) was read directly, including the text and page images.

- **Original PDF p.2:** Regulation 2.1.1 includes **Lead → “Foods not specified” → 2.5 mg/kg**, and the separate special high-sulphated-ash sugar/starch-conversion entry. The individual as-marketed food must first be excluded from all applicable named rows.
- **Original PDF p.3:** The **Copper → “Foods not specified” → 30 mg/kg** provision is distinct from the named **pulp and pulp products of any fruit → 5 mg/kg**. Seedless tamarind's actual commercial physical form is decisive; do not select either number without supplier identity and signed interpretation.
- The official **2025 Version VIII compendium** displays both fallback rows, and the **2026 Version IX** source is indexed but full direct inspection could not be completed here. Relevant intervening Gazette amendments and the actual food category remain a mandatory independent check.

This authenticates two *original legal text provisions*, **not** legal applicability to 18 flagged dossiers, not an approved case numeric limit, and certainly not a lab PASS.

## Major verified analytical method collision — 2024 FSSAI manuals

Two FSSAI-hosted documents use *the same numerical method identifier for materially different procedures*, and the repository has not established an official precedence/adoption/supersession order.

1. [**Trace Elements** manual (https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/MANUAL_FOR_ANALYSIS_OF_METALS_dt_18_04_2024.pdf)](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/MANUAL_FOR_ANALYSIS_OF_METALS_dt_18_04_2024.pdf), PDF p.3, lists **FSSAI 09.001:2024 = microwave digestion + AAS** (FAAS for Cu/Zn/Fe, GFAAS for Pb/Cd), with approximate Pb capability **0.4 mg/kg**. This named method expressly **excludes food ≥40% fat and milk powder**.
2. [**Metals and Minerals** manual (https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual%20of%20Methods%20of%20Analysis%20of%20Foods-%20Metals%20and%20Minerals.pdf)](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual%20of%20Methods%20of%20Analysis%20of%20Foods-%20Metals%20and%20Minerals.pdf), PDF p.3, lists **FSSAI 09.001:2024 = microwave digestion + ICP-OES**, with published quantification limits **Pb 6, Cu 0.3, Cr 5, Ni 3 mg/kg**. Its text permits additional matrices only upon suitable performance demonstration.
3. Other code collisions exist: **Cr/Se/Mo by ICP-MS in infant/nutritional foods** is listed as **09.006** in the Trace Elements manual, **09.005** in Metals and Minerals. In the second manual, **09.006 refers to iodine** instead. Neither Cr method thereby covers misri or Ni in generic polyols.
4. The published Pb **6 mg/kg** ICP-OES LOQ is above the **2.5 mg/kg** historic generic lead-candidate limit: such a method as published cannot determine compliance at that threshold. Published Cr LOQ **5 mg/kg** is far above named refined-sugar Cr comparator **0.02 mg/kg**; published Ni LOQ **3 mg/kg** is above named sorbitol Ni comparator **2 mg/kg**. These are *comparison cautions*, not grants of applicability of those legal limits to Misri or all polyols.
5. This increases the need for **specific high-fat cocoa mass and coconut milk powder** checks. Full method title, operative adoption notification and laboratory-accredited **achieved** LOQ/matrix performance are required; a report quoting only “FSSAI 09.001:2024” is too ambiguous.

This explicit safety block is added to **every one of the 35 source-research cases**, including correctly routing the OTA/aflatoxin/pesticide/antibiotic cases **away from metal methods**. **Zero laboratory-method approvals or product releases** were entered.

### Consultation provenance of the second manual — verify before adopting

FSSAI's official [notices for public comments](https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-law/notifications?notification=notice-for-comments) list the **26 June 2024** notice inviting stakeholder feedback on *Manual of Methods of Analysis of Foods — Metals and Minerals* and *Sweets & Confectionery*. It says these were recommended/approved by the **Scientific Panel**, which is **not, alone, an authenticated final Authority adoption/supersession order**. Both PDFs may be hosted on an official domain yet differ in procedural authority. Thus this review **does not assert that both versions are equally operative**. Obtain the Authority's notified/current laboratory-method index and actual adoption documents before selecting a procedure. In particular, the publication of the ICP-OES manual for comments must **not** trigger an automatic analytical-method substitution.

## Explicit source-level decision — Dried Glucose Syrup (case #18)

FSSR **2.8.7 Dried Glucose Syrup**, as published in FSSAI's [2021 product-standards compendium](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Compendium_Food_Additives_Regulations_12_11_2021.pdf), imposes **sulphated ash no more than 1.0%** and **total solids at least 93%**. The original CTR lead table's special **starch conversion products** category applies only when **sulphated ash exceeds 1.0%**. The two conditions do **not** overlap for a product conforming to the Dried Glucose Syrup product standard. Consequently **the special high-ash lead row is excluded for a standards-conforming finished Dried Glucose Syrup** as a *source-level classification conclusion*. This is narrower than approving a substitute lead limit: first authenticate that the commercial lot indeed meets FSSR 2.8.7, then identify the correct operative lead provision, lab method and actual lot report.

## Original salt-substitute Gazette verified — case #22

[The 2017 FSSAI Fifth Amendment Regulation, Original Gazette](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Special_Dietary_Food_22_06_2017.pdf) specifically inserted **FSSR 2.9.30(6)**, a separate standard for salt substitutes. It prescribes permitted potassium, calcium, magnesium and other salt constituents under specified limits; **sodium content not over 120 mg per 100 g** of the salt-substitute mixture; and additional **low-sodium-salt-substitute label/cation declaration**. This unequivocally distinguishes a lawfully marketed salt substitute from a generic untreated common-salt identity. The source authenticates **product-class regulation**, not a lead maximum. Do not assign the **common sodium-chloride salt** contaminant row without a written applicability decision based on the commercial formula.

## Specific original standard found within grouped polyols — case #21

The [original FSSAI 29 November 2017 vertical-standards Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Vertical_Standards_29_11_2017%281%29.pdf) notifies a named **Sorbitol** food-substance standard (INS **420**, CAS **50-70-4**), requiring **Nickel ≤2.0 mg/kg** and **Lead ≤1.0 mg/kg** as *sorbitol-grade purity characteristics*. This is a **real original published numeric monograph for one compound**. It does **not** automatically impose 2 mg/kg nickel on a generic listed **“polyols excluding standardized ones”** FoSCoS group, or on other polyols/mixtures.

**Further exact catalogue exclusion:** the [official FoSCoS standardized product registry](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product) calls this grouped polyol article **'excluding those already standardized'**, while original 2017 FSSR specifically standardizes Sorbitol under 3.2.16. The 2 mg/kg Ni sorbitol monograph therefore cannot be a default for that grouped category, even before actual ingredient identification. This is a positive *non-transfer source-level disposition*, not a lot release.

**Case decision improved:** original substance-specific purity law is *authenticated for sorbitol only*; the entire grouped case remains **HELD** until the manufacturer identifies the exact purchased polyol(s), current standards and actual test lot. The numeric 2.0 mg/kg figure is **not** falsely promoted to an approved case-wide CTR contaminant limit.

## More appropriate published lead assay candidate — pressure digestion + ICP-MS

Both [Trace Elements](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/MANUAL_FOR_ANALYSIS_OF_METALS_dt_18_04_2024.pdf) and [Metals and Minerals](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Manual%20of%20Methods%20of%20Analysis%20of%20Foods-%20Metals%20and%20Minerals.pdf) describe pressure digestion + **ICP-MS to quantify lead in foods** with **published Pb scope approximately 0.04 mg/kg dry matter**, indexed as **FSSAI 09.005:2024** and **09.004:2024** respectively. This is much more sensitive on paper than the proposed ICP-OES 09.001 Pb LOQ 6 mg/kg, and may be a **sensible laboratory shortlist candidate for the 24 lead cases**.

**It is NOT approved for any individual case here:** exact notified/current method adoption order remains unresolved, and that dry-matter performance does not establish actual laboratory **achieved LOQ in fat-rich cocoa, coconut, dairy, compound solids, dissolved sugars or vended water**. The low-concentration source is not an automatic <0.02 mg/kg wet-material clearance. Request the current official complete manual title, laboratory accreditation, actual analyte/matrix scope, LOQ and recovery before assigning a method or claiming a PASS.

## Mycotoxin analytical methods reviewed against actual product matrices — cases #2, #28, #29

The official [FSSAI revised Mycotoxins manual, July 2021](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order_Manual_Analysis_Mycotoxins_20_07_2021.pdf), published table of contents (PDF pp.5–6), supplies **FSSAI 07.009:2020** for AFB1 and total aflatoxins by immunoaffinity cleanup/HPLC fluorescence and **FSSAI 07.015:2020** for B1/B2/G1/G2 in foods other than the already named matrices. These are **method-development candidates** for cases **28** (specific nutraceutical ingredient) and **29** (functional ingredient premix), subject to actual material and matrix method performance.

The same manual lists **FSSAI 07.017** and **07.018** for **ochratoxin in barley**, and **07.019** for OTA/aflatoxins in **cereals and processed cereals**. These **must not be treated as verified coffee-chicory blend OTA assays** (case #2). An independently accredited method in roasted/soluble coffee-chicory actual matrix is required. Crucially, the manual explicitly states that the respective laboratory is responsible for verifying the selected method’s **on-site performance**. Neither an authoritative manual nor a validated method alone determines a legal contaminant ceiling or a lot PASS.

## Child formulation original product standard — case #3

The [2018 notified FSSR children’s formulated supplements Gazette](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2018/11/5bfd0a04c4d4bGazette_Notification_Children_Supplement_26_11_2018.pdf) is an original product-standard reference for the article **formulated supplements for children above 24 months until 36 months**, rather than a default infant formula. **Cases #33 and #34, Ayurveda Aahara**: the [FSSAI 25 July 2025 Category A recipe list](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Order%20dated%2025-07-2025%20enclosing%20Ayurveda%20Aahara.pdf), issued with competent-authority approval under the Ayurveda Aahara Regulations 2022, contains **Narikela Ksheerika** and **Ksheerapaka**. **All 35, current laboratory law**: FSSAI's [notification archive](https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-law/notifications) lists **18 September 2026** final Food Safety and Standards (Laboratory and Sample Analysis) Amendment Regulations addressing sampling timelines, **methods of analysis** and reporting formats. **The original PDF content has not been directly authenticated here**. It is mandatory to inspect its final operative wording and effective date, not just the notice title, when signing current official method precedence or a testing report.
