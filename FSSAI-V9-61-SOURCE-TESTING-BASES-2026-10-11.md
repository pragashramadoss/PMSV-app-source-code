# PMSV FSSAI Helper — Source testing-basis source lock (11 October 2026)

The source-specific screening panel now pins **61 exact FSSAI Version IX metal article references** against the official local Section 2.1 source table (article, metal, limit, mg/kg or mg/L, **measurement/analytical basis**). All referenced values remain **REVIEW ONLY, NOT APPLIED** to finished products.

**Seven specific measurement bases now locked and displayed**:
- Lead in cocoa powder: dry fat-free substance basis.
- Copper in cocoa powder: fat-free substance basis.
- Copper in tomato puree/paste/powder/cocktail: dried tomato solids.
- Lead in dehydrated onions, herbs and spices: dry matter basis.
- Lead in milk: concentration-factor adjustment for partly or wholly dehydrated milk.
- Arsenic in non-synthetic food colouring: dry colouring matter basis.
- Arsenic in preservative/antioxidant/emulsifier/stabilizer and synthetic colour articles: dry matter basis.

The other 54 article references carry a zero-length additional condition, signalling that **no separate article-row basis is stated**—not permission to assume laboratory method, unit conversions or legal applicability.

The previously linked **387** source-article screen checks and all **121** unresolved first-source research worksheets now preserve this basis field. A changed or absent basis, source numeric value, product identity or falsely asserted compliance PASS makes the panel withhold evidence. This release does not promote new finished-product contaminant evidence or claim full legal/laboratory compliance.

Official regulation: [FSSAI Contaminants Version IX (03.02.2026), Section 2.1](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf). GitHub `gh-pages` only; WordPress not modified.
