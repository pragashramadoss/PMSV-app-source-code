## Result and limitation

The research covers **all 35 currently unresolved finished-product entries**, making the exact commodity mismatch and the decisive next documentary question explicit for each one. The primary official legal source is [FSSAI CTR Regulations Version IX, 03 February 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf), including §2.1.1 named food-by-metal rows, §2.2.1 crop toxins and §2.3 pesticide MRL schedules.

**No case can be promoted merely because a named comparator, generic fallback or raw ingredient appears elsewhere in the regulations.** Product-specific official article applicability remains unverified until an exact finished-food identity, operative legal source and matrix basis have been independently signed off. No laboratory PASS or regulatory certification is claimed.

### Why the 35 are not legally closed

- **The published article differs from the actual finished food.** Examples: cocoa powder versus cocoa beans/liquor; sorbitol versus polyols; salt versus salt substitutes.
- **Processing and analytical basis differ.** Examples: high-sulphated-ash glucose versus ordinary dried glucose syrup, liquid eggs versus egg powder/pickled eggs, named crop versus frozen processed fruit.
- **The ingredient identity is still missing.** Broad nutraceutical schedules, enzymes, blends, traditional alkalis and botanical products need exact grades, origin, processing and/or formulas before deciding contaminant scope.
- **FSSR product standards are not themselves CTR named contaminant entries.** Product identity clauses for carob, silver leaf and trehalose are useful positive identity evidence, but not evidence that a different named contaminant maximum applies.

- **SILVER LEAF (Chandi-ka-warq)** — [Official FSSAI additional document](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Silver_Leaf_19_07_2016.pdf). ## Required decision record before any legal-source promotion

1. Confirm product ID against the current FoSCoS/FSSR finished-article name, actual composition, process and packaging.
2. Record the operative article and analyte **verbatim from an official current legal publication**, including page/section, exemptions, named-versus-fallback precedence and analytical basis.
3. Verify real applicable finished-lot sampling context; independently adjudicate whether the finished form is legally within the selected contaminant article.
4. Obtain supporting documentary evidence and independent review sign-off. Any laboratory conformity decision requires the actual analytical report and the approved correct limit; a receipt checkbox, note or source link is not a laboratory PASS.
5. Only then, through a separately controlled approved-source promotion process, change the canonical source match. This research batch contains **no promotion mechanism**.
