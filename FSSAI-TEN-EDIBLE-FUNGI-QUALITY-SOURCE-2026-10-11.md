# FSSAI Helper — Complete edible-fungi quality audit (11 October 2026)

Scope: **10 unresolved** local FoSCoS products with exact **FSSR 2.3.62** standardized route. Official compendium [Chapter 2.3, PDF pp. 97–103](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_3_Fruit%20%20Vegetable%20products.pdf), and [Contaminants Version IX](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf). Records cover **55 individually typed, source-paged product-standard quality checks** across quick-frozen, dried, Shiitake, powder/grits, extract/concentrate, dried concentrate, oil-packed, salted, sterilized, pickled and fermented subtypes. The source distinguishes process variants, permitted ingredient composition, species naming, quantitative defect allowances and preservation parameters.

### Chemical-source exclusions
- Agaric acid **100 ppm** is written for **food containing mushrooms**, not automatically every fungus species. FSSR 2.3.62 expressly permits generic fungi species and names Agaricus/mushrooms as an example; enforce species confirmation.
- **Tin 250 mg/kg** and **lead 1 mg/kg** are named for **canned mushrooms**; sterilized or airtight does not itself confirm a canned mushroom identity. Even packaging confirmation does not grant a compliance PASS.
- Preserve variant-specific quality limits and their legal analytical/processing qualifiers. **0 automatic contaminant limit, 0 full product approvals**; the global source ledger remains **410 partially evidenced and 123 first-source pending**.
