# PMSV FSSAI Helper — Interactive finished-food subtype research selector

Added a practical selector to the **nine** unresolved FoSCoS product identities that have **21** official, named finished-product candidate forms and **43** metal-article source references. The user can now:

1. See all named subtype source candidates.
2. Select an exact food form for further research (e.g. canned mangoes, canned green peas, pickled cucumber, processed tomato concentrate).
3. Select **none of these articles fits the finished food**, and PMSV hides candidate numeric reference rows instead of suggesting an unconfirmed generic match.
4. Return to all candidate forms without losing the reviewed official article references.

**Compliance guarantee:** This selector does not confirm recipe identity, packaging, product standard, mandatory test basis, amendments or laboratory outcomes. It cannot change legal applicability, promote first source evidence, or return PASS. The source-specific full article, analyte, numerical value and measurement basis remain verified against the official FSSAI Version IX source at display time. A modified or missing source row fails closed. No selection is saved across tabs; no WordPress changes.

Automated tests exercise all 21 forms, cross-product isolation, tampering protection, no-match cases, and the absence of automatic finished-product compliance. The baseline remains **533 catalogue identities, 412 partial source evidence, 121 pending first exact source, zero full compliance passes**.

Official regulator source: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf
