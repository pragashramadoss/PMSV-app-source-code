# PMSV FSSAI: Dried Mango Slices and Amchur — exact spice category and aflatoxin evidence

**Source review date:** 10 October 2026. **Status: partial product-specific regulatory evidence, not full compliance.**

FSSAI Food Products Standards and Food Additives, Chapter 2.9 Version 4 (1 August 2025), identifies:
- §2.9.23 **Dried Mango Slices**, FoSCoS FCS **12.2.1**; PMSV product ID `12-12-2-dried-mango-slices`.
- §2.9.24 **Dried Mango Powder (Amchur)**, FoSCoS FCS **12.2.1**; PMSV product ID `12-12-2-dried-mango-powder-amchur`.

FSSAI Contaminants, Toxins and Residues, Version IX (3 February 2026), §2.2.1, official article **Spices/Spice Mix**:
- **Total Aflatoxins: 30 µg/kg**
- **Aflatoxin B1: 15 µg/kg**

The exact products are legally standardized in the salt/spices/condiments chapter and fall under the FoSCoS 12.2.1 spice identity used in the Helper. The official source rows, numeric values, units, exact catalogue names, FSSR clauses and FCS code are checked by the audit and the UI source gate.

These values are **not inherited** by Seasoning (FCS 12.2.2), spice oleoresins, fresh mango, other dried fruit products, mango pulp, canned mangoes or unrelated mango foods. Other contaminants, pesticide MRLs, processing, sampling and effective amendments remain separate checks. No complete compliance PASS is created.

**Audit progression:** 533 total catalogue identities; 305 with some exact product-specific contaminant evidence; 228 without exact identity evidence (previously 303 and 230). **Complete contaminant/residue compliance approvals: 0.**
