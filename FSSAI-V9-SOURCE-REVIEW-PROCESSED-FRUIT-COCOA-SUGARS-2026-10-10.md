# Official Version IX review — processed fruit, cocoa, sugars (10 October 2026)

**Regulatory source:** [FSSAI Contaminants Regulations Version IX, 03 February 2026](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf), section 2.1.1 (lead table), especially PDF pages 2–3 (zero-indexed pages 1–2). This is a source-evidence note and not a product compliance certificate.

## Exact source rows checked

| Official lead article | Lead maximum | Matrix or analytical condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Canned fruit cocktail | 1.0 mg/kg | Finished **canned** fruit cocktail only |
| Cocoa powder | 5.0 mg/kg | **Dry fat-free substance basis** |
| Canned tomatoes | 1.0 mg/kg | Finished canned tomatoes |
| Canned mushrooms | 1.0 mg/kg | Finished canned mushrooms |
| Cereal grains, except buckwheat, canihua and quinoa | 0.2 mg/kg | Grain identity; explicit exclusions |
| All types of sugars, sugar syrup, invert sugar and direct consumption coloured sugars with sulphated ash **exceeding 1.0%** | 5.0 mg/kg | Ash threshold is required |
| Anhydrous dextrose and dextrose monohydrate, refined white sugar (sulphated ash **not exceeding 0.03%**) | 0.5 mg/kg | Exact substance or qualified refined sugar |

## Catalogue decisions — do not broaden by name

- `04-04-1-thermally-processed-fruit-salad-cocktail-mix` (FSSR 2.3.2): **Do not assign** the canned fruit cocktail 1 mg/kg row to the entire catalogue identity. The standard identity also permits process/package cases which must be separately established.
- `05-05-1-cocoa-powder`: Retain the dry fat-free substance basis alongside any displayed lead limit. Do not extend to `04-04-2-cocoa-beans` or `05-05-1-cocoa-mass-or-cocoa-chocolate-liquor-and-cocoa-cake`.
- `04-04-1-dry-fruits-and-nuts`, `04-04-1-raisins`: the cited lead rows for canned fruit do **not** support limits for dried fruit; keep product-specific review pending.
- Sugar and syrup identities: do **not** assume sulphated ash. Different official rows apply under different chemical/identity conditions. A generic sugar match must not be used as a finished-product PASS.
- Cereal grains: do **not** apply the grouped limit to quinoa or to composite cereal foods, flour, breakfast cereal or biscuits by name alone.

**Queue consequence:** These source rows are established, but they do not establish exact applicability for any additional unresolved combined identity without the necessary subtype/processing/analytical input. Do not decrement the queue on the basis of this note. Check other metals, mycotoxins, residues and subsequent amendments independently.
