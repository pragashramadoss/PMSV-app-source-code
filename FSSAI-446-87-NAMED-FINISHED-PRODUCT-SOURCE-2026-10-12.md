**Named conditional route 1:** FoSCoS Fruits and Vegetable Chutney §2.3.41 may include strictly identified, separately standardized *Mango Chutney* §2.3.42. CTR IX records *Mango chutney* lead 1 mg/kg and tin 250 mg/kg. This is conditional on the finished product meeting 2.3.42, including mango identity, at least 50% total soluble solids, at least 40% fruit, and pH <=4.6. Mixed/general chutney is **not** covered by the named route. The lot and testing are not verified.

**Named conditional route 2:** FSSR Seasoning §2.9.31 admits dry and wet/composite products. The actual finished *dry curry powder or mix masala* subroute is explicitly named in CTR IX lead 10 mg/kg on a **dry matter basis**. Not automatically applicable to wet pastes, other composite seasonings, or constituent spice ingredients. Lot identity and laboratory testing are not verified.
