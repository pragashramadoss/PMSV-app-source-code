### Exact FSSR quality source: vending-machine purified water

[Official FSSR 2.10 beverages chapter, Version 3 (07.05.2025), PDF printed page 24](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_10_BEVERAGES_Other_than_Dairy_and_Fruits_Vegetables_based.pdf) states that the vending product is **other than packaged drinking water or natural mineral water**, must be **clear and free from sediments, suspended particles and extraneous matter**, and comply with **IS:10500**. A vending machine is a decentralized purification/dispensing system, not captive-consumption-only treatment. The FSSR cross-reference to IS:10500 does **not** authorize copying the packaged-drinking-water metals/pesticide values from FSSR 2.10.8, nor is any numeric IS limit recorded without separately sourced current text.

### Exact Ayurveda Aahara recipe identity (not stand-alone contaminant passes)

The [official FSSAI Ayurveda Aahara Compendium](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Ayurveda%20Aahara%20Compendium.pdf) lists:
- **Narikela Ksheerika:** Ksheera group **29.8**, PDF p.111 (index 110), coconut endosperm and one part milk; recipe includes cooking coconut in milk, and classical recipe use-by within a day. FoSCoS catalogue label **14.3** is not the Compendium index—crosswalk requires affirmative verification.
- **Ksheerapaka:** Ksheera group **29.12**, PDF pp.114–115 (index 113–114), buffalo milk, aromatic flowers, water and sugar; classical recipe boils fragrant milk and water and then adds sugar; indicated within a day. FoSCoS catalogue label **14.7** is not the Compendium index.

The [Ayurveda Aahara Regulations 2022](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2022/05/62789a20b54bdGazette_Notification_Ayurveda_Aahara_09_05_2022.pdf) include additional formulation, Schedule B, contaminant **Schedule D**, ingredient purity, hygiene and labelling obligations. A named classical recipe is evidence of identity only; final product, use category, recipe processing and laboratory verification remain required.
