## Primary sources checked

- FSSAI 2025 RAFT Vol. 2.0 analytical method/kit validation guidance, chapter 29 pp. 8–12 (PDF pages 14–17): https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Validation%20Guidelines%2005_06_2025.pdf
- FSSAI methods and adoption-orders index: https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-testing/manuals-methods-analysis
- Signed consultation notice, 26 June 2024: https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Notice%20for%20comments_26062024.pdf

The RAFT text covers specificity, calibration, matrix effect, LOD/LOQ, bias/recovery, precision, ruggedness and measurement uncertainty in the context of **method/kit validation for RAFT**. It does **not** serve as an official lab result, legally operative CTR article determination, approved lab method, or passed lot. These checks are useful supporting evidence questions, not universal stand-alone legal obligations.
