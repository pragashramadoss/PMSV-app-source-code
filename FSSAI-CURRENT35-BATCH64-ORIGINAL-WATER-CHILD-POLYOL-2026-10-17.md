## Principal correction: vending-machine purified water has its OWN enacted standard

The authentic [FSSAI original First Amendment Gazette, notified 29 January 2019](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Drinking_Water_05_02_2019.pdf), *Gazette PDF page 4, English original*, inserts FPSFA **2.10.9 Drinking Water (Purified)**. The final legal text defines vending-machine water **other than packaged drinking water or natural mineral water**, dispensed by decentralised purification systems, excluding installations for captive use; subsection 2 requires water clarity and compliance with **IS:10500**. This final-original text, rather than the earlier **2018 draft**, is the valid source of the distinct named food article. [Current compiled FSSAI beverages chapter, version 2 (4 November 2024), PDF page 24](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_10_BEVERAGES_Other%20than%20Dairy%20and%20Fruits%20%20Vegetables%20based.pdf) reproduces 2.10.9.

A **separate June 2025 DRAFT proposal** to substitute 2.10.9(2) with six detailed tables is available from [FSSAI official PDF](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Notification%20dt%2004_06_2025-Cheese%20powder%20draft.pdf), including a **proposed lead 0.01 mg/L** requirement (English PDF p.11). The document expressly invites public comments and **cannot be considered enacted** on that basis alone. This review found no independently authenticated final replacement, but **absence of confirmation is not a claim that none exists**. Do not copy the draft's numeric value, or the separately named packaged-water CTR lead article, into this vending-water case.

**Exact remaining closure:** commercially sold, non-captive dispensing identity; current IS:10500 edition/lead parameter and applicable statutory source; search for any actually final post-June-2025 amendment; original effective analyte-food/sampling basis; independent recognized lab accreditation matrix/analyte/method and actual vended-water lot/report.

## Formulated supplements for children — specific AGE window, NOT all infant foods

The [2018 original FSSAI Gazette on formulated supplements for children](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/notifications/2018/11/5bfd0a04c4d4bGazette_Notification_Children_Supplement_26_11_2018.pdf) was located through official indexed text. It introduced the product standard **FPSFA 2.4.11(4)**, specifying **children above 24 months up to 36 months**. The [current chapter 2.4 FSSAI version 4 (7 May 2025)](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Chapter%202_4_Cereals_and_Cereal_products.pdf) retains this scope. It permits a particular formulated-food class supplementing family food. It references the *source compounds* under infant nutrition provision 2.1.19 and *follow-up formula* microbiology, but such cross-references are **not proof that the packaged product is an infant milk substitute/infant food for CTR lead**.

Product identity requires actual specified ages on pack, label, ingredients, physical form and use instructions. **Do not automatically apply an infant-food lead comparator** to this separate age-defined article. The original 2018 Gazette PDF's full English pages were **not independently rendered this turn**; record provenance accordingly.

## Non-standardized polyols — only named Sorbitol has the specified nickel CTR row

The [FoSCoS product registry](https://fcstraining.fssai.gov.in/standard-product) has the pooled listing **“Polyols permitted under Appendix-A ... excluding those already standardized”** under sweetener category 11.6. This category is **not** a named chemical, CAS/INS number, actual manufacturer recipe, or product-specific CTR metal requirement.

The [official FSSAI CTR Compendium IX, 3 February 2026, PDF p.13](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Contaminants_Regulations_03_02_2026_IX.pdf) specifically lists **Sorbitol** at **nickel 2.0 mg/kg**. This is a **Sorbitol-only published source article**, not a universally applied nickel limit for unnamed polyols, or a signed legal maximum for the catalogue's broad group. Request named substance, INS/CAS, grade, carrier, legal original identity/monograph and lab/lot scope before any compliance disposition.
